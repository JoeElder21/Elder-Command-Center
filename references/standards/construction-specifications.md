## 1. Earthwork

### 1.1 Clearing and Grubbing

| Item | Specification |
|------|--------------|
| Scope | Remove all trees, stumps, brush, roots, and organic debris within limits of disturbance |
| Stump Removal | Remove to 12 inches below finished subgrade (18 inches in pavement areas) |
| Topsoil Stripping | Strip and stockpile topsoil from all areas to be graded; minimum 6-inch strip depth |
| Topsoil Storage | Stockpile in designated area with silt fence perimeter; maximum height 6 ft |
| Tree Protection | Install tree protection fencing at drip line of trees to remain before any site work |
| Disposal | Haul all debris off-site to approved facility; no on-site burial without written approval |

### 1.2 Excavation

| Parameter | Specification |
|-----------|--------------|
| Unclassified Excavation | All materials regardless of type (rock, soil, organics) |
| Rock Excavation | Material requiring drilling, blasting, or mechanical breaking. Classified per contract. |
| Unsuitable Material | Organic soil, muck, peat, topsoil, frozen soil, or material not meeting compaction requirements |
| Trench Excavation | Per OSHA 29 CFR 1926 Subpart P — trench safety required for depths > 5 ft |
| Dewatering | Contractor responsible; maintain dry conditions during construction |
| Stockpile Location | Within designated area; silt fence around perimeter; do not block drainage |
| Over-Excavation | Where directed by engineer; backfill with approved material and compact |

### 1.3 Fill and Embankment

| Parameter | Specification |
|-----------|--------------|
| Approved Fill Material | Inorganic soil free of debris, organic matter, frozen soil, and rocks > 6 inches |
| Maximum Lift Thickness | 8 inches loose (6 inches in structural areas) |
| Compaction — General Fill | 95% Standard Proctor (ASTM D698) |
| Compaction — Under Pavement | 98% Standard Proctor within top 12 inches of subgrade |
| Compaction — Under Structures | 95% Standard Proctor minimum; per geotechnical engineer's recommendation |
| Compaction — Utility Trench | 95% Standard Proctor in 6-inch lifts |
| Moisture Content | Within +/- 2% of optimum moisture content |
| Testing Frequency | 1 test per 2,000 sf per lift, or 1 per 500 cy, minimum 1 per lift [VERIFY] |
| Testing Method | Nuclear density gauge (ASTM D6938) or sand cone (ASTM D1556) |
| Proof Roll | Required for all pavement subgrade; loaded tandem-axle dump truck (minimum); identify soft spots |
| Geotextile | Place woven geotextile on soft subgrade where directed by geotechnical engineer |

### 1.4 Subgrade Preparation

| Parameter | Specification |
|-----------|--------------|
| Proof Roll | All pavement and building pad subgrade; identify deflection > 1 inch under loaded truck |
| Subgrade Compaction | 98% Standard Proctor, top 12 inches |
| Treatment of Soft Spots | Over-excavate and replace with #57 stone or approved granular material per engineer |
| Lime Stabilization | Where recommended by geotechnical report; typically 4-6% hydrated lime mixed into top 8-12 inches |
| Geogrid | Where recommended by geotechnical report; install per manufacturer's instructions |
| Chemical Stabilization | Portland cement, lime-fly ash, or other per geotechnical engineer [VERIFY with geotech] |
| Subgrade Elevation Tolerance | +/- 0.1 ft of design grade |

## 3. Concrete

### 3.1 Concrete Flatwork (Sidewalk, Patio, Pad)

| Parameter | Specification |
|-----------|--------------|
| Thickness | 4 inches (standard pedestrian); 6 inches (vehicle crossing or heavy loads) |
| Compressive Strength | 4,000 psi at 28 days (minimum) |
| Air Entrainment | 5-7% (required for freeze-thaw durability in Kentucky) |
| Slump | 4 inches (+/- 1 inch) |
| Reinforcement | 6x6-W2.9xW2.9 (6x6-6/6) welded wire mesh or #4 bars at 18 inches on center, each way |
| Subgrade | Compacted to 95% Standard Proctor; 4-inch compacted granular base (DGA or #57 stone) |
| Joints — Contraction | Saw-cut or tooled at 1/4 depth of slab; spacing not to exceed 3x the slab thickness in feet (12 ft max for 4-inch slab) |
| Joints — Expansion | 1/2-inch pre-molded expansion joint material at structures, inlets, and every 50-100 ft |
| Finish — Pedestrian | Medium broom finish perpendicular to direction of travel |
| Finish — Vehicle | Broom finish or exposed aggregate |
| Curing | Apply curing compound immediately after finishing; maintain for minimum 7 days |
| Cold Weather | Do not place when air temperature is below 40 degrees F and falling; protect from freezing for 72 hours minimum |
| Hot Weather | Do not place when concrete temperature exceeds 90 degrees F without mitigation measures |
