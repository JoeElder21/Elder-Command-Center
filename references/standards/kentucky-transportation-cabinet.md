## 1. Overview

The Kentucky Transportation Cabinet (KYTC) administers all state-maintained roadways and establishes design standards for highway construction, access management, drainage, and encroachment permits. Projects with frontage on state-maintained routes or requiring access to state highways must comply with KYTC standards.

**Key KYTC Divisions:**

| Division | Responsibility |
|----------|---------------|
| Division of Highway Design | Geometric design, roadway standards |
| Division of Structural Design | Bridges, retaining walls, culverts |
| Division of Maintenance | Encroachment permits, right-of-way |
| Division of Environmental Analysis | NEPA, environmental compliance |
| Division of Traffic Operations | Signals, signage, pavement markings |
| District Offices | Local project administration, permit review |

| 20 | 73.5 | 38.4 | 115 |
| 25 | 91.9 | 60.0 | 155 |
| 30 | 110.3 | 86.4 | 200 |
| 35 | 128.6 | 117.6 | 250 |
| 40 | 147.0 | 153.6 | 305 |
| 45 | 165.4 | 194.4 | 360 |
| 50 | 183.8 | 240.0 | 425 |
| 55 | 202.1 | 290.3 | 495 |
| 60 | 220.5 | 345.5 | 570 |

Assumptions: 2.5-second perception-reaction time, deceleration rate 11.2 ft/s^2, level grade.
Source: AASHTO Green Book Table 3-1 [VERIFY].

**Grade Adjustment:** On sustained grades, increase SSD for downgrades and decrease for upgrades per AASHTO Table 3-2.

### 3.2 Intersection Sight Distance (ISD)

For stop-controlled approaches, the required sight distance along the major road is based on a time gap for the minor road vehicle to enter or cross.

**Departure Sight Triangle — Passenger Vehicles:**

| Major Road Speed (mph) | Left Turn from Stop (ft) | Right Turn from Stop (ft) | Crossing Maneuver (ft) |
|------------------------|--------------------------|---------------------------|------------------------|
| 25 | 280 | 240 | 200 |
| 30 | 335 | 290 | 240 |
| 35 | 390 | 335 | 280 |
| 40 | 445 | 385 | 320 |
| 45 | 500 | 430 | 360 |
| 50 | 555 | 480 | 400 |
| 55 | 610 | 530 | 440 |

Time gaps: 7.5 sec (left turn), 6.5 sec (right turn), varies for crossing [VERIFY].
Source: AASHTO Green Book Table 9-7 [VERIFY].

**Sight Triangle Requirements for Commercial Driveways:**
- Clear sight triangle must be maintained within the area defined by the intersection of the driveway edge and the travel lane edge
- No obstructions between 2.0 ft and 7.0 ft height within the sight triangle
- Vegetation, signs, fences, and structures must be cleared or relocated

## 5. Encroachment Permit Requirements

### 5.1 When Required

An encroachment permit is required for any of the following within KYTC right-of-way:

- New driveway or access point
- Modification to existing driveway (widening, relocation, change of use)
- Utility installation, relocation, or repair
- Drainage structure installation or modification
- Sign installation
- Construction activity (staging, temporary access, traffic control)
- Sidewalk or multiuse path construction
- Mailbox installation or relocation
- Landscaping within right-of-way

### 5.2 Permit Application Requirements

| Item | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Application Form | TC 10-20 (Encroachment Permit Application) [VERIFY current form] |
| Site Plan | Scaled plan showing existing conditions and proposed work within ROW |
| Traffic Study | May be required for commercial access — see turn lane warrant criteria |
| Drainage Analysis | Required if work affects drainage within ROW |
| Sight Distance Certification | Required for new or modified access points |
| Bonding/Insurance | May be required; amount set by KYTC District |
| Fee | Varies by district and scope [VERIFY current fee schedule] |

### 5.3 Permit Process

1. **Pre-Application Meeting** — Contact KYTC District Office, Division of Maintenance
2. **Application Submission** — Submit TC 10-20 with required attachments
3. **Plan Review** — KYTC reviews for compliance with design standards
4. **Traffic Review** — District Traffic Engineer reviews access and sight distance
5. **Conditional Approval** — Permit issued with conditions (may require revisions)
6. **Construction** — Work performed per approved plans with KYTC inspection
7. **Final Inspection** — KYTC inspects completed work before permit closure

**Typical Review Timeline:** 30-90 days [VERIFY]. Complex projects may take longer.

### 5.4 Traffic Impact Study Triggers

A Traffic Impact Study (TIS) may be required when:

| Trigger | Threshold |
|---------|-----------|
| Trip Generation | > 100 peak hour trips (ITE Trip Generation Manual) |
| ADT on State Route | > 5,000 ADT and proposed development > 50 peak hour trips |
| Operational Concerns | Existing LOS D or worse at adjacent intersections |
| Safety History | Crash history at or near proposed access point |
| District Request | At discretion of KYTC District Traffic Engineer |

A left turn lane may be warranted based on:

**Volume-Based Warrant (simplified):**

| Major Road ADT | Minor Road / Driveway Volume (vpd) | Left Turn Lane |
|---------------|-------------------------------------|----------------|
| > 10,000 | > 25 | Warranted |
| 5,000-10,000 | > 50 | Warranted |
| 2,000-5,000 | > 100 | Warranted |
| < 2,000 | > 200 | Warranted |

[VERIFY] against current KYTC warrants. KYTC may use NCHRP Report 745 or other methodology.

**Left Turn Lane Design:**

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Width | 11-12 ft |
| Taper Length | Per AASHTO / KYTC standards (varies by speed) |

### 6.2 Right Turn Lane Warrants

Right turn deceleration lanes are typically warranted when:
- Right turn volume > 50 vph in design hour
- Major road speed > 40 mph
- Sight distance is limited
- Safety concerns exist

## Verification Notes

- All values in this reference require verification against current editions of cited documents.
- KYTC updates standards, specifications, and standard drawings periodically. Always confirm the current edition is being used.
- Local jurisdictions (cities, counties) may impose additional requirements beyond KYTC standards.
- KYTC District Offices have authority to impose project-specific requirements. Always coordinate with the applicable district early in the design process.
- Design values from AASHTO are national standards; KYTC may adopt modified values. When conflicts exist, KYTC standards govern for state routes.
- Encroachment permit requirements and fees vary by district. Contact the District Maintenance Office for current information.
