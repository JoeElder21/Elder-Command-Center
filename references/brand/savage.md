# Savage Investments Brand Standards

Real estate investment and development entity. Owned by the Apex Brain (a business). Kept separate from the Elder Land Design (JEDS) brand.

| Element | Value |
|---------|-------|
| Beige | `#F5F0E8` |
| Metallic Gold | `#D4AF37` |
| Mark | SI monogram with snake emblem (artwork is not stored in this repository) |
| Tagline | "Built Different, Invest Different." |
| Aesthetic | Luxury and premium: beige grounds, gold accents, clean serif typography |

## Rules

- Beige is the ground. Use near-black or dark green for text. Gold on beige is about 1.9:1, so gold is for rules, emblem and large graphics only, never text.
- The snake emblem reinforces the "Savage" identity. Use only the supplied artwork; do not redraw it.
- Do not use JEDS green, leaf, or the JEDS contour mark on Savage materials, and do not use the Savage emblem on JEDS materials.
- Correspondence uses the Savage entity name and tagline; the signature title for Joe follows `references/brand/jeds.md` section 4 (no PE, PLA, "engineer" or "landscape architect").
