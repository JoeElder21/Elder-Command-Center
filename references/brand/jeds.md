# Elder Land Design (JEDS) Brand System

**Elder Land Design** is the public name. **JEDS** (Joe Elder Design Studios) is the studio name and internal shorthand. Tagline: **"Shaping land. Building legacy."**

Assets in this folder: `jeds-mark.svg` (full mark, 48 px and up), `jeds-mark-small.svg` (16 to 47 px), `jeds-tokens.css` (all values below), `jeds-brand-sheet.html` (visual reference).

## 1. What the studio is

| Evidence in this repository | What it shows |
|-----------------------------|---------------|
| 32 task prompts, 7 templates, 6 checklists | Work spans the whole project lifecycle, from site feasibility to closeout |
| Project types: lodging, institutional (education, religious), restaurant and brewery, residential subdivision, mixed-use, agricultural, mixed-use concept | Clients are developers, institutions and landowners, not homeowners |
| Zoning profiles: Bardstown/Nelson County, Elizabethtown, Lebanon/Marion County, Lexington/Fayette, Louisville/Jefferson County | Kentucky corridor practice; local code fluency is the proof of competence |
| Brand tokens already in use: dark green, gold, light green, cream; Cormorant Garamond and Outfit | The palette and type are established; this system formalizes them |

**Reading.** Elder Land Design sits where land development, site and civil design, and landscape design overlap. Its edge is that one person reads the ground, the code and the pro forma together, then draws the route from raw land to approved, buildable, well-composed ground.

## 2. Positioning

> For developers, institutions and landowners in Kentucky who need land to become approved, buildable and beautiful, Elder Land Design is the design studio that reads the ground first: feasibility, form and approvals in one line of sight, working with the licensed professionals of record who seal regulated work.

**Brand idea: Land, read closely.**

| Pillar | Lifecycle phase | Promise |
|--------|-----------------|---------|
| **Read** | Pre-development, due diligence | Know the site, the code and the numbers before money moves |
| **Shape** | Concept, design development | Give the land a form that works and lasts |
| **Approve** | Construction documents, permitting | Take the project through review without surprises |
| **Build** | Construction, closeout | Keep what was designed what gets built |

**Personality:** grounded, exacting, rooted, quietly premium. Not flashy, not folksy, not corporate.

## 3. Voice

Lead with the conclusion. Use numbers, sources and dimensions. Plain professional English, active voice, sentences under about 20 words.

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| "The north parcel drains to the existing inlet; detention is likely required (VERIFY with the reviewing agency)." | "World-class stormwater solutions." |
| "We recommend the PUD route. It adds about three weeks and removes the variance risk." | "It should be noted that a PUD may potentially be considered." |
| "Draft for review. Not for submittal." | Implying a seal, certification or licensure |

## 4. Name, titles and compliance

- Signature: `Joe Elder / Founder & Principal / Elder Land Design`. Add the tagline on external letters.
- Joe is not a licensed PE or PLA. Do not use PE, PLA, "engineer", "landscape architect", "licensed" or "engineer of record" for Joe, and do not describe Elder Land Design as an engineering or landscape-architecture practice. Use **land design**, **site design**, **land development**, **entitlement** and **design studio**.
- Regulated work (sealed civil or landscape-architecture documents) is issued by the licensed professional of record. Say so in proposals and on drawings.
- **Verify before publishing:** which titles and activities Kentucky restricts (engineers fall under KRS 322, landscape architects under KRS 323A) and what an unlicensed design studio may offer. Confirm wording with the Kentucky boards. This is unverified here and is a professional and legal decision for Joe.

## 5. Mark

**Concept: contour and benchmark.** Gold contour lines (the landscape side) rise around a cream survey benchmark triangle (the civil side). The bold third ring is the index contour, as on a topographic map. The lines run off the tile edge because land continues.

- **Lockup:** mark at left, wordmark right, aligned on the mark's vertical center. Wordmark **ELDER LAND DESIGN** in Cormorant Garamond 600, letterspacing +0.12em. Descriptor **JOE ELDER DESIGN STUDIOS** below in Outfit 500, +0.18em, in Gold Deep on cream or Gold on green.
- **Clear space:** the height of the benchmark triangle on every side.
- **Minimum size:** full mark 48 px (0.5 in); small variant 16 to 47 px (favicon, avatar); lockup 160 px wide.
- **Never:** recolor the tile, stretch or rotate, add effects, redraw the contours, place the mark on busy imagery, or add credentials to the lockup.

## 8. Graphic language

- **Hairline gold rule** (1 px) between sections. **Benchmark triangle** as the bullet and the section marker. **Contour-line pattern** at 8% opacity for covers and dividers.
- **Plans and diagrams:** north arrow, scale bar and legend on every sheet. Existing in Stone linework, Demolition dashed in Ink, Proposed in Green, call-outs in Leaf (matches the E/D/P rule in `CLAUDE.md`).
- **Imagery:** aerial and site photography at low sun, Kentucky land and built context, no stock people, no filters. Caption with location and date.

## 9. Documents

- **Cover:** Cream ground, mark top-left, title in Cormorant 56 px Green, a Green band across the bottom with project, client, date and revision.
- **Header and footer:** small mark and "Elder Land Design" left; "Bardstown | Louisville, KY" and page number right.
- **Status stamp** top-right on every page: `DRAFT`, `IN REVIEW`, `REVISED`, `FINAL`. Anything outbound is `DRAFT` until Joe approves.
- **Tables:** Green header row with Cream text, Paper zebra rows, Line rules.
- **File names:** `ELD_<ProjectCode>_<Deliverable>_Rev<N>_<YYYY-MM-DD>.<ext>`.
- **Email footer:** the signature block above plus the tagline.

## 11. Five-second brand test

1. Cover the logo: do cream ground, green band and gold hairline still say Elder Land Design?
2. Is any gold or leaf text sitting on cream?
3. Is the wordmark in Cormorant Garamond and the body in Outfit?
4. Does any title or signature claim a license Joe does not hold?
5. Is the status stamp present and correct?
