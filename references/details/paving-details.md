## General Notes

1. All pavement construction shall conform to KYTC Standard Specifications for Road and Bridge Construction, current edition, unless otherwise specified.
2. Contractor shall verify all subgrade conditions and notify the Engineer of any unsuitable soils, springs, or other conditions that differ from the design assumptions prior to paving.
3. Asphalt paving shall not be performed when the air temperature is below 40 degrees F and falling, or when the surface is wet, frozen, or otherwise unsatisfactory. Minimum compacted lift temperature: 250 degrees F for surface course, 225 degrees F for base course.
4. Portland cement concrete shall conform to ACI 318 and ACI 301 for structural concrete and ACI 330R for parking lot design. Minimum 28-day compressive strength: 4,000 psi unless otherwise noted.
5. All concrete shall be air-entrained (5-7% per ASTM C260) for freeze-thaw durability in Kentucky climate (USDA Zone 6b).
6. Subgrade preparation shall conform to Detail G-4 (Subgrade Preparation) in the Site Grading Details. Proof rolling required for all pavement subgrades.
7. All pavement areas shall be designed to drain; minimum pavement slope 1.5%, maximum 5% for parking areas.
8. Contractor shall protect all freshly placed pavements from traffic until curing is complete. Minimum cure time: 7 days for concrete (wet cure or curing compound), 24 hours for asphalt before opening to traffic.

## Detail P-1: Asphalt Pavement Sections

### Description

Standard asphalt pavement sections for various loading conditions. Sections are based on CBR 5 subgrade unless geotechnical report provides site-specific values. Adjust aggregate base thickness for higher or lower CBR values per KYTC pavement design methodology.

### Dimensions & Specifications

| Layer | Light Duty Parking | Heavy Duty Parking/Drive | Fire Lane / Truck Route |
|-------|-------------------|--------------------------|------------------------|
| Surface course (HMA) | 1.5 inches | 2.0 inches | 2.0 inches |
| Base course (HMA) | 2.0 inches | 3.0 inches | 4.0 inches |
| Dense Graded Aggregate (DGA) | 6.0 inches | 8.0 inches | 10.0 inches |
| **Total section** | **9.5 inches** | **13.0 inches** | **16.0 inches** |
| Compacted subgrade | Top 12 in @ 98% Std. Proctor | Top 12 in @ 98% Std. Proctor | Top 12 in @ 98% Std. Proctor |

### Materials

| Component | Specification |
|-----------|---------------|
| HMA surface course | KYTC CL 3 0.375A (PG 64-22) or approved equal |
| HMA base course | KYTC CL 2 0.75 (PG 64-22) or approved equal |
| Dense Graded Aggregate | KYTC DGA (Section 805); ASTM D1241 |
| Tack coat | KYTC SS-1h emulsion; applied at 0.05-0.10 gal/SY between lifts |
| Prime coat | KYTC MC-30 or approved emulsion prime; 0.20-0.30 gal/SY on DGA base |

### Notes

1. Light Duty Parking: automobile traffic only; no regular truck loading.
2. Heavy Duty Parking/Drive: occasional delivery trucks (SU-30), refuse trucks, and bus traffic.
3. Fire Lane: designed for fire apparatus loading (75,000 lbs GVW); coordinate with local fire marshal for additional requirements (turning radius, width, marking).
4. Apply tack coat between all asphalt lifts and on any existing asphalt surface receiving overlay.
5. Apply prime coat on top of compacted DGA base minimum 24 hours before paving. Do not pave over unprimed aggregate base.
6. All mix designs shall be submitted to the Engineer for review 14 days prior to paving.

## Detail P-2: Concrete Pavement Sections

### Description

Standard concrete pavement sections for sidewalks, plazas, and dumpster pads. All concrete shall be placed on a compacted aggregate base over prepared subgrade.

### Dimensions & Specifications

| Application | Concrete Thickness | Aggregate Base | Reinforcement | Subgrade Compaction |
|-------------|-------------------|---------------|---------------|-------------------|
| Sidewalk (pedestrian only) | 4 inches | 4 inches DGA | None (or WWF 6x6 W1.4xW1.4) | 95% Std. Proctor |
| Sidewalk (vehicle crossing) | 6 inches | 6 inches DGA | WWF 6x6 W2.9xW2.9 | 98% Std. Proctor |
| Plaza / patio | 4 inches | 4 inches DGA | WWF 6x6 W1.4xW1.4 | 95% Std. Proctor |
| Dumpster pad | 6 inches | 6 inches DGA | #4 rebar @ 18 in O.C. each way | 98% Std. Proctor |
| Loading dock apron | 8 inches | 8 inches DGA | #4 rebar @ 12 in O.C. each way | 98% Std. Proctor |
| Concrete drive (commercial) | 6 inches | 6 inches DGA | WWF 6x6 W2.9xW2.9 or #4 @ 18 in O.C. EW | 98% Std. Proctor |

### Materials

| Component | Specification |
|-----------|---------------|
| Portland cement concrete | 4,000 psi (28-day); Type I/II cement; air-entrained 5-7% |
| Aggregate base | KYTC DGA (Dense Graded Aggregate) per Section 805 |
| Welded wire fabric (WWF) | ASTM A185; placed on chairs at mid-depth of slab |
| Reinforcing steel | ASTM A615 Grade 60; minimum 2-inch clear cover |
| Curing compound | ASTM C309, Type 1, Class B (white-pigmented membrane) |
| Joint filler | Preformed expansion joint filler per ASTM D1751 (fiber) or ASTM D6690 (self-leveling sealant) |

### Notes

1. Concrete finish: medium broom finish perpendicular to direction of travel for pedestrian surfaces. Float finish for dumpster pads and loading areas.
2. Place welded wire fabric on chairs or supports at mid-depth of the slab. Do not place on subgrade and "hook up" — this does not achieve proper positioning.
3. Cure concrete for a minimum of 7 days using white-pigmented curing compound (ASTM C309) or wet-cure with burlap and water. Do not allow concrete to dry during curing period.
4. Dumpster pads shall be a minimum of 12 ft x 12 ft (single container) or 20 ft x 12 ft (dual container), with 6-inch integral curb on three sides and bollard protection as required.
5. All exposed concrete edges shall receive a 3/8-inch radius edging tool finish.

## Detail P-5: Concrete Joint Spacing and Types

### Description

Joint types, spacing, and construction requirements for concrete flatwork to control cracking and accommodate thermal movement.

### Dimensions & Specifications

| Joint Type | Purpose | Spacing | Depth | Width | Filler/Sealant |
|------------|---------|---------|-------|-------|----------------|
| Contraction (control) joint | Control shrinkage cracking | 10 ft max O.C. or 2-3x slab thickness | 1/4 of slab depth (min 1 in) | 1/8 inch (saw cut) or tooled | Optional sealant (ASTM D6690) |
| Expansion (isolation) joint | Accommodate thermal expansion | At structures, fixed objects, direction changes, 100 ft max | Full depth of slab | 1/2 inch | Preformed filler (ASTM D1751) with sealant cap |
| Construction joint | Planned stopping point | As required by placement schedule | Full depth | Keyed or doweled | Sealant optional |

### Joint Layout Rules

1. Panels formed by contraction joints should be approximately square. Aspect ratio shall not exceed 1.5:1 (length to width).
2. Maximum panel size: 15 ft x 15 ft for 6-inch slab, 12 ft x 12 ft for 4-inch slab.
3. Joints shall align through slab edges — do not terminate a joint within a slab panel.
4. Reentrant corners require a contraction joint extending from the corner at 45 degrees or orthogonally to the nearest joint or slab edge.
5. Saw-cut joints within 4-12 hours of placement (before random cracking occurs). Earlier in hot weather, later in cool weather.

### Materials

| Component | Specification |
|-----------|---------------|
| Preformed expansion joint filler | ASTM D1751 (fiber) or ASTM D1752 (closed-cell foam), 1/2-inch thickness |
| Self-leveling joint sealant | ASTM D6690, Type I or II; silicone or polyurethane |
| Backer rod | Closed-cell polyethylene foam; diameter 25% larger than joint width |
| Dowel bars (construction joints) | Smooth, round, epoxy-coated steel per ASTM A615; diameter = slab thickness / 8 |

### Notes

1. Do not saw-cut joints deeper than 1/3 of slab thickness — overcutting reduces load transfer.
2. Isolation joints required around all columns, manholes, hydrants, light poles, and other fixed objects within the slab.
3. Where control joints intersect expansion joints, the expansion joint takes precedence (continues through).
4. Apply sealant only after the joint has fully opened (typically 60-90 days after placement in Kentucky climate).

## Detail P-6: Paver Installation

### Description

Installation details for interlocking concrete pavers (ICPI method) for both vehicular and pedestrian applications.

### Dimensions & Specifications

**Pedestrian Application**

| Layer | Material | Thickness |
|-------|----------|-----------|
| Pavers | Interlocking concrete pavers | 2-3/8 inches (60 mm) min |
| Bedding sand | ASTM C33, concrete sand | 1 inch (compacted) |
| Aggregate base | KYTC DGA or crushed stone | 6 inches (compacted) |
| Subgrade | Compacted native soil | 95% Std. Proctor |

**Vehicular Application**

| Layer | Material | Thickness |
|-------|----------|-----------|
| Pavers | Interlocking concrete pavers | 3-1/8 inches (80 mm) min |
| Bedding sand | ASTM C33, concrete sand | 1 inch (compacted) |
| Aggregate base | KYTC DGA or crushed stone | 8-12 inches (compacted) |
| Subgrade | Compacted native soil | 98% Std. Proctor |

### Materials

| Component | Specification |
|-----------|---------------|
| Concrete pavers | ASTM C936; min. compressive strength 8,000 psi; max. absorption 5% |
| Bedding sand | ASTM C33; clean, washed concrete sand |
| Joint sand | ASTM C144 or polymeric joint sand per manufacturer |
| Edge restraint | Aluminum or steel edging (L-profile, 3/16-inch thick min); precast concrete curb; or cast-in-place concrete curb |
| Geotextile (optional) | Non-woven, AASHTO M288 Class 2; placed between subgrade and aggregate base |

### Notes

1. Edge restraint is required on all sides not bounded by a curb, building, or other rigid structure. Secure edging with 12-inch steel spikes at 24 inches on center.
2. Do not compact bedding sand layer — screed to 1-inch uniform thickness. Pavers are vibrated into the bedding after placement.
3. After paver placement, compact with a plate compactor (minimum 2 passes in each direction). Then sweep joint sand into joints and compact again.
4. For vehicular applications, use a herringbone (45 or 90 degree) laying pattern for optimal interlock.
5. Maintain a minimum 1/8-inch joint between pavers. Maximum 3/16-inch joint width.
6. Cut pavers at edges shall be no smaller than 1/3 of a full unit.

## Detail P-8: Geotextile Fabric Placement Notes

### Description

General notes for geotextile fabric placement beneath pavements, aggregate bases, and in separation/stabilization applications.

### Material Specifications

| Application | Geotextile Type | Specification |
|-------------|----------------|---------------|
| Separation (subgrade/aggregate) | Non-woven | AASHTO M288, Class 2 |
| Stabilization (soft subgrade) | Woven | AASHTO M288, Class 1 |
| Filtration (drainage applications) | Non-woven | AASHTO M288, Class 2; AOS per soil gradation |
| Reinforcement (road base) | Woven, high-strength | Per design; min. tensile strength per ASTM D4595 |

### Installation Requirements

1. Subgrade shall be graded smooth and free of sharp objects, rocks larger than 2 inches, stumps, and debris before fabric placement.
2. Unroll fabric in the direction of aggregate placement (equipment travel).
3. Overlap adjacent fabric rolls a minimum of 18 inches on firm subgrade, 36 inches on soft subgrade (CBR < 3).
4. Pin overlaps with 6-inch wire staples or 12-inch landscape staples at 5 ft O.C. along the overlap.
5. Do not allow equipment to drive directly on geotextile. Place a minimum 6-inch lift of aggregate on the fabric before equipment travel.
6. Repair any tears or punctures with a patch extending a minimum of 24 inches beyond the damaged area in all directions.
7. Protect fabric from UV exposure. Do not leave uncovered for more than 14 days.
