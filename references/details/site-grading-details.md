## General Notes

1. All grading shall conform to the lines, grades, and elevations shown on the approved construction drawings. Contractor shall verify all existing grades and elevations prior to beginning earthwork operations.
2. All earthwork shall be performed in accordance with KYTC Standard Specifications for Road and Bridge Construction, current edition, Section 206 — Excavation and Embankment, unless otherwise noted.
3. Finished grades shall drain positively away from all structures. No ponding shall occur within 10 feet of any building foundation.
4. Contractor shall notify the Engineer of any discrepancies between existing conditions and the information shown on the plans prior to proceeding with grading operations.
5. All fill material shall be free of organic matter, debris, frozen material, and stones larger than 6 inches in diameter. Fill shall be placed in maximum 8-inch loose lifts and compacted to 95% Standard Proctor Density (ASTM D698) unless otherwise specified.
6. Topsoil shall be stripped and stockpiled separately for reuse in landscape areas. Minimum topsoil stripping depth: 6 inches or as encountered, whichever is less.
7. Erosion and sediment control measures shall be installed and maintained in accordance with the approved SWPPP/BMP Plan prior to any land disturbance.
8. All elevations reference NAVD88 datum unless otherwise noted on the plans.
9. Contour interval: 1-foot for finished grading, 2-foot for existing conditions (unless otherwise shown).

## Detail G-1: Typical Slopes Table

### Description

Minimum and maximum slopes for graded surfaces by application. All slopes are measured as vertical rise per horizontal run (V:H) or as a percentage (%). Slopes shall be maintained as shown unless site constraints require deviation, in which case the Engineer shall be notified for approval.

### Dimensions & Specifications

| Application | Minimum Slope | Maximum Slope | Notes |
|-------------|---------------|---------------|-------|
| Building perimeter (first 10 ft) | 2% (1/4 in/ft) | 5% | Away from foundation; positive drainage required |
| Parking lot pavement | 1.5% | 5% | Cross-slope to inlets; 2% preferred |
| Drive aisles | 1% | 8% | Longitudinal grade; max 5% at ADA routes |
| Sidewalks (longitudinal) | 0.5% | 5% (8.33% with ramp) | ADA maximum running slope 5%; ramps per ADA |
| Sidewalks (cross-slope) | 1% | 2% | ADA maximum cross-slope 2% |
| Grassed swales | 1% | 5% | Side slopes 3:1 max; bottom width 2 ft minimum |
| Roadside ditches | 0.5% | 8% | Line with erosion control blanket above 3% |
| Mowed embankments | - | 3:1 (H:V) | Steeper requires retaining wall or erosion control |
| Unmowed embankments | - | 2:1 (H:V) | Requires erosion control blanket and permanent stabilization |
| Detention basin side slopes | - | 3:1 (H:V) | 4:1 preferred for mowing and maintenance access |
| Detention basin bottom | 1% | 2% | Slope to low-flow channel or outlet |
| Riprap-lined channels | 0.5% | 10% | Size riprap per velocity; see drainage details |
| ADA-accessible ramps | - | 8.33% (1:12) | With handrails; 5 ft landing every 30 ft of run |

### Notes

1. Slopes between structures and property lines shall not direct runoff onto adjacent properties without approved drainage easement or pre-existing drainage pattern.
2. All grassed slopes steeper than 4:1 shall receive temporary erosion control blanket during establishment period.
3. Where mowed slopes exceed 4:1, specify "NO MOW" zone and plant with low-maintenance groundcover or native seed mix.
4. Slopes within sight triangles at intersections shall not exceed 6:1 for clear visibility.

## Detail G-2: Retaining Wall General Notes

### Description

General requirements for retaining walls used to transition between grade changes exceeding feasible open slopes. Wall type selection depends on retained height, surcharge loading, soil conditions, and aesthetic requirements.

### Wall Type Selection

| Wall Type | Typical Height Range | Application | Geotechnical Required |
|-----------|---------------------|-------------|----------------------|
| Gravity (mass concrete or stone) | 0-4 ft | Low walls, landscape features | No (under 4 ft) |
| Segmental Retaining Wall (SRW) | 0-6 ft (gravity), 6-20 ft (reinforced) | Commercial, residential, roadway | Yes (above 4 ft) |
| Cast-in-Place Cantilever | 4-25 ft | Structural applications, high loads | Yes |
| Mechanically Stabilized Earth (MSE) | 5-40+ ft | Highway, large commercial, high walls | Yes |
| Sheet Pile | 5-30 ft | Temporary shoring, waterfront, tight sites | Yes |
| Soldier Pile and Lagging | 5-30 ft | Temporary and permanent, urban sites | Yes |

### Dimensions & Specifications

1. **Design Standard:** Retaining walls exceeding 4 feet in exposed height (measured from bottom of footing to top of wall) shall be designed by a licensed Professional Engineer registered in the Commonwealth of Kentucky.
2. **Surcharge Loading:** All walls adjacent to vehicular traffic or parking shall be designed for a minimum surcharge of 250 psf (equivalent to 2 feet of soil) applied within the active pressure zone, or HL-93 loading for walls adjacent to roadways.
3. **Safety Factors:**
   - Overturning: FS >= 2.0
   - Sliding: FS >= 1.5
   - Bearing Capacity: FS >= 3.0
   - Global Stability: FS >= 1.5
4. **Drainage:** All retaining walls shall include a drainage system behind the wall consisting of free-draining granular backfill (KYTC Type II aggregate or ASTM No. 57 stone) and a perforated underdrain pipe (4-inch minimum, AASHTO M252) at the base of the wall, daylighting to a positive outlet or connected to the storm drainage system.
5. **Backfill:** Granular backfill zone shall extend a minimum of 12 inches behind the wall face (or 60% of wall height for reinforced SRW) and be compacted to 95% Standard Proctor (ASTM D698). Do not use heavy compaction equipment within 3 feet of the wall face.
6. **Geogrid Reinforcement (SRW):** Geogrid type, length, and spacing per wall design. Minimum embedment length: 0.7 x wall height or 4 feet, whichever is greater. Geogrid shall be pulled taut and pinned before backfilling.

### Annotation Requirements

All retaining walls shall be annotated on the grading plan with the following:
- Top of wall (TW) elevation at each end and at grade breaks (minimum every 25 ft)
- Bottom of wall (BW) elevation at each end and at grade breaks
- Exposed wall height at each annotation point
- Wall type designation
- Limits of wall (station or dimensions)
- Geogrid lengths and elevations (if reinforced SRW)
- Guardrail or fence requirements at top of wall (required when exposed height exceeds 30 inches)

### Materials

| Component | Specification |
|-----------|---------------|
| SRW units | ASTM C1372; min. compressive strength 3,000 psi; max. absorption 10% |
| Geogrid reinforcement | Per wall engineer's design; MARV tensile strength per ASTM D6637 |
| Drainage aggregate | KYTC Type II or ASTM No. 57 stone |
| Perforated pipe | 4-in min., AASHTO M252 Type S or ASTM F405 |
| Filter fabric | AASHTO M288, Class 2 geotextile |
| Cast-in-place concrete | 4,000 psi min. (28-day); KYTC Class A concrete |
| Reinforcing steel | ASTM A615 Grade 60; epoxy-coated per ASTM A775 in corrosive environments |

### Notes

1. Contractor shall not begin wall construction until geotechnical investigation is complete and wall design is approved by the Engineer (walls over 4 ft).
2. Foundation subgrade shall be inspected and approved by the Geotechnical Engineer prior to placement of leveling pad or footing.
3. Weep holes (4-inch diameter PVC, spaced 10 ft on center) required in cast-in-place walls where underdrain cannot daylight.
4. Walls adjacent to public right-of-way or within utility easements require agency review and approval.
5. Provide a minimum 6-inch leveling pad of compacted KYTC Type II aggregate beneath SRW units.

## Detail G-3: Earthwork Transitions

### Description

Details for transitions between cut and fill areas, daylight lines, and benching requirements for fill placement on existing slopes.

### Cut-to-Fill Transition

1. At the transition from cut to fill, remove all topsoil, organic material, and unsuitable soils from the existing ground surface before placing fill.
2. Scarify existing ground surface to a minimum depth of 6 inches and recompact to 95% Standard Proctor before placing fill.
3. Where fill is placed on slopes steeper than 4:1 (H:V), bench the existing slope in horizontal steps. Bench height: 12-18 inches. Bench width: minimum 4 feet or as directed by Geotechnical Engineer.
4. Moisture-condition all fill material to within 2% of optimum moisture content (ASTM D698) before compaction.

### Daylight Lines

1. Daylight lines represent the intersection of proposed graded slopes with existing ground.
2. All daylight lines shall be contained within the project limits or within approved grading easements on adjacent properties.
3. Show daylight lines on the grading plan with a dashed line labeled "LIMITS OF GRADING" or "DAYLIGHT LINE."
4. Provide a minimum 5-foot horizontal offset from daylight lines to property lines to allow for construction tolerances.
5. Where daylight lines approach property boundaries, verify existing grades by field survey before construction.

### Dimensions & Specifications

| Parameter | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| Topsoil stripping depth | 6 inches minimum or to competent subgrade |
| Scarification depth at cut/fill transition | 6 inches minimum |
| Bench height (fill on slope) | 12-18 inches |
| Bench width (fill on slope) | 4 feet minimum |
| Compaction (general fill) | 95% Standard Proctor (ASTM D698) |
| Compaction (under pavements) | 98% Standard Proctor, top 12 inches |
| Compaction (under structures) | 98% Standard Proctor, full depth, or per Geotechnical Report |
| Maximum lift thickness (loose) | 8 inches |
| Moisture content tolerance | Within +/- 2% of optimum |

### Notes

1. Contractor shall provide compaction testing at a frequency of one test per 2,000 SF per lift, or as directed by the Geotechnical Engineer.
2. Do not place fill on frozen ground or incorporate frozen material into fill.
3. Rock fill shall be placed in maximum 18-inch lifts with voids filled with granular material and compacted with vibratory equipment.

## Detail G-4: Subgrade Preparation

### Description

Subgrade preparation requirements for pavements, buildings, and other improvements. Proper subgrade preparation is critical to long-term performance of overlying construction.

### Dimensions & Specifications

| Application | Subgrade Depth | Compaction | Additional Requirements |
|-------------|---------------|------------|------------------------|
| Asphalt pavement | Top 12 inches | 98% Std. Proctor | Proof roll with loaded tandem axle truck |
| Concrete pavement | Top 12 inches | 98% Std. Proctor | Proof roll; uniform support required |
| Building slab-on-grade | Full depth to competent soil | 98% Std. Proctor | Per Geotechnical Report |
| Sidewalks | Top 6 inches | 95% Std. Proctor | Verify no organic material |
| Utility trenches | Trench bottom | 95% Std. Proctor | See utility details for bedding |

### Proof Rolling Requirements

1. Proof roll all pavement subgrades with a loaded tandem axle dump truck (minimum gross vehicle weight 25 tons) making a minimum of two complete passes over the entire subgrade area.
2. Proof rolling shall be observed by the Geotechnical Engineer or qualified testing firm.
3. Areas exhibiting deflection, pumping, or rutting greater than 1 inch shall be undercut and replaced with approved granular material (KYTC DGA or crushed stone) and recompacted.
4. Proof rolling shall not be performed when the subgrade is saturated or frozen.

### Undercut Detail

Where unsuitable subgrade is encountered:
1. Excavate unsuitable material to competent subgrade or to a minimum depth of 18 inches below subgrade elevation, whichever is greater.
2. Place non-woven geotextile fabric (AASHTO M288, Class 2) on the bottom and sides of the undercut area.
3. Backfill with KYTC Dense Graded Aggregate (DGA) or approved crushed stone in 8-inch lifts, compacted to 98% Standard Proctor.
4. Document all undercut locations, depths, and limits for as-built records.

### Notes

1. Subgrade shall be protected from traffic and weather after preparation. If subgrade is disturbed or becomes saturated after proof rolling, re-prepare and re-proof roll at no additional cost to the Owner.
2. Notify the Engineer immediately if unsuitable soils, springs, or groundwater are encountered during subgrade preparation.

## Detail G-5: Building Pad Preparation

### Description

Preparation of building pad areas for slab-on-grade and foundation construction. Requirements supplement the Geotechnical Report, which governs where conflicts exist.

### Dimensions & Specifications

| Layer | Material | Thickness | Compaction |
|-------|----------|-----------|------------|
| Existing subgrade | Native soil (stripped of topsoil) | - | Scarify 6 in, compact to 98% Std. Proctor |
| Structural fill (if required) | KYTC Select Granular Fill or per Geotech | Per Geotech Report | 98% Std. Proctor in 8-in lifts |
| Capillary break | ASTM No. 57 or No. 67 stone | 4 inches minimum | Hand tamp; no heavy equipment directly on stone |
| Vapor barrier | 15-mil polyethylene (ASTM E1745, Class A) | - | Lap joints 12 in min, tape all seams |
| Sand blotter (optional) | Clean washed sand | 2 inches | Leveled, not compacted |
| Concrete slab | Per structural drawings | Per structural | Per ACI 318 and structural engineer |

### Notes

1. Building pad elevation shall match the Finished Floor Elevation (FFE) shown on the plans minus the slab thickness, capillary break, and vapor barrier.
2. Verify FFE provides a minimum 6-inch freeboard above the 100-year flood elevation (where applicable) and a minimum 8 inches above adjacent exterior grade.
3. All underground utilities, sleeves, and sub-slab plumbing shall be installed and tested before vapor barrier placement.
4. Do not puncture the vapor barrier. Repair any tears or punctures with compatible tape and a minimum 12-inch overlap patch.
5. Geotechnical Engineer shall inspect and approve the building pad subgrade before structural fill or capillary break placement.

### Notes

1. All accessible routes shall connect building entrances to the public right-of-way, accessible parking spaces, and site amenities.
2. Changes in level up to 1/4 inch may be vertical. Changes between 1/4 inch and 1/2 inch shall be beveled at 1:2. Changes greater than 1/2 inch require a ramp.
3. Grates and openings in walking surfaces shall have openings no greater than 1/2 inch in the direction of travel.
4. Score joints (1/4-inch max width) shall be used instead of tooled joints at accessible routes to minimize trip hazards.
5. Where walkways cross vehicular routes, provide detectable warning surfaces (truncated domes) per ADAAG 705.

## Detail G-7: Erosion Control Blanket on Slopes

### Description

Installation of erosion control blanket (ECB) on graded slopes to prevent erosion during vegetation establishment. ECB type varies by slope steepness and expected flow conditions.

### Dimensions & Specifications

| Slope Condition | ECB Type | Longevity | Anchoring |
|-----------------|----------|-----------|-----------|
| 3:1 or flatter | Single-net straw/coconut (S75) | 12 months | Staples at 4 ft O.C. (grid pattern) |
| 3:1 to 2:1 | Double-net straw/coconut (S150) | 24 months | Staples at 3 ft O.C. (grid pattern) |
| 2:1 to 1.5:1 | Double-net coconut (C125) | 36 months | Staples at 2 ft O.C. with check slots |
| Channel lining (low flow) | Turf Reinforcement Mat (TRM) — permanent | Permanent | Per manufacturer; 4-inch min staples |
| Channel lining (high flow) | TRM with permanent reinforcement | Permanent | Per manufacturer; 6-inch min staples |

### Installation Procedure

1. Prepare seedbed: apply seed and fertilizer per seeding specifications before blanket installation.
2. Begin at the top of slope. Anchor blanket in a 6-inch deep by 6-inch wide anchor trench at the crest. Backfill and compact the anchor trench.
3. Unroll blanket down the slope in the direction of flow. Maintain direct contact with the soil — no voids or tenting.
4. Overlap adjacent rolls a minimum of 4 inches (side laps) and 6 inches (end laps). Shingle end laps in the direction of flow.
5. Secure blanket with 6-inch wire staples (11-gauge, U-shaped) driven flush with the soil surface. Staple pattern per slope condition table above.
6. At the toe of slope, extend blanket a minimum of 24 inches beyond the toe and anchor in a terminal trench or overlap onto the flat area.

### Materials

| Component | Specification |
|-----------|---------------|
| Erosion control blanket | Per KDOW BMP Manual and ECTC guidelines; C-factor per design |
| Wire staples | 11-gauge wire, 6-inch legs, 1-inch crown; galvanized |
| Seed | Per permanent or temporary seeding specifications |
| Fertilizer | Per soil test recommendations or 10-20-20 at 400 lbs/acre |

### Notes

1. Do not install ECB on frozen ground or over standing water.
2. Inspect ECB after every rainfall event exceeding 0.5 inches. Repair any dislodged areas immediately.
3. ECB shall remain in place until vegetation is established to 80% coverage density.
4. For permanent channel applications, use Turf Reinforcement Mat (TRM) with minimum permissible shear stress matching design flow conditions.
