## General Notes

1. All erosion and sediment control (ESC) measures shall be installed in accordance with the approved Stormwater Pollution Prevention Plan (SWPPP), the KDOW (Kentucky Division of Water) Best Management Practices Manual, and the Kentucky Erosion Prevention and Sediment Control Field Guide.
2. ESC measures shall be installed before any land-disturbing activity begins and shall remain in place and be maintained until permanent stabilization is achieved (80% vegetative coverage or equivalent permanent cover).
3. A Kentucky NPDES KYR10 General Permit (Notice of Intent — NOI) is required for land disturbance of 1 acre or more, or for disturbance of less than 1 acre that is part of a common plan of development exceeding 1 acre.
4. Inspections of all ESC measures shall be conducted a minimum of once every 7 calendar days and within 24 hours of a rainfall event producing 0.5 inches or more of precipitation. Inspection reports shall be maintained on-site and available for review.
5. Damaged, displaced, or ineffective ESC measures shall be repaired or replaced within 24 hours of discovery.
6. Sediment that accumulates in ESC measures (silt fence, inlet protection, sediment basins) shall be removed when it reaches 50% of the design storage capacity.
7. All ESC measures shall be removed after final stabilization is achieved, and the disturbed areas beneath the removed measures shall be permanently stabilized.
8. The Contractor is responsible for preventing sediment-laden runoff from leaving the construction site and for preventing the tracking of mud onto public roads.

## Detail E-1: Silt Fence Installation

### Description

Silt fence is the primary perimeter sediment control BMP for construction sites. It intercepts sheet flow runoff and allows sediment to settle before water passes through the fabric. Silt fence is not designed for concentrated flow or channel applications.

### Dimensions & Specifications

| Parameter | Standard Silt Fence | Wire-Backed Silt Fence (Heavy Duty) |
|-----------|--------------------|------------------------------------|
| Fabric height (above grade) | 24 inches minimum, 36 inches maximum | 24-36 inches |
| Post type | Hardwood stakes (2x2 in min) or steel T-posts | Steel T-posts |
| Post length | 36-48 inches (driven 12-18 inches into ground) | 48-60 inches (driven 18-24 inches) |
| Post spacing | 6 feet maximum O.C. | 8 feet maximum O.C. (wire provides structural support) |
| Toe-in trench | 6-inch wide x 6-inch deep; bury bottom of fabric and backfill with compacted soil | Same |
| Wire backing | N/A | 14-gauge welded wire mesh or woven wire, attached to upslope side of fabric |
| Maximum drainage area | 1/4 acre per 100 linear feet of fence | 1/2 acre per 100 linear feet (with wire backing) |
| Maximum slope behind fence | 2:1 (H:V) | 2:1 (H:V) |

### Fabric Specifications

| Property | Minimum Value | Test Method |
|----------|---------------|-------------|
| Tensile strength | 100 lbs (grab) | ASTM D4632 |
| Elongation at failure | 15-30% | ASTM D4632 |
| Permittivity | 0.05 sec^-1 | ASTM D4491 |
| Apparent opening size (AOS) | 20-100 (US Sieve No. 20-100) | ASTM D4751 |
| UV resistance (% strength retained after 500 hrs) | 70% | ASTM D4355 |

### Installation Procedure

1. Excavate a toe-in trench 6 inches wide by 6 inches deep along the entire fence alignment.
2. Set posts on the downslope side of the fabric at 6-foot maximum spacing (8-foot for wire-backed). Drive posts 12-18 inches into firm ground.
3. Attach fabric to the downslope side of the posts with wire ties, heavy-duty staples (minimum 1-inch), or integrated post pockets. Fabric shall be taut between posts.
4. Place the bottom 6 inches of fabric in the trench, extending upstream (upslope). Backfill trench with compacted native soil.
5. Overlap adjacent fabric rolls a minimum of 6 inches, wrapping around a common post. Do not leave gaps between rolls.
6. For wire-backed silt fence: attach wire mesh to the upslope side of the posts; attach fabric to the upslope side of the wire mesh.

### Notes

1. Silt fence is effective only for sheet flow. Do not place silt fence across channels, swales, ditches, or concentrated flow paths — use check dams, sediment traps, or basins for concentrated flow.
2. Install silt fence along the contour (parallel to slope contours), not up-and-down the slope.
3. Turn the ends of silt fence upslope (J-hook) to prevent runoff from flowing around the ends.
4. Silt fence shall not be used as a primary BMP for drainage areas exceeding 1 acre per 200 linear feet of fence.
5. Remove accumulated sediment when it reaches 1/3 of the exposed fence height (approximately 50% of design storage).

## Detail E-2: Construction Entrance

### Description

Stabilized construction entrance to prevent tracking of mud and sediment from the construction site onto public roads. Required at all points of ingress and egress.

### Dimensions & Specifications

| Parameter | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| Entrance length | 50 feet minimum (75-100 feet preferred for active construction sites) |
| Entrance width | 20 feet minimum (full width of travel way) |
| Aggregate depth | 6 inches minimum (8 inches preferred) |
| Aggregate type | KYTC No. 2 or No. 3 stone (3-inch to 6-inch washed stone) |
| Geotextile fabric | Non-woven, AASHTO M288 Class 2; placed beneath aggregate |
| Perimeter | Silt fence or earthen berm along both sides to contain runoff |
| Drainage | Grade to drain toward the site (not toward the public road); provide a sediment trap or basin at the low end if significant drainage area contributes to the entrance |
| Tire wash (optional) | Required if tracking persists despite aggregate entrance; pressurized water system with sediment collection |

### Materials

| Component | Specification |
|-----------|---------------|
| Aggregate | KYTC No. 2 or No. 3 stone (clean, washed) |
| Geotextile | Non-woven, AASHTO M288 Class 2 |
| Silt fence | Per Detail E-1 |

### Notes

1. The construction entrance shall be the only point of vehicle access to and from the site. Close and barricade all other potential access points.
2. Replenish aggregate as it becomes fouled with mud. Do not allow the aggregate surface to become a muddy, ineffective surface.
3. If mud tracking onto the public road occurs despite the stabilized entrance, the Contractor shall immediately clean the road (sweeping, not washing) and address the source of tracking.
4. At project completion, remove geotextile and contaminated aggregate. Restore the entrance area to permanent condition per the site plan.
5. Provide signage at the construction entrance: "CONSTRUCTION ENTRANCE — ALL VEHICLES MUST ENTER AND EXIT HERE."

## Detail E-3: Sediment Basin

### Description

Temporary sediment basin for capturing and settling sediment from disturbed construction areas with drainage areas generally greater than 5 acres. Sediment basins provide the highest level of sediment removal on construction sites.

### Dimensions & Specifications

| Parameter | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| Minimum storage volume | 3,600 cubic feet per acre of contributing drainage area (or per KDOW BMP Manual) |
| Surface area (at design depth) | 1% of contributing drainage area minimum |
| Length-to-width ratio | 2:1 minimum (3:1 preferred); use baffles if geometry cannot achieve |
| Depth | 3-5 feet (3-foot minimum wet storage + freeboard) |
| Side slopes | 3:1 (H:V) maximum; 4:1 preferred |
| Embankment top width | 8 feet minimum |
| Embankment compaction | 95% Standard Proctor in 8-inch lifts |
| Outlet structure | Perforated riser pipe (with skimmer) or spillway with stone outlet |
| Emergency spillway | Vegetated earth channel; capacity to pass 25-year, 24-hour storm without overtopping embankment |
| Freeboard | 1 foot minimum above the emergency spillway crest to top of embankment |
| Dewatering device | Floating skimmer (preferred) or perforated riser with gravel jacket |

### Outlet Sizing

| Component | Specification |
|-----------|---------------|
| Principal spillway (riser) | Size to dewater the basin within 48-72 hours |
| Riser diameter | 12-inch minimum |
| Riser perforations | 1-inch diameter holes at 6-inch O.C. (bottom half); wrapped with geotextile and gravel jacket |
| Barrel pipe | 12-inch minimum RCP or HDPE; slope 1% minimum |
| Anti-seep collar | One collar minimum at mid-embankment |
| Emergency spillway | Sized to pass 25-year, 24-hour peak inflow with the principal spillway fully blocked |

### Notes

1. Sediment basins required for drainage areas > 10 acres per KDOW KYR10 General Permit. Recommended for drainage areas > 5 acres.
2. Remove accumulated sediment when storage volume is reduced by 50%.
3. Maintain the basin until all contributing drainage areas are permanently stabilized. Then dewater, remove accumulated sediment, remove the embankment, and restore the area to the final grades shown on the plans.
4. If the sediment basin location coincides with a permanent detention basin, coordinate grading so the temporary basin can be converted to the permanent facility.
5. Sediment-laden water shall not be discharged from the basin to Waters of the Commonwealth without achieving adequate settling (visually clear discharge with no visible plume).

## Detail E-4: Sediment Trap

### Description

Temporary sediment trap for capturing sediment from smaller disturbed areas (less than 5 acres). Simpler and smaller than a sediment basin, using a stone weir outlet instead of a riser structure.

### Dimensions & Specifications

| Parameter | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| Maximum drainage area | 5 acres |
| Minimum storage volume | 3,600 cubic feet per acre of drainage area |
| Shape | Excavated depression or embankment with stone outlet weir |
| Depth | 2-4 feet |
| Side slopes | 2:1 (H:V) or flatter |
| Stone weir outlet | KYTC No. 2 or No. 57 stone; 12-inch minimum depth; crest width per design flow |
| Stone weir length | Per weir flow calculation; minimum 6 feet |
| Geotextile | Wrap filter fabric on upstream side of stone weir |
| Emergency overflow | Low point in embankment, armored with riprap or lined with ECB |

### Notes

1. Sediment traps are appropriate for drainage areas up to 5 acres. For larger drainage areas, use a sediment basin (Detail E-3).
2. Remove sediment when it reaches 50% of design storage depth.
3. Stone weir flow rate must allow the basin to dewater within 48-72 hours after a storm event but slow enough for effective settling.
4. Locate sediment traps at low points in the site where runoff naturally concentrates (swale bottoms, ditch outlets, downstream of disturbed areas).

## Detail E-5: Inlet Protection

### Description

Temporary sediment control measure installed around storm drain inlets to prevent construction sediment from entering the storm drainage system. Multiple inlet protection types are available depending on inlet configuration and flow conditions.

### Type 1 — Stone and Fabric (Standard)

| Parameter | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| Application | Area inlets (grate inlets) in flat to moderate slope areas |
| Stone type | KYTC No. 57 stone |
| Stone ring width | 12 inches minimum around all sides of inlet |
| Stone height | 12-18 inches above inlet grate |
| Filter fabric | Non-woven geotextile wrapped around stone ring, secured to ground |
| Overflow | Stone height shall allow overtopping during large events to prevent flooding |

### Type 2 — Fabric and Stake (Light Duty)

| Parameter | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| Application | Area inlets in low-flow, flat areas |
| Fabric | Non-woven geotextile or filter fabric |
| Stakes | Hardwood 2x2 or metal stakes at 3 ft O.C. around inlet |
| Fabric attachment | Stapled to stakes; bottom of fabric staked to ground |
| Height | 18 inches maximum |

### Type 3 — Block and Gravel (Curb Inlets)

| Parameter | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| Application | Curb-opening inlets |
| Concrete blocks | Standard CMU (8x8x16) laid on side with openings facing outward |
| Block rows | 2 rows maximum (16-inch height) |
| Gravel fill | KYTC No. 57 stone packed into block openings and against upstream face |
| Wire mesh | Hardware cloth (1/2-inch mesh) on upstream side of blocks (optional for fine sediment) |
| Filter fabric | Wrapped around the block/gravel assembly at the base |

### Notes

1. Inlet protection is a secondary BMP — it does not replace perimeter controls (silt fence) or sediment basins.
2. Remove and clean inlet protection after every significant storm event. Replace filter fabric when clogged.
3. Do not allow ponding from inlet protection to encroach on active traffic lanes, adjacent properties, or building pad areas.
4. Remove all inlet protection after final stabilization. Clean inlets and pipe system of all construction sediment before final acceptance (video inspect if required by the local jurisdiction).

## Detail E-6: Check Dam

### Description

Temporary check dam installed in swales, ditches, and channels to reduce flow velocity and promote sediment deposition. Two types: rock check dam and fiber roll (wattle) check dam.

### Rock Check Dam

| Parameter | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| Application | Channels and ditches with contributing drainage area < 10 acres |
| Rock type | KYTC Class I riprap or No. 2 stone |
| Dam height | 18 inches maximum (center of channel to top of rock) |
| Center weir | Low point at center of dam (6 inches lower than edges) to direct flow to channel center |
| Wing height | Top of rock at edges shall be 6 inches higher than center to direct overflow to center |
| Spacing | Space so the toe of the upstream dam is at the elevation of the crest of the downstream dam |
| Geotextile | Place on upstream side of rock to improve sediment capture |

### Fiber Roll (Wattle) Check Dam

| Parameter | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| Application | Channels and swales with low velocity (< 3 fps) and shallow flow |
| Roll diameter | 9-12 inches |
| Roll material | Straw, coconut fiber, or excelsior (wood fiber) enclosed in tubular netting |
| Staking | Hardwood stakes (2x2) or rebar through the center of the roll at 3 ft O.C. |
| Trenching | Set roll in a 3-inch deep trench to prevent undermining |
| Spacing | Same principle as rock check dams (toe-to-crest alignment) |

### Notes

1. Check dams are temporary measures. Remove after the channel is permanently stabilized (riprap, turf, or permanent lining).
2. Remove accumulated sediment when it reaches 50% of dam height.
3. Do not install check dams in flowing streams or jurisdictional waters without a permit.
4. Check dams reduce velocity but do not provide water quality treatment — they are sediment control devices only.
5. Rock check dams are preferred over fiber rolls for higher-velocity channels and larger drainage areas.

## Detail E-7: Temporary Seeding Specifications

### Description

Temporary seeding specifications for stabilizing disturbed areas that will not receive permanent landscaping within 14 days. Temporary cover prevents erosion during construction activities.

### Temporary Seed Mixes (Kentucky)

**Warm Season (May 1 - September 15)**

| Species | Rate (lbs/1,000 SF) | Rate (lbs/acre) |
|---------|---------------------|-----------------|
| German Millet | 1.0 | 40 |
| *or* Sudangrass | 1.0 | 40 |
| *or* Annual Ryegrass | 1.5 | 60 |

**Cool Season (September 16 - April 30)**

| Species | Rate (lbs/1,000 SF) | Rate (lbs/acre) |
|---------|---------------------|-----------------|
| Annual Ryegrass | 1.5 | 60 |
| *or* Cereal Rye (winter rye) | 2.5 | 100 |
| *or* Winter Wheat | 2.5 | 100 |

### Application

1. Prepare seedbed: remove debris, loosen top 2-3 inches of soil by raking or light discing.
2. Apply seed at the specified rate using a broadcast spreader, drill seeder, or hydroseeder.
3. Apply straw mulch at 2 tons per acre (1 bale per 800 SF). Crimp mulch into soil or apply tackifier.
4. On slopes steeper than 3:1, apply erosion control blanket over seed instead of straw mulch (see Detail G-7).
5. Apply starter fertilizer (10-20-20) at 400 lbs/acre if soil test is not available.

### Notes

1. Temporary stabilization is required on all disturbed areas that will remain inactive for 14 or more days (per KYR10 permit).
2. Temporary seeding does not satisfy the permanent stabilization requirement. Areas that have received temporary seed must still receive permanent seed or other permanent cover.
3. If the area will be re-disturbed within 30 days, temporary seeding is still required — it reduces erosion during the interim period.
4. Water temporarily seeded areas during dry periods to promote germination. Minimum 1/2 inch of water within 48 hours of seeding if no rain occurs.

### Description

Permanent seeding specifications for final stabilization of all disturbed areas. See also Detail L-13 (Seeding and Sodding Specifications) in the Landscape Details for lawn-quality turf specifications.

| Species | Percentage by Weight | Rate (lbs/acre) |
|---------|---------------------|-----------------|
| Little Bluestem | 25% | 10 (PLS) |
| Indiangrass | 20% | 8 (PLS) |
| Switchgrass | 15% | 6 (PLS) |
| Virginia Wildrye | 15% | 6 (PLS) |
| Native wildflower mix (regional ecotype) | 25% | 4 (PLS) |
| **Total** | **100%** | **~34 (PLS)** |

*PLS = Pure Live Seed. Adjust bulk seed rate based on germination and purity percentages on the seed tag.*

### Seeding Dates

| Mix | Primary Window | Secondary Window |
|-----|---------------|-----------------|
| Mix 1 | September 1 - October 15 | March 1 - April 30 |
| Mix 2 | September 1 - October 15 | March 15 - May 1 |
| Mix 3 | November 1 - March 1 (dormant seed) | N/A (dormant seeding only for natives) |

### Soil Preparation

1. Amend soil per soil test results. If no soil test: apply agricultural lime at 2 tons/acre and 10-20-20 fertilizer at 400 lbs/acre.
2. Prepare seedbed: disc or till to 4-inch depth; final grade with landscape rake or culti-packer.
3. For native seed (Mix 3): do not fertilize — natives prefer low-fertility soils. Mow weeds at 6-inch height during the first growing season but do not mow native seedlings.

### Notes

1. Permanent stabilization is defined as 80% vegetative cover over the entire disturbed area, including concentrated flow paths.
2. If permanent cover is not achieved within one growing season, re-seed and re-mulch bare areas.
3. Do not mow permanently seeded areas until grass reaches 4 inches in height. Mow to 3 inches; do not scalp.
4. Native seed mixes (Mix 3) require 2-3 growing seasons to fully establish. Manage expectations with the owner and permitting agencies.

## Detail E-9: Erosion Control Blanket Specifications

### Description

Rolled erosion control products (RECPs) for temporary and permanent erosion protection on slopes and in channels. ECB type is selected based on slope steepness, expected flow velocity, and required longevity. See also Detail G-7 (Erosion Control Blanket on Slopes) in the Site Grading Details.

### Product Selection Matrix

| Application | Slope/Velocity | Product Type | Longevity | C-Factor |
|-------------|---------------|-------------|-----------|----------|
| Mild slopes (4:1 and flatter) | Sheet flow only | Single-net straw (S75) | 12 months | 0.15 |
| Moderate slopes (3:1 to 2:1) | Sheet flow | Double-net straw/coconut (SC150) | 24 months | 0.10 |
| Steep slopes (2:1 to 1.5:1) | Sheet flow | Double-net coconut (C125) | 36 months | 0.07 |
| Channels (V < 6 fps) | Concentrated flow | Double-net coconut/straw (SC150) | 24 months | N/A |
| Channels (V = 6-10 fps) | Concentrated flow | Turf Reinforcement Mat (TRM) | Permanent | N/A |
| Channels (V > 10 fps) | Concentrated flow | TRM with permanent reinforcement | Permanent | N/A |

### Material Specifications

| Property | Straw/Coconut Blankets | Turf Reinforcement Mats (TRM) |
|----------|----------------------|------------------------------|
| Standard | ECTC/TxDOT classification | ECTC guidelines |
| Mass per unit area | Per classification | 8-16 oz/SY |
| UV stability (500 hrs) | 80% strength retained | 80% strength retained |
| Net type | Photodegradable or biodegradable | UV-stabilized permanent |
| Permissible shear stress (unvegetated) | 1.5-2.0 lbs/SF | 2.0-6.0 lbs/SF |
| Permissible shear stress (vegetated) | 2.0-3.0 lbs/SF | 6.0-12.0+ lbs/SF |

### Installation

See Detail G-7 (Site Grading Details) for complete installation procedure. Key points:
1. Seed the surface before blanket placement.
2. Anchor in a 6-inch x 6-inch trench at the top of the slope.
3. Unroll downslope in the direction of flow.
4. Overlap side laps 4 inches, end laps 6 inches (shingled in flow direction).
5. Staple per manufacturer's schedule and slope condition table.

### Notes

1. ECB is a complement to vegetation — not a substitute. Always seed beneath the blanket.
2. Select biodegradable/photodegradable blankets for temporary applications. Use permanent TRM only where long-term reinforcement is needed (channels, steep slopes).
3. Inspect after every rain event. Re-anchor any areas where the blanket has pulled away from the soil surface.
4. For KDOW compliance, ECB performance must meet or exceed the C-factor assumed in the sediment yield calculations of the SWPPP.

## Detail E-10: Stabilized Construction Access

### Description

Supplementary stabilization measures for construction access points beyond the primary construction entrance (Detail E-2). Includes temporary access roads, crane pads, and staging areas.

### Dimensions & Specifications

| Application | Surface Treatment | Thickness | Base |
|-------------|-------------------|-----------|------|
| Temporary access road | KYTC No. 2 or DGA stone | 6 inches minimum | Geotextile on subgrade |
| Crane pad / equipment staging | KYTC DGA | 12 inches minimum | Geotextile; compact to 95% Std. Proctor |
| Material staging area | KYTC No. 2 stone | 4 inches minimum | Geotextile |
| Temporary parking | KYTC DGA or No. 2 | 6 inches minimum | Geotextile |

### Notes

1. All temporary access roads shall have perimeter sediment controls (silt fence, gravel berms) to prevent runoff from carrying sediment off the stabilized surface.
2. Grade temporary access roads to drain toward the interior of the site, not toward the public road or off-site drainage.
3. Remove all temporary stone, geotextile, and contaminated soil at project completion. Restore areas to final grades per the approved plans.
4. Temporary access roads shall not be located in floodplains, wetlands, or stream buffers without specific approval from the applicable regulatory agencies.

## Detail E-11: Concrete Washout Area

### Description

Designated concrete washout area to prevent concrete washwater and residual concrete from entering the storm drainage system or Waters of the Commonwealth.

### Dimensions & Specifications

| Parameter | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| Location | Minimum 50 feet from storm drains, drainage channels, and property boundaries |
| Size | 10 ft x 10 ft minimum (larger for high-volume pours) |
| Type (above-ground) | Prefabricated steel/plastic container with 10-mil poly liner |
| Type (below-grade) | Excavated pit lined with 10-mil polyethylene; 12-inch freeboard |
| Capacity | Sufficient to hold all washwater from a day's concrete operations |
| Signage | "CONCRETE WASHOUT — ALL CONCRETE WASTE MUST BE DEPOSITED HERE" |
| Berming | 12-inch compacted soil berm around pit (below-grade type) |

### Notes

1. All concrete trucks, pumps, and tools shall be washed only in the designated washout area. No washwater shall be discharged to the ground surface, ditches, inlets, or storm drains.
2. Solid concrete waste may be allowed to harden in the washout and then removed as solid waste to a licensed landfill.
3. Liquid washwater shall not be discharged without treatment. Allow water to evaporate or pump to a sanitary sewer (with utility permission) or licensed waste hauler.
4. Replace or clean the washout when it reaches 75% capacity.
5. At project completion, remove all hardened concrete, liner material, and contaminated soil. Restore the area to final grade.
6. The washout area location shall be shown on the SWPPP site map and identified during the pre-construction meeting.

## Detail E-12: Inspection and Maintenance Schedule Template

### Description

Template for ESC inspection and maintenance tracking per the KDOW KYR10 General Permit requirements.

### Inspection Frequency

| Trigger | Requirement |
|---------|-------------|
| Routine inspection | Every 7 calendar days during active construction |
| Rain event inspection | Within 24 hours of a rainfall event producing 0.5 inches or more |
| Post-stabilization | Monthly until NOT (Notice of Termination) is filed |

### Inspection Checklist

| BMP | Inspection Item | Acceptable Condition | Corrective Action Required |
|-----|----------------|---------------------|---------------------------|
| Silt fence | Fabric intact, posts upright, toe buried | No tears > 6 in; no undermining; sediment < 1/3 fence height | Repair/replace within 24 hours |
| Construction entrance | Aggregate clean, adequate depth | No mud tracking on public road; 6-inch min stone depth | Replenish stone; sweep road |
| Sediment basin/trap | Outlet functional, embankment stable | Sediment < 50% of design volume; no erosion on embankment | Clean out sediment; repair embankment |
| Inlet protection | Filter in place, not clogged | Fabric/stone intact; sediment < 50% capacity | Remove sediment; replace fabric |
| Check dams | Intact, not flanked | Rock/fiber roll in place; sediment < 50% dam height | Rebuild; remove sediment |
| Erosion control blanket | In contact with soil, anchored | No tenting, tears, or displacement | Re-anchor; repair tears |
| Temporary seeding | Germination, coverage | 80% coverage for permanent; visible germination for temporary | Re-seed bare areas |
| Concrete washout | Contained, not overflowing | < 75% capacity; no discharge | Clean out; repair liner |
| Stabilized areas | Maintained vegetation | 80% vegetative cover; no rills or gullies | Re-seed; repair erosion |

### Inspection Report Requirements

Each inspection report shall include:
1. Inspector name, date, and time of inspection
2. Weather conditions (rain in past 24 hours, rainfall amount)
3. Phase of construction and areas currently disturbed
4. Condition of each BMP (using checklist above)
5. Description of any discharges observed
6. Corrective actions needed, responsible party, and deadline
7. Corrective actions completed from previous inspections
8. Photographs of deficiencies and corrective actions
9. Inspector signature and certification

### Notes

1. Inspection reports shall be maintained on-site in the SWPPP binder and available for review by KDOW, local MS4 program, or other regulatory inspectors at all times.
2. Retain all inspection reports for a minimum of 3 years after the Notice of Termination (NOT) is filed.
3. The SWPPP shall be updated to reflect any changes in BMPs, site conditions, or construction phasing within 7 days of the change.
4. Failure to inspect, maintain, or document ESC measures may result in enforcement action, fines, and stop-work orders from KDOW or the local MS4 authority.
5. For JEDS projects, the Engineer or Landscape Architect shall conduct or review ESC inspections at critical milestones: pre-construction, after initial grading, after utility installation, and before final stabilization sign-off.
