## Overview

The City of Lebanon is the county seat of Marion County, Kentucky, located approximately 60 miles southeast of Louisville along US 68. Lebanon operates under a mayor-council form of government. Planning and zoning within the city limits are administered by the Lebanon-Marion County Planning Commission, which serves as the joint planning body for both the city and unincorporated areas of Marion County. Zoning regulations are codified in the Lebanon-Marion County Zoning Ordinance.

Lebanon is a smaller municipality with a more streamlined regulatory environment than Louisville or Lexington. The planning staff is compact, and personal relationships with planning staff and commissioners are important for efficient project processing.
