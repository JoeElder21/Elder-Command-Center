# Agency Context

| Agency | Jurisdiction | Relevance |
|--------|-------------|-----------|
| LFUCG | Lexington-Fayette Urban County | Zoning, subdivision, stormwater |
| Elizabethtown P&Z | City of Elizabethtown | Planning and zoning review |
| Lebanon/Marion County | City of Lebanon / Marion County | Planning, permitting |
| KYTC | Kentucky Transportation Cabinet | Encroachment permits, roadway access |
| Army Corps of Engineers | Federal | Wetlands, Section 404 permits, floodplain |

Always reference the correct jurisdiction for each project. Never assume one jurisdiction's standards apply to another.
