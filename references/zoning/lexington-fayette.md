## Overview

Lexington-Fayette Urban County Government (LFUCG) is a merged city-county government encompassing all of Fayette County, Kentucky. It is the second-largest city in Kentucky by population. Planning and zoning are administered through the Division of Planning within the Department of Planning, Preservation and Development. Zoning regulations are codified in the Lexington-Fayette Urban County Zoning Ordinance (Articles 1-33). The Comprehensive Plan (known as "Goals and Objectives") guides long-range land use policy, and the Urban Service Area boundary defines the extent of urban infrastructure services.

## Assumptions & Verification Notes

- All dimensional standards, parking ratios, and procedural requirements are based on general knowledge of LFUCG zoning practices. **Every value marked [VERIFY] must be confirmed with the current LFUCG Zoning Ordinance and Land Subdivision Regulations.**
- LFUCG has undergone multiple zoning ordinance updates. Confirm the current effective edition before relying on any specific article or section number.
- The Comprehensive Plan (Goals and Objectives) has been updated periodically. Verify the current plan designation for any specific property before preparing CUP applications.
- LFUCG stormwater requirements are administered by the Division of Water Quality, separate from the Division of Planning. Coordinate with both divisions early in the process.
- Kentucky American Water (a private utility) provides water service in Lexington. Sewer service is provided by LFUCG. Coordinate with both entities for capacity verification.
- The Urban Service Area boundary is a critical constraint in Lexington. Development outside the USA boundary cannot connect to urban water and sewer services. Confirm site is within USA.
- School-related CUP applications in residential zones typically face significant neighborhood opposition. Proactive neighborhood engagement and a robust traffic management plan are essential.
