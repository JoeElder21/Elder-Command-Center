# Deliverables: Catalog, Phase Matrix and Revision Tracking

## Deliverable Types

| 6 | **Construction Observation Report** | Field observation documentation including work completed, materials used, weather conditions, contractor personnel, conformance with plans, and items requiring correction. |
| 7 | **Fee Proposal** | Professional services proposal including scope of work, deliverables, schedule, fee structure (lump sum, hourly, or percentage), and terms and conditions. |
| 8 | **Due Diligence Checklist** | Comprehensive assessment of a development site or acquisition target including zoning, utilities, environmental, geotechnical, flood zone, access, and market factors. |
| 9 | **Development Pro Forma** | Financial analysis of a development project including land cost, hard costs, soft costs, financing, revenue projections, and return metrics (ROI, IRR, cash-on-cash). |
| 10 | **Pre-Application Meeting Prep** | Briefing document for agency pre-application meetings including project description, site plan, zoning analysis, anticipated variances, and discussion questions. |
| 11 | **Punch List Closeout** | Itemized list of incomplete or defective work requiring correction before project acceptance. Includes location, description, responsible party, and deadline. |
| 12 | **Change Order Review** | Analysis of contractor change order requests including scope review, cost verification, schedule impact, and recommendation to approve, negotiate, or deny. |
| 13 | **Client Progress Report** | Periodic project status report including work completed, upcoming milestones, budget status, schedule status, issues, and decisions needed from the client. |

| 27 | **Environmental Review** | Screening-level environmental assessment including wetlands, threatened species, cultural resources, hazardous materials, and regulatory implications. |

## Revision Tracking

### Status Definitions

| Status | Meaning | Action |
|--------|---------|--------|
| **Draft** | Initial production, not reviewed | Internal use only — do not distribute |
| **In Review** | Under QC review or client review | Collect comments, do not revise yet |
| **Revised** | Updated based on review comments | Ready for re-review or distribution |
| **Final** | Approved for use, no further changes expected | Official record — archive |
| **Superseded** | Replaced by a newer version | Mark clearly, retain for record |

### Revision Log Format

Every deliverable includes a revision log at the top:

```
## Revision Log

| Rev | Date | Author | Description |
|-----|------|--------|-------------|
| 0 | [Date] | [Author] | Initial draft |
| 1 | [Date] | [Author] | Revised per [reviewer] comments dated [date] |
| 2 | [Date] | [Author] | Final — approved by [approver] |
```

Rules:
- Rev 0 is always the initial draft
- Each revision includes a description of what changed and why
- Reviewer/approver names are included
- Date format: YYYY-MM-DD
- Superseded versions are not deleted — they are marked and retained
