## Work Completed This Period

### Storm Sewer Installation (Sta. 28+00 to Sta. 42+50)
- Installed 1,450 LF of 24" RCP storm sewer along the east side of KY-31W between Helmwood Drive and Pear Orchard Road.
- Set 6 Type-A junction boxes and 4 curb inlets per KYTC Standard Drawings.
- Connected new storm system to the existing 48" outfall at the Severns Valley tributary.
- Completed video inspection of installed pipe; results submitted to KYTC District 4 for review.

### Curb and Gutter (Sta. 22+00 to Sta. 35+00)
- Placed 2,600 LF of KYTC standard curb and gutter (Type 1) along the widened northbound lane.
- Formed and poured 14 ADA-compliant curb ramps at intersections with Morningside Drive and Woodland Drive.

### Subgrade Preparation
- Completed proof rolling and geotechnical verification of subgrade from Sta. 22+00 to Sta. 32+00.
- Undercut and replaced 180 CY of unsuitable material near Sta. 29+50 (localized wet area). Replaced with KYTC Class II fill and recompacted to 95% Standard Proctor density.

### Erosion Control
- Installed 2,200 LF of silt fence along the project's eastern limits adjacent to Severns Valley tributary.
- Seeded and mulched 0.8 acres of completed earthwork areas per the approved SWPPP.
- Passed KYTC erosion control inspection on June 18, 2026 with no deficiencies noted.

### Utility Coordination
- AT&T completed aerial cable relocation from Sta. 30+00 to Sta. 36+00.
- LG&E completed gas main lowering at the Helmwood Drive intersection.

## Schedule Status

**Current Status: Approximately 2 weeks behind the approved baseline schedule.**

The project experienced 9 rain days in June 2026 (versus 4 anticipated), which delayed storm sewer installation and subgrade operations. A total of 14 weather days have been logged against the contract through the end of June. The contractor has submitted a formal request for a 14-calendar-day time extension per KYTC Standard Specifications Section 108.08.

The critical path currently runs through storm sewer installation and base stone placement. The contractor plans to add a second pipe crew beginning the week of July 7 to recover schedule. If weather cooperates in July, the project team believes 7-10 days can be recovered before paving operations begin in August.

A revised CPM schedule will be submitted to KYTC District 4 by July 11, 2026.

## Issues and Concerns

### 1. AT&T Aerial Relocation Delays
AT&T's relocation contractor (MasTec) has fallen behind their committed schedule for aerial cable relocation north of Sta. 36+00. Their revised completion date of July 18 conflicts with planned base stone operations in that area. Elder Engineering has escalated through KYTC's Utility Section and requested a coordination meeting with AT&T for the week of July 7.

**Risk:** If AT&T does not complete relocation by July 18, base stone and paving operations from Sta. 36+00 to Sta. 42+50 will be delayed, potentially adding 1-2 weeks to overall project duration.

### 2. LG&E Coordination at Pear Orchard Road
LG&E has identified that their existing 4" steel gas main at the Pear Orchard Road intersection requires a full replacement (not adjustment) due to the main's age and condition. LG&E will fund the replacement, but the work window overlaps with our planned signal foundation installation. Coordination is ongoing to sequence the two operations.

### 3. Elizabethtown Municipal Water
Elizabethtown Water has not yet confirmed their schedule for the 8" water main adjustment at Pear Orchard Road. Elder Engineering sent a follow-up request on June 27 and is awaiting response. This work must be completed before base stone placement in that area.

### 4. KPDES Permit Compliance
The KPDES KYR10 permit requires stabilization of all disturbed areas within 14 days of final grading. With the accelerated schedule for subgrade work in July, the contractor must maintain adequate seed/mulch crews to stay in compliance. This was discussed at the June 25 progress meeting and the contractor has acknowledged.
