## Project Understanding

Bluegrass Development Group, LLC ("Client") is developing an approximately 15-acre parcel at the northeast corner of West Dixie Avenue (US 31W) and Pear Orchard Road in Elizabethtown, Hardin County, Kentucky. The proposed development, Towne Centre at Elizabethtown, will consist of approximately 125,000 square feet of retail and restaurant space in a multi-building configuration with associated parking, access drives, stormwater management, utilities, and landscaping.

The site is currently undeveloped agricultural land (Crider silt loam, 2--6% slopes per USDA Web Soil Survey). The parcel is zoned B-2 (General Business) under the Elizabethtown Zoning Ordinance and lies within the West Dixie Avenue Overlay District, which imposes additional architectural, signage, and landscaping requirements. Public water is available from Hardin County Water District No. 1 along West Dixie Avenue, and sanitary sewer is available from the Elizabethtown Sewer Department via an existing 12-inch main along Pear Orchard Road.

Elder Engineering ("Engineer") proposes to provide site civil engineering design services as described in this proposal. Architectural, structural, MEP, landscape architecture (beyond code-minimum requirements), and environmental services are not included and are assumed to be provided by others.

## Scope of Services

### Phase 1 -- Due Diligence and Site Analysis

**Fee: $8,500 (Lump Sum)**

Elder Engineering will perform initial site analysis to support the Client's acquisition due diligence and design feasibility evaluation.

**Deliverables:**

1. **Site Reconnaissance** -- Visit the property to document existing conditions including topography, drainage patterns, vegetation, adjacent land uses, visible utilities, and potential constraints.

2. **Utility Research** -- Contact and compile available-service letters from the following utility providers:
   - Hardin County Water District No. 1 (potable water)
   - Elizabethtown Sewer Department (sanitary sewer)
   - Elizabethtown Electric (electric service)
   - Atmos Energy (natural gas)
   - AT&T and Spectrum (telecommunications)

3. **Regulatory Summary Memorandum** -- Prepare a memorandum summarizing applicable regulatory requirements including:
   - Elizabethtown Zoning Ordinance (B-2 district and West Dixie Avenue Overlay)
   - Elizabethtown Subdivision Regulations
   - Elizabethtown Stormwater Management Ordinance
   - KYTC encroachment permit requirements for access to US 31W
   - FEMA floodplain status (FIRM Panel 21093C0215E, effective 12/16/2015)
   - Phase I Environmental Site Assessment recommendations (by others)
   - Kentucky Division of Water requirements (KPDES KYR10 permit, if applicable)

4. **Preliminary Constraints Exhibit** -- Prepare a constraints exhibit on a recent aerial photograph base showing property boundaries, setbacks, easements, floodplain limits, existing utilities, and preliminary access locations.

**Schedule:** 3 weeks from notice to proceed.

---

### Phase 2 -- Preliminary Site Plan

**Fee: $15,000 (Lump Sum)**

Based on the Client's program and conceptual building layouts (provided by Client's architect), Elder Engineering will prepare a preliminary site plan suitable for Client review, tenant coordination, and pre-application meeting with the City of Elizabethtown Planning and Zoning Department.

**Deliverables:**

1. **Preliminary Site Plan (24x36 sheet)** showing:
   - Building footprints and finished floor elevations
   - Parking layout with stall count, drive aisle widths, and ADA-accessible space locations
   - Vehicular access points on West Dixie Avenue and Pear Orchard Road
   - Preliminary grading concept with proposed contours and spot elevations
   - Preliminary stormwater management layout (detention basin location and approximate sizing)
   - Utility service routing (water, sewer, gas, electric)
   - Setback and buffer yard compliance summary

2. **Preliminary Stormwater Narrative** -- Brief narrative describing the stormwater management approach, preliminary detention sizing using the Rational Method, and water quality treatment strategy.

3. **Pre-Application Meeting Support** -- Attend one pre-application meeting with City of Elizabethtown Planning & Zoning to present the preliminary plan and receive initial feedback.

4. **Traffic Impact Study Coordination** -- Coordinate with the Client's traffic engineer (by others) to ensure access point locations and turn-lane requirements are incorporated into the site design.

**Schedule:** 5 weeks from Client approval of Phase 1 deliverables and receipt of architectural program.

---

### Phase 3 -- Construction Documents

**Fee: $45,000 (Lump Sum)**

Elder Engineering will prepare construction-ready civil engineering drawings and specifications for the project. Documents will be prepared in accordance with the City of Elizabethtown standards and suitable for competitive bidding.

**Deliverables:**

1. **Civil Construction Drawing Set** (estimated 22--28 sheets) including:
   - Cover Sheet and General Notes
   - Existing Conditions / Demolition Plan
   - Geometric / Layout Plan
   - Grading and Drainage Plan
   - Erosion Prevention and Sediment Control Plan (EPSC)
   - Utility Plan (water, sanitary sewer, storm sewer)
   - Utility Profiles
   - Detail Sheets (pavement sections, curb details, inlet details, manhole details, pipe bedding, ADA ramp details, etc.)
   - Stormwater Management Plan (detention basin grading, outlet structure details)

2. **Stormwater Management Report** -- Full hydrologic and hydraulic analysis including:
   - Pre- and post-development runoff calculations (SCS TR-55 / HydroCAD)
   - Detention basin routing
   - Water quality volume calculations per Elizabethtown Stormwater Ordinance
   - Storm sewer sizing calculations
   - Downstream analysis (to the point of adequate receiving channel)

3. **Project Specifications** -- Technical specifications for earthwork, paving, storm drainage, sanitary sewer, water distribution, and erosion control, referencing KYTC Standard Specifications and City of Elizabethtown standards as applicable.

4. **Engineer's Opinion of Probable Construction Cost** -- Itemized cost estimate based on the construction documents.

5. **Plan Revisions** -- One round of revisions based on consolidated Client, tenant, and plan-review comments. Additional revision rounds will be invoiced as Additional Services.

**Schedule:** 10 weeks from Client approval of Preliminary Site Plan and receipt of final architectural plans.

---

### Phase 4 -- Permitting

**Fee: $12,000 (Lump Sum)**

Elder Engineering will prepare and submit permit applications on behalf of the Client for the following approvals:

1. **City of Elizabethtown Development Plan Review** -- Submit civil drawings for Development Plan review per the Elizabethtown Zoning Ordinance. Attend one Planning Commission meeting if required.

2. **KPDES KYR10 Stormwater Construction Permit** -- Prepare and submit the Notice of Intent (NOI) and Stormwater Pollution Prevention Plan (SWPPP) to the Kentucky Division of Water for land-disturbing activities exceeding one acre.

3. **KYTC Encroachment Permit** -- Prepare and submit the encroachment permit application for the proposed access point on US 31W (West Dixie Avenue) to the KYTC District 4 office in Elizabethtown. Coordinate with Client's traffic engineer regarding turn-lane design.

4. **Elizabethtown Water & Sewer Plan Approval** -- Submit water and sewer construction plans to Elizabethtown Water (water) and Elizabethtown Sewer Department (sewer) for review and approval.

5. **Floodplain Development Permit** -- If any portion of the development impacts the FEMA-mapped floodplain, prepare and submit a Floodplain Development Permit application to the Hardin County Floodplain Administrator.

6. **Plan Review Response** -- Respond to one round of review comments from each reviewing agency. Additional review cycles will be invoiced as Additional Services.

**Schedule:** Concurrent with Phase 3, plus 8--12 weeks for agency review (durations controlled by reviewing agencies).

---

### Phase 5 -- Construction Administration

**Fee: $18,000 (Lump Sum)**

Elder Engineering will provide construction-phase services during site construction to verify the work is being performed in general conformance with the construction documents.

**Services Include:**

1. **Pre-Construction Conference** -- Attend and participate in one pre-construction meeting with the Client, general contractor, and relevant subcontractors.

2. **Site Observations** -- Conduct periodic site observations during key construction activities. The following visits are anticipated:
   - Erosion control installation (1 visit)
   - Rough grading and subgrade preparation (3 visits)
   - Underground utility installation -- storm sewer (2 visits)
   - Underground utility installation -- sanitary sewer (2 visits)
   - Underground utility installation -- water main (2 visits)
   - Curb and gutter forming (1 visit)
   - Stone base placement (1 visit)
   - Detention basin grading and outlet structure (2 visits)
   - Final grading and punch list (1 visit)
   - Total: approximately 15 site visits

3. **Shop Drawing Review** -- Review contractor submittals for pipe, structures, geotextiles, and other specified materials (up to 20 submittals).

4. **Observation Reports** -- Prepare and distribute a written field observation report following each site visit.

5. **Record Drawings** -- Prepare record ("as-built") drawings based on the contractor's redline markups. Record drawings will note field changes; an independent as-built survey is not included.

6. **Project Closeout** -- Prepare a final punch list and issue a letter of substantial completion when warranted.

**Schedule:** Duration of site construction (estimated 6--8 months).

## Assumptions

This proposal is based on the following assumptions. If any assumption proves incorrect, Elder Engineering reserves the right to adjust the scope and fee accordingly with written notice to the Client.

1. A boundary and topographic survey of the property, including location of existing utilities, will be provided by the Client's surveyor at no cost to Elder Engineering. The survey shall be provided in AutoCAD (.dwg) format on the Kentucky Single Zone State Plane Coordinate System (NAD83) with elevations referenced to NAVD88.

2. A geotechnical investigation report, including pavement design recommendations and earthwork recommendations, will be provided by the Client at no cost to Elder Engineering.

3. Architectural floor plans, building elevations, and a tenant program will be provided by the Client's architect in sufficient detail and in a timely manner to support the design schedule.

4. A Traffic Impact Study (TIS), including access and turn-lane recommendations, will be performed by the Client's traffic engineer. Elder Engineering will coordinate with the traffic engineer but is not responsible for traffic engineering services.

5. The site is not expected to contain regulated wetlands, threatened or endangered species habitat, or hazardous materials. If environmental constraints are identified, scope and fee adjustments may be necessary.

6. The FEMA floodplain does not significantly impact the developable area of the site. If a CLOMR or LOMR is required, it will be treated as an Additional Service.

7. No more than one round of consolidated review comments is anticipated from each reviewing agency during Phase 4. Additional review cycles will be treated as Additional Services.

8. The scope does not include landscape architecture design beyond code-minimum buffer plantings shown on the site plan. A landscape architect retained by the Client will prepare detailed landscape plans.
