## Executive Summary

This report presents the stormwater management strategy for the Bardstown Commons Phase 1 commercial pad site. The proposed system uses a dry detention basin and grass swale BMPs to meet Nelson County and Kentucky Division of Water post-construction stormwater requirements.

## Assumptions

- Rainfall data from NOAA Atlas 14, Nelson County, KY
- SCS Type II rainfall distribution, TR-55 method
- Soil data from USDA Web Soil Survey
- Pre-development assumes no existing impervious surfaces

## Risk Flags

- HSG C soils limit infiltration-based BMPs
- Nelson County karst geology — geotech investigation needed before basin construction
- Downstream Town Creek tributary capacity should be verified
- Long-term maintenance depends on property owner compliance
