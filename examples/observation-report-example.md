## Work Observed

### 1. Rough Grading -- Lots 14 through 22 (Phase 2A)

Rough grading operations continued on the western portion of the site encompassing Lots 14 through 22 along the proposed Cedar Ridge Drive alignment. The contractor was cutting from the high point near Lot 16 (approximate existing elevation 774.2 ft) and filling toward the detention basin outfall area near Lot 22 (design subgrade elevation 758.5 ft).

Fill lifts observed were approximately 8 inches loose prior to compaction, consistent with the geotechnical report requirement of 8-inch maximum loose lift thickness (see Terracon Report No. 2025-1892, dated November 14, 2025). Material being placed appeared to be on-site clay fill, tan-brown in color, visually consistent with the CL classification identified in the geotechnical borings.

The Bomag roller was making a minimum of four passes per lift in the fill areas observed. No independent density testing was performed during today's observation. The contractor's QC technician from River City Testing was not on site. **The contractor was reminded that nuclear density testing is required at a minimum frequency of one test per 2,500 square feet per lift in accordance with the project specifications (Section 31 2000, Paragraph 3.04.B).**

### 2. Sanitary Sewer Installation -- Cedar Ridge Drive (Sta. 12+50 to Sta. 15+80)

The pipe crew installed approximately 330 linear feet of 8-inch SDR-26 PVC sanitary sewer along Cedar Ridge Drive from the manhole at Sta. 12+50 (MH-7) to Sta. 15+80. Pipe was bedded in KYTC No. 8 crushed limestone aggregate per the approved shop drawings and the Elizabethtown Water & Sewer specification.

- **Pipe:** 8-inch SDR-26 PVC, manufacturer JM Eagle, per ASTM D3034
- **Bedding Material:** KYTC No. 8 limestone, sourced from Nolin Quarry (Hanson Aggregates)
- **Trench Width:** Approximately 36 inches at pipe zone, consistent with specification minimum of pipe OD + 12 inches each side
- **Bedding Depth:** 6 inches below pipe invert observed at two random check locations (Sta. 13+00 and Sta. 14+75)
- **Pipe Grade:** Contractor's laser was set at 0.50% slope; two spot checks with hand level confirmed pipe invert elevations within 0.02 ft of design profile

Joints were assembled using factory-applied gaskets. No visible joint deflection or misalignment was noted at the sections observed.

### 3. Erosion and Sediment Control

The perimeter silt fence along the southern property line (adjacent to the existing Heartland Elementary School property) was inspected. The construction entrance on Ring Road was inspected for mud tracking. The sediment basin at the southwest corner of the site (Basin SB-1) was inspected for accumulated sediment depth.

## Issues and Deficiencies

### Issue 1: Inadequate Trench Backfill Compaction (Sta. 13+25 to Sta. 13+75)

**Severity:** Requires Corrective Action

At approximately Sta. 13+25 through Sta. 13+75 (50 LF section), the pipe zone backfill above the aggregate bedding was being placed in a single lift of approximately 30 inches without intermediate compaction. The project specifications (Section 33 3100, Paragraph 3.05.C) require backfill above the pipe zone to be placed in maximum 8-inch loose lifts with each lift compacted to 95% Standard Proctor density before the next lift is placed.

The foreman (Skaggs) stated his crew was planning to compact the full depth at once with the excavator bucket. This method was rejected. The contractor was directed to remove the non-compliant backfill in this 50-LF section and re-place it in conforming lifts with mechanical compaction and density testing. Refer to Contractor Directive CD-009 below.

### Issue 2: Erosion Control Deficiency -- Silt Fence Failure at South Property Line

**Severity:** Requires Immediate Repair

A section of silt fence approximately 80 feet long near the southeast corner of the site (between erosion control stakes EC-42 and EC-46) was found undermined and partially collapsed. Accumulated sediment on the upstream side had overtopped the fabric in at least two locations, and turbid runoff had deposited a sediment fan approximately 15 feet onto the adjacent Heartland Elementary School property.

This is a violation of the project's KPDES KYR10 permit (Permit No. KYR100982) and the approved Erosion Prevention and Sediment Control (EPSC) Plan. The contractor was directed to repair the silt fence, install a secondary row of silt fence, and remove deposited sediment from the school property by end of business July 23, 2026. Refer to Contractor Directive CD-010 below.

### Issue 3: Missing Density Test Documentation

**Severity:** Administrative

The contractor has not submitted density test reports for fill lifts placed on July 17 and July 18, 2026 (Lots 17--19 rough grading). QC testing was reportedly performed by River City Testing on those dates but results have not been provided to Elder Engineering. Test reports are required within 48 hours of testing per the contract General Conditions. The contractor was reminded of this requirement verbally and in writing (see Directive CD-011).

## Contractor Directives Issued

### CD-009: Trench Backfill Correction (Sta. 13+25 to Sta. 13+75)

**Issued To:** Travis Benton, Superintendent, Heartland Grading & Utilities, Inc.
**Date:** July 22, 2026

Remove all pipe zone backfill placed in non-conforming lifts between Sta. 13+25 and Sta. 13+75. Re-place backfill in maximum 8-inch loose lifts with mechanical compaction (vibratory plate compactor or trench roller). Each lift shall be tested to verify 95% Standard Proctor density prior to placement of the next lift. Compaction test results shall be submitted to Elder Engineering within 48 hours of testing.

**Required Completion:** Prior to any paving operations on Cedar Ridge Drive.

### CD-010: Erosion Control Repair -- South Perimeter Silt Fence

**Issued To:** Travis Benton, Superintendent, Heartland Grading & Utilities, Inc.
**Date:** July 22, 2026

1. Repair or replace the damaged silt fence between stakes EC-42 and EC-46 (approximately 80 LF).
2. Install a secondary (redundant) row of silt fence 6 feet upslope of the repaired primary fence for the full length of the southern property boundary.
3. Remove all deposited sediment from the Heartland Elementary School property and restore the affected area to pre-disturbance condition.
4. Photograph completed repairs and submit documentation to Elder Engineering.

**Required Completion:** End of business July 23, 2026.

### CD-011: Submit Outstanding Density Test Reports

**Issued To:** Travis Benton, Superintendent, Heartland Grading & Utilities, Inc.
**Date:** July 22, 2026

Submit all outstanding nuclear density test reports from River City Testing for fill placement on July 17 and July 18, 2026 (Lots 17--19). Reports are to include test location, lift number, elevation, wet density, dry density, moisture content, and percent compaction relative to Standard Proctor maximum.

**Required Completion:** July 24, 2026.
