### 3.2 Height Non-Compliance

The proposed building height of 48 ft exceeds the 45-ft maximum by 3 ft. This overage is attributable to the parapet wall extending above the fourth-floor roof line. Two options are available:

1. **Parapet Exception**: Section 4.3.2(d) of the Zoning Ordinance permits parapets, chimneys, mechanical equipment screens, and similar architectural features to exceed the height limit by up to 6 ft, provided they do not contain habitable space. If the 3-ft parapet qualifies under this exception, no variance is required.
2. **Variance**: If the Building Inspector determines the parapet exception does not apply, a height variance of 3 ft would be required from the Board of Adjustment.

**Recommendation**: Submit architectural drawings to the Building Inspector for a determination under Section 4.3.2(d) prior to site plan submittal. The parapet appears to qualify for the exception.

## 10. Conclusion

The proposed Gateway Inn hotel is a permitted use in the B-2 Highway Commercial District and complies with the substantial majority of applicable zoning standards. The single dimensional non-compliance (building height) is attributable to a parapet wall and is expected to be resolved through the architectural feature exception in Section 4.3.2(d) without a variance. Landscaping, parking, signage, and site design standards are met. Stormwater management design is pending and will be submitted as part of the site plan application package.

The project is ready to proceed to site plan preparation and pre-application conference with the City of Elizabethtown.
