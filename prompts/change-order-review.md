# Construction Change Order Review and Analysis

## Purpose

Provide structured review and analysis of construction change orders for civil engineering and landscape architecture projects in Kentucky. Evaluates technical justification, cost reasonableness, schedule impact, and contractual compliance to support the owner's approval or negotiation process.

## When to Use

- Contractor submits a proposed change order or construction change directive
- Unforeseen site conditions require scope modification (differing site conditions per KYTC 104.02)
- Owner-directed design revisions during construction
- Value engineering proposals from the contractor
- Resolution of RFI responses that result in added or deleted scope
- Claims analysis for disputed work on KYTC or locally funded projects

## Required Inputs

- Original contract documents: plans, specifications, and agreement (including unit prices and allowances)
- Contractor's change order proposal with itemized cost breakdown and supporting documentation
- Applicable KYTC Standard Specifications for Road and Bridge Construction (current edition)
- KYTC Guidance Manual for Change Orders (for state-funded projects)
- Project schedule (baseline and current update) in CPM format
- Daily construction reports and photographs covering the period of changed work
- Relevant RFIs, ASIs, field directives, and meeting minutes
- Prevailing wage rates per KRS 337.505-550 (applicable to public projects over $250,000)
- RS Means, KYTC weighted average unit prices, or other cost reference data

## Procedure

1. **Entitlement Analysis**: Determine whether the contractor has a contractual right to additional compensation or time. Review the contract's changes clause (typically FAR 52.243 for federal, KYTC 104.02 for state, or AIA A201 Article 7 for private). Classify the change: owner-directed, differing site condition (Type I or Type II), constructive change, or design error/omission. Verify that the contractor provided timely written notice per the contract requirements.

2. **Scope Verification**: Compare the proposed changed work against the original contract scope. Identify whether the work is truly additional, was arguably included in the original scope, or represents a deletion of contract work. Review specifications for performance requirements that may already encompass the proposed work. For KYTC projects, verify alignment with the original project letting documents.

3. **Cost Analysis -- Labor**: Verify labor classifications against KY prevailing wage determinations (KRS 337.505-550) for public projects. Check crew compositions and productivity rates against industry standards. Confirm labor burden rates (FICA, SUTA/FUTA, workers comp per Kentucky rates, health and welfare) are within reasonable ranges -- typically 35-45% of base wage for Kentucky construction trades. Reject duplicated costs between labor burden and general conditions markup.

4. **Cost Analysis -- Materials**: Compare unit material costs against recent supplier quotes, RS Means data, or KYTC weighted average bid prices (published quarterly). Verify quantities by independent takeoff from revised drawings. Check for sales tax applicability -- Kentucky has 6% sales tax on materials; contractors on public projects may not be exempt. Confirm material specifications match the contract requirements or approved substitutions.

5. **Cost Analysis -- Equipment**: Verify equipment rates against the contractor's submitted rate schedule, FHWA Equipment Watch rental rates, or KYTC equipment rental rate schedule. Distinguish between owned and rented equipment. Check for standby time versus operating time rates. Confirm equipment is appropriate for the work (not oversized for the task).

6. **Markup Evaluation**: Apply contract-specified markups for overhead and profit. In the absence of contract terms, apply KYTC standard markups: 10% overhead and 10% profit on self-performed work; 5% markup on subcontracted work. For projects using AIA documents, reference A201 Section 7.3.3 for cost-of-work definitions. Verify that bond premium increases are documented with surety confirmation.

7. **Schedule Impact Assessment**: Evaluate whether the changed work affects the critical path. Require a time impact analysis (TIA) using a CPM fragmentary network inserted into the current schedule. Determine if concurrent delays reduce the contractor's entitlement to time extension. Assess liquidated damages exposure if time extension is not granted.

8. **Documentation and Recommendation**: Prepare a written analysis memorandum recommending approval, approval with modifications, or rejection. Include an independent cost estimate for comparison. For KYTC projects, complete the required change order forms (TC 63-23 or current version). For public projects, verify that cumulative change orders do not exceed thresholds requiring additional legislative or governing body approval per KRS 45A.

## Output Format

- Change order review memorandum (2-4 pages) with sections: Background, Entitlement Analysis, Cost Analysis, Schedule Impact, and Recommendation
- Side-by-side cost comparison table: Contractor's Proposal vs. Engineer's Estimate with variance and notes
- Revised contract summary showing original amount, prior approved changes, this change, and new contract total
- Schedule impact summary with critical path analysis findings

## Quality Checks

- Entitlement basis is clearly identified and tied to a specific contract clause
- Independent cost estimate varies no more than 10-15% from recommended amount (larger variances require explanation)
- Labor rates comply with applicable KY prevailing wage determinations for the county of work
- Markups do not exceed contract-specified percentages or KYTC maximums
- Cumulative change order total is tracked against any governing body approval thresholds (KRS 45A.180 for state agencies)
- Time extension request is supported by CPM schedule analysis, not simply a duration assertion
- All backup documentation (quotes, invoices, timesheets, equipment logs) has been reviewed for consistency

## Template Reference

- KYTC TC 63-23 Change Order Form
- KYTC Guidance Manual, Chapter 9: Contract Changes
- AIA G701 Change Order (with supporting G702/G703 continuation sheets)
- EJCDC C-941 Change Order
- KRS 45A.180 (modification limits for public contracts)
- KRS 337.505-550 (Kentucky prevailing wage law)
- ASCE 48-23 (Guide for Design Professionals Engaged as Experts in Construction Disputes)
