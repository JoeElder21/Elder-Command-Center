# Specification Review

## Purpose

Systematically review construction specifications for civil engineering and landscape architecture projects in Kentucky to verify completeness, internal consistency, regulatory compliance, and coordination with the construction drawings. Catch conflicts, omissions, and outdated references before bid or permit submittal.

## When to Use

- Before issuing a specification package for bid on a public or private project
- Prior to submitting to KYTC for plan review on state-funded transportation projects
- When reviewing specifications prepared by subconsultants for incorporation into a prime deliverable
- During value engineering reviews to verify specification requirements align with cost assumptions
- When updating a master specification library to reflect current Kentucky codes and standards

## Required Inputs

1. **Full project specification book** (all divisions, front-end through technical sections)
2. **Construction drawings** (plan set, including general notes, details, and schedules)
3. **Geotechnical report** (soil borings, recommendations for earthwork, foundations, pavement)
4. **Project-specific regulatory requirements** (KDOW permits, local agency conditions of approval)
5. **Contract requirements** (owner-furnished general conditions, supplemental conditions, bid form)
6. **Applicable standards**: KYTC Standard Specifications for Road and Bridge Construction (current edition), Kentucky Building Code (KBC per 815 KAR 7:120), Louisville/Jefferson County or LFUCG local amendments

## Procedure

1. **Verify Division 00 and 01 coordination.** Confirm that the bidding requirements, contract forms, and general conditions are consistent with the owner's standard documents. For KYTC projects, verify that the Supplemental Specifications and Special Provisions align with the current KYTC Standard Specifications edition. For Louisville MSD projects, confirm MSD General Conditions are incorporated.
2. **Check specification section list against drawings.** Cross-reference every material, product, and system shown on the drawings to confirm a corresponding specification section exists. Flag any drawn detail that references a spec section not included in the book.
3. **Review Division 31 (Earthwork).** Verify compaction requirements match the geotechnical report recommendations. Confirm rock excavation definitions align with Kentucky practice (refusal at 100 blows per foot or 15-minute auger refusal). Verify fill material requirements reference the KYTC classification system (AASHTO soil groups) where applicable.
4. **Review Division 32 (Exterior Improvements).** For pavement sections, confirm layer thicknesses and material grades match the pavement design report and KYTC standard drawings (e.g., RPM series). Verify plant material specifications include ANSI Z60.1 sizing, B&B or container-grown requirements, and are appropriate for USDA Hardiness Zone 6a/6b (most of Kentucky). Confirm that the seed mix references KYTC Seed Mixtures per Standard Specification Section 212 or the local jurisdiction's requirements.
5. **Review Division 33 (Utilities).** Verify pipe materials, joint types, and testing requirements match the serving utility's standards (e.g., Louisville Water Company, Kentucky American Water, Louisville MSD, local sanitary sewer district). Confirm fire hydrant and valve specifications match the local water utility's approved products list. Verify sanitary sewer materials meet KDOW 401 KAR 8:100 requirements.
6. **Review stormwater and erosion control sections.** Confirm BMP specifications align with the Kentucky Erosion Prevention and Sediment Control Field Guide (current edition). Verify that detention/retention structure specifications match the approved stormwater management plan. For manufactured treatment devices, confirm they are on the KDOW-approved list or have appropriate third-party certification (e.g., NJDEP verification).
7. **Check for outdated references.** Verify all ASTM, AASHTO, ACI, and AWWA standards are cited by current designation. Confirm referenced KYTC standard drawings match the current series. Verify Kentucky Building Code edition matches the currently adopted version under 815 KAR 7:120.
8. **Verify measurement and payment provisions.** For unit-price contracts, confirm that every bid item has a corresponding measurement and payment clause. Verify pay item descriptions match KYTC standard pay item codes on state projects. Confirm incidental items are explicitly listed.
9. **Confirm quality assurance and testing requirements.** Verify that specified testing frequencies meet KYTC minimums (e.g., one proctor per 1,000 CY of fill, density tests per KYTC Standard Specification 204.03.04). Confirm laboratory qualifications require KYTC-certified labs where applicable.

## Output Format

- Specification review comment log in tabular format with columns: Spec Section, Page, Comment Type (conflict/omission/outdated reference/coordination issue), Description, Recommended Resolution, Reviewer Initials
- Summary memorandum identifying critical issues vs. minor editorial corrections
- Marked-up specification sections (PDF with cloud markups) for sections with substantive issues
- Separate list of outdated standard references with current replacements

## Quality Checks

- [ ] Every drawn material has a corresponding specification section
- [ ] All KYTC Standard Specification references cite the current edition year
- [ ] Geotechnical report recommendations are reflected accurately in earthwork and foundation specs
- [ ] Plant material specs are appropriate for Kentucky hardiness zones and local ordinance requirements
- [ ] Pipe material and testing specs match each serving utility's current standards
- [ ] Measurement and payment clauses exist for every bid item in the proposal form
- [ ] Erosion control specifications reference the current Kentucky EP&SC Field Guide
- [ ] No conflicting requirements exist between specification sections (e.g., concrete strength specified differently in Division 03 vs. Division 32)
- [ ] Substitution and submittal procedures are clearly defined and not contradicted between sections

## Template Reference

- KYTC Standard Specifications for Road and Bridge Construction (current edition)
- KYTC Supplemental Specifications and Recurring Special Provisions (updated annually)
- CSI MasterFormat 2020 for section numbering and organization
- Kentucky Building Code as adopted per 815 KAR 7:120
- Louisville MSD Design Manual and Standard Specifications
- LFUCG Standard Specifications for Road and Storm Sewer Construction
- KYTC Standard Drawings (current RPM, RDM, RDR, and other series)
