# Construction Observation Report

## Purpose
Document field observations during active construction to create a defensible record of work performed, materials used, and compliance with the approved plans and specifications. These reports protect the engineer of record, inform the client, and satisfy agency inspection requirements.

## When to Use
- During any site visit to an active construction project where the firm is the engineer of record
- When a contractor requests a PE field verification for utility or subgrade work before backfill
- At milestone inspections required by Louisville MSD, LFUCG, KDOW, or other permitting agencies
- When documenting work that deviates from approved plans

## Required Inputs
1. **Approved construction plans** (current revision with all approved change orders)
2. **Project specifications** (technical specifications and special provisions)
3. **Previous observation reports** for continuity
4. **Daily weather conditions** (temperature, precipitation, wind)
5. **Contractor's daily log or superintendent's report** (if available)
6. **Testing reports** (compaction, concrete, pipe pressure tests) received since last visit

## Procedure
1. **Pre-Visit Preparation** -- Review the most recent observation report, any open deficiency items, and the contractor's schedule to understand what work should be in progress. Bring the current plan set, specifications, a measuring device, level rod, and camera.
2. **Site Conditions** -- Record date, time of arrival and departure, weather conditions, and general site conditions. Note erosion control BMP status: are silt fences intact, is the construction entrance functional, are sediment basins dewatered, and is temporary seeding established on inactive areas per the SWPPP schedule.
3. **Work in Progress** -- Document each activity observed: earthwork (approximate area, lift thickness, compaction equipment in use), utility installation (pipe material, size, bedding type, trench width, depth of cover), concrete placement (mix design ticket review, slump, air content, cylinder specimens taken), paving (material temperature, lift thickness, rolling pattern), and landscaping (species verification, root ball condition, planting depth).
4. **Dimensional Verification** -- Spot-check critical dimensions against the approved plans: pipe inverts (within 0.10 feet of plan), manhole rim elevations, curb and gutter flow line grades, building pad elevations, and pavement cross-slopes. Document measurements taken and their locations.
5. **Materials Verification** -- Verify materials match specifications: pipe material and class (SDR, wall thickness), aggregate gradation certifications, concrete mix design submittals, geotextile specification compliance, and plant material species and caliper. Note approved submittal numbers for materials observed.
6. **Deficiency Documentation** -- Clearly describe any work that does not conform to the plans or specifications. Include the specific plan sheet and specification section violated, a photograph with reference, and the corrective action required. Categorize as: observation (informational), deficiency (requires correction), or stop-work (safety or critical non-conformance).
7. **Testing Status** -- Record any tests witnessed or results received. For compaction, note the test location, required density, and result. For pipe pressure tests, note the section tested, test pressure, duration, and pass/fail status. For concrete, note the placement location and cylinder break schedule.

## Output Format
- Report header: project name, number, date, weather, personnel on site
- Numbered observation items with plan sheet references
- Deficiency log with status tracking (new, open, resolved)
- Photo log with numbered captions tied to observation items
- Signature block for the observing PE/EIT with license number
- Distribution list (client, contractor, agency if required)

## Quality Checks
- Report issued within 48 hours of the site visit
- All photographs captioned with location, direction, and observation item reference
- Deficiency items carry forward from previous reports until documented as resolved
- Dimensional measurements recorded with instrument used and precision stated
- No language implying the engineer supervised, directed, or guaranteed the contractor's work (maintain observation vs. supervision distinction per KRS 322)
- Report clearly states the observation was limited to the items described and does not constitute full-time inspection or a warranty of the contractor's work

## Template Reference
- EJCDC C-620 (Engineer's Letter Regarding Field Observation)
- KPDES KYR10 SWPPP inspection checklist (erosion control observations)
- Louisville MSD Construction Inspection Manual
- KYTC Standard Specifications for Road and Bridge Construction (material testing requirements)
- KRS 322.360 (Professional Engineers practice standards)
