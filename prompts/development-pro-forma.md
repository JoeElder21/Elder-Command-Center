# Development Pro Forma Financial Analysis

## Purpose
Build a detailed development pro forma modeling land acquisition, entitlement, design, construction, and disposition costs against projected revenues for a Kentucky real estate development. Enables a PE/PLA to advise on project feasibility, support infrastructure cost allocation, and prepare public incentive applications.

## When to Use
- Evaluating financial viability of a proposed subdivision, commercial site, or mixed-use development.
- Preparing cost estimates for a zone change application or development plan.
- Supporting a Tax Increment Financing (TIF) application under KRS 65.490-65.499.
- Analyzing infrastructure cost-sharing for a development agreement with a municipality.

## Required Inputs
1. **Site data**: Acreage, PVA assessed value, proposed purchase price, topographic and geotechnical conditions.
2. **Program**: Unit count and type (lots, buildings, SF), phasing plan, and absorption timeline.
3. **Jurisdiction**: Applicable impact fees, tap fees, and permit costs (Louisville Metro, LFUCG, or county).
4. **Market data**: Comparable sale prices or lease rates from local MLS or CoStar.
5. **Financing**: Loan-to-value ratio, construction loan rate, permanent debt terms, equity requirement.
6. **Infrastructure scope**: Roads, utilities, stormwater, grading, landscaping, amenities.

## Procedure
**Step 1 -- Land Basis**: Record contract price or PVA value (KY PVA targets 100% FMV per KRS 132.191). Add closing costs: deed transfer tax at $0.50 per $500 of consideration (KRS 142.050), title insurance, attorney fees, survey. Include due diligence costs (Phase I ESA, geotech, boundary survey). Model multi-parcel assemblages separately.

**Step 2 -- Soft Costs**: Zone change fees per local fee schedule (LFUCG or LMCO Ch. 1). Engineering/design fees at 8-12% of hard costs for civil, landscape architecture, architecture, and surveying. Legal fees for zoning, deeds, HOA documents. Environmental permits: KDOW floodplain permit, USACE Section 404, KPDES stormwater NOI. KYTC encroachment permit and traffic study. Development plan/plat review fees. Builder's risk insurance.

**Step 3 -- Hard Costs**: Line-item estimates for: mass grading (include rock excavation for central KY limestone/karst); roads to local standards (Louisville Metro 26-ft pavement, LFUCG per Land Subdivision Regs); sanitary sewer (gravity mains, manholes, laterals, Louisville MSD or LFUCG capacity assurance tap fees by meter size); water distribution (mains, hydrants at 500-ft spacing, service taps); stormwater BMPs (detention, bioretention, permeable pavers per Louisville MSD Design Manual or LFUCG standards); electric/gas/telecom underground extension costs; landscaping (LFUCG requires one street tree per 40 LF frontage in many zones); erosion control per KPDES KYR10 general permit.

**Step 4 -- Revenue**: Lot sales priced from comparables; model absorption at 2-6 lots/month typical for KY residential subdivisions. Vertical construction sale price per SF less build cost if developer is building. Commercial NOI from projected lease rates and stabilization timeline. Apply 2-3% annual escalation for phased projects, verified against local market trends.

**Step 5 -- Financing and Carry**: Model acquisition loan draw schedule. Construction loan interest on average outstanding balance monthly. Origination fees at 0.5-1.0%. Property taxes during development from PVA assessment; note reassessment upon platting can increase basis significantly (KRS 132.450). HOA/maintenance costs during sell-out if developer-responsible.

**Step 6 -- Incentives**: Model TIF districts (KRS 65.490), CDBG funds, New Markets Tax Credits. Cost-sharing offsets: oversized utility reimbursements, KYTC cost participation for turn lanes/signals, parkland dedication credits per LFUCG Subdivision Regs.

**Step 7 -- Summary and Sensitivity**: Calculate total cost, total revenue, gross margin, net profit, ROI, and IRR. Run scenarios: 10% cost overrun, 20% slower absorption, 5% price reduction, 150 bps interest rate increase. Identify break-even absorption point.

## Output Format
1. **Sources and Uses table**: Equity, debt, and other sources vs. land, hard costs, soft costs, contingency.
2. **Development budget**: Line-item detail with unit costs, quantities, and totals.
3. **Revenue schedule**: Monthly/quarterly projections by phase.
4. **Cash flow waterfall**: Period-by-period inflows/outflows showing cumulative position.
5. **Return metrics**: Profit, margin, ROI, IRR with sensitivity matrix.
6. **Risk summary**: Top three financial risks with mitigation strategies.

## Quality Checks
- Unit prices must reflect Kentucky market rates for the current year, not national averages.
- Jurisdiction fees must come from the current published fee schedule with effective date noted.
- Revenue assumptions must cite comparable sales with MLS numbers or CoStar references.
- Contingency minimum: 10% of hard costs, 5% of soft costs.
- Sensitivity analysis must include at least three adverse scenarios and break-even thresholds.

## Template Reference
- Louisville MSD fee schedule and Design Manual; LFUCG Land Subdivision Regulations.
- KRS 142.050 (deed transfer tax), KRS 65.490-65.499 (TIF), KRS 132.191 (property valuation).
- RSMeans Site Work and Landscape Cost Data (annual edition) for unit price cross-checks.
