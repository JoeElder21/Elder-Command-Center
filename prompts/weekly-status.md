# Weekly Status Report Generation

## Purpose
Produce a consistent, concise weekly status report that keeps project stakeholders informed of progress, upcoming milestones, issues requiring attention, and budget health for civil engineering and landscape architecture projects. The report serves as both a communication tool and a contemporaneous project record that supports claims documentation, schedule analysis, and client relationship management.

## When to Use
- Every active project on a weekly cycle, typically issued each Friday by close of business.
- Adjust frequency to biweekly or monthly for projects in holding patterns (e.g., awaiting KDOW permit review, waiting on client-furnished data), but document the reason for reduced frequency.
- During construction administration, align reporting with the contractor's pay application cycle and weekly progress meeting schedule.
- When a client's contract or standard operating procedure specifies a particular reporting cadence, follow that requirement.

## Required Inputs
- Current project schedule with baseline and actual milestone dates.
- Timesheet data for the reporting period showing hours expended by task and staff member.
- Budget summary: contract amount, amount billed to date, amount earned to date, remaining budget by phase.
- Action item log from the most recent meeting minutes.
- Permit and regulatory submittal tracker with current status.
- Subconsultant progress updates received during the reporting period.
- Any client correspondence, RFIs, or change requests received or issued during the week.
- Field observation notes or site visit reports from the week.

## Procedure
1. **Progress Summary** -- Describe work completed during the reporting period in 3-7 bullet points. Use specific, measurable language: "Completed 60% design plans for the sanitary sewer extension along KY 32" rather than "Worked on sewer plans." Reference plan sheet numbers, calculation packages, or specification sections as applicable.
2. **Milestone Tracker** -- List the next 3-5 upcoming milestones with target dates and current status (on track, at risk, delayed). If a milestone is at risk or delayed, state the cause and the recovery plan. For KYTC projects, reference the specific phase completion milestones in the notice to proceed.
3. **Issues and Risks** -- Identify any issues encountered during the week that require client attention, agency follow-up, or internal resolution. Categorize as: design issue, permitting issue, client-side delay, subconsultant issue, or budget concern. For each issue, state the impact and the recommended action.
4. **Permit and Regulatory Status** -- Update the status of all active permit applications:
   - KDOW construction permits: date submitted, reviewer assigned (if known), anticipated review completion.
   - KYTC encroachment or access permits: submittal status, review comments received, resubmittal date.
   - Local planning and zoning: next hearing date, staff review comments, conditions of approval to address.
   - USACE Section 404: pre-application meeting status, jurisdictional determination, permit type.
5. **Budget Health** -- Report budget status by phase using three metrics: percent complete (based on deliverables), percent of budget consumed (based on hours or dollars), and earned value variance. Flag any phase where budget consumed exceeds percent complete by more than 10 percentage points. Note upcoming invoices and any outstanding receivables beyond Net 30 terms.
6. **Action Items Update** -- Carry forward all open action items from meeting minutes and prior status reports. Update status: completed, in progress, overdue. Highlight any items overdue by more than one week with the responsible party and escalation path.
7. **Upcoming Week Plan** -- List the planned activities for the following week in 3-5 bullet points. Include scheduled meetings, planned submittals, field work, and any client deliverables due. Note staff assignments for key tasks.
8. **Subconsultant Status** -- Summarize progress from each active subconsultant: survey completion percentage, geotechnical report delivery status, environmental assessment timeline, or structural design progress. Note any subconsultant schedule slippage and its impact on the critical path.
9. **Photos and Field Documentation** -- Include 1-3 photos from site visits or field work conducted during the week, with captions noting the date, location on site, and relevance to current design or construction activities.

## Output Format
- One-page status report (front side) with a supplemental detail page (back side) if needed. Structure:
  - Header: project name, number, reporting period, PM name, report number.
  - Progress Summary: bullet list.
  - Milestone Tracker: table with milestone, target date, status, and notes.
  - Issues/Risks: numbered list with severity indicator (red/yellow/green).
  - Budget Health: summary table by phase with percent complete and percent consumed.
  - Action Items: table with item, owner, due date, status.
  - Upcoming Week: bullet list.
- Distribute via email to the client PM and internal project team, with a PDF attachment for filing.

## Quality Checks
- Report is issued by close of business Friday; any delay beyond Monday of the following week is escalated to the principal-in-charge.
- Progress descriptions are specific and verifiable, not vague summaries.
- Budget percentages reconcile with the accounting system; no mental math or estimates when actual data is available.
- Every open action item from the prior report is accounted for -- nothing silently dropped.
- Milestone dates align with the current approved schedule, not the original baseline, unless a variance is being reported.
- Issues flagged as "red" have been communicated to the client verbally before appearing in the written report -- no surprises.
- Report tone is professional and factual; problems are stated with recommended solutions, not complaints.

## Template Reference
- Weekly Status Report template (Word or PDF) with firm letterhead and pre-formatted sections.
- Budget Health Dashboard template (spreadsheet) with formulas for earned value calculations.
- Action Item Tracker template (spreadsheet) sortable by project, owner, and due date.
- Milestone Tracker template with conditional formatting for on-track/at-risk/delayed status.
- Status Report Distribution List template tracking who receives each project's report.
