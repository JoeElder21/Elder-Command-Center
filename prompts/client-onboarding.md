# Client Onboarding Process and Documentation

## Purpose
Standardize the intake and onboarding of new clients for a Kentucky-based civil engineering and landscape architecture consulting firm. Ensure all legal, financial, and technical prerequisites are captured before project work begins, reducing downstream risk and establishing professional expectations aligned with Kentucky Board of Licensure requirements.

## When to Use
- A new client contacts the firm for the first time requesting services.
- An existing client initiates a project under a new contract or entity.
- A referral from ACEC-KY, KSPE, or KALA contacts leads to a prospective engagement.
- A public agency issues an RFQ/RFP and the firm is shortlisted for interview.

## Required Inputs
- Client legal name, entity type (LLC, municipality, utility district, etc.), and Kentucky Secretary of State registration status.
- Primary contact name, title, phone, email, and mailing address.
- Project description or scope summary as provided by the client.
- Anticipated project location (county, city, planning jurisdiction).
- Client budget range or funding source (KIA loan, CDBG, FEMA HMGP, local general fund, private equity).
- Any existing survey, geotechnical, or environmental data the client can furnish.

## Procedure
1. **Initial Contact Log** -- Record the date, source of referral, and nature of inquiry in the CRM. Tag the record with the relevant service line (site civil, stormwater, landscape design, transportation, water/wastewater).
2. **Conflict Check** -- Verify no existing engagement creates a conflict of interest per NSPE Code of Ethics and KRS 322.360 (PE practice act) or KRS 323A (landscape architecture practice act). Document the result.
3. **Entity Verification** -- Confirm the client entity is in good standing with the Kentucky Secretary of State. For public clients, confirm the contracting authority (fiscal court, city council, board of directors for sanitation or water districts per KRS Chapter 74 or 220).
4. **Preliminary Scope Meeting** -- Schedule a 30-60 minute call or in-person meeting to discuss project goals, site constraints, permitting pathway (Kentucky Division of Water, local planning and zoning, KYTC encroachment permits), and timeline expectations.
5. **Fee Proposal or SOQ Preparation** -- Prepare a proposal letter or Statement of Qualifications. For public projects subject to KRS 45A.730-750 (qualifications-based selection for engineering), follow the Brooks Act-style process without fee in the initial submittal.
6. **Master Services Agreement** -- Issue the firm's standard MSA covering scope, fees, payment terms (Net 30), limitation of liability, insurance certificates (professional liability, general liability, auto, workers' comp per Kentucky requirements), and termination provisions.
7. **Certificate of Insurance** -- Provide current COI listing the client as certificate holder. Confirm professional liability coverage meets the project's risk profile.
8. **Accounts Setup** -- Create the client and project records in the accounting and project management systems. Assign a project number, project manager, and billing code. Set up the project folder structure per the firm's file management standards.
9. **Kickoff Authorization** -- Obtain a signed agreement or notice to proceed before authorizing any billable work.
10. **Welcome Packet** -- Send the client a welcome packet containing key contacts, communication protocols, invoice schedule, and a summary of how submittals and deliverables will be managed.

## Output Format
- Completed client intake form (digital or PDF).
- Signed Master Services Agreement or project-specific contract.
- Certificate of Insurance on file.
- CRM record with contact details, conflict check notation, and project tag.
- Project number and folder structure established in firm systems.

## Quality Checks
- Conflict check is documented and signed off by a principal.
- MSA reviewed by firm counsel if non-standard terms are requested.
- COI confirms coverage dates span the anticipated project duration.
- KRS entity verification printout or screenshot is saved to the project file.
- All required inputs are populated before the first invoice is generated.

## Template Reference
- Master Services Agreement template (firm standard, reviewed annually by legal counsel).
- Client Intake Form template with fields mapped to the procedure above.
- COI request letter template for the firm's insurance broker.
- Welcome Packet template including firm overview, org chart, and communication matrix.
- Proposal letter template conforming to ACEC-KY recommended format.
