# Market Analysis for Development Opportunities

## Purpose
Evaluate the viability and strategic fit of geographic markets, project types, or client sectors for a Kentucky civil engineering and landscape architecture firm. Produce a data-driven assessment that supports go/no-go decisions on pursuing new market segments, expanding into adjacent Kentucky counties, or targeting specific funding programs.

## When to Use
- The firm is considering geographic expansion into a new Area Development District (ADD) region.
- A new state or federal funding program creates potential project demand (e.g., Bipartisan Infrastructure Law allocations through Kentucky Infrastructure Authority, FEMA BRIC grants, KY Transportation Cabinet Six-Year Highway Plan).
- Leadership wants to evaluate demand for a new service line such as green infrastructure design, EV charging site design, or broadband conduit engineering.
- Annual strategic planning sessions require updated market intelligence.

## Required Inputs
- Target geographic area defined by county, ADD region, or planning jurisdiction.
- Service line or project type under evaluation.
- Firm's current capability statement and staff certifications (PE, PLA, CPESC, CPSWQ, CFM, LEED AP).
- Historical project data from the firm's portfolio in the target area.
- Publicly available data sources: U.S. Census, Kentucky State Data Center, KYTC project lettings, KIA Intended Use Plan, PVA property records, local comprehensive plans.

## Procedure
1. **Define the Market Boundaries** -- Identify the geographic scope using ADD regions (e.g., Bluegrass ADD, KIPDA, Buffalo Trace ADD). Note which planning and zoning jurisdictions are active within the boundary and whether they enforce Kentucky Revised Statutes Chapter 100 (planning and zoning enabling legislation).
2. **Demographic and Growth Analysis** -- Pull population trends, housing permits, and commercial construction data from the Kentucky State Data Center and U.S. Census ACS 5-year estimates. Identify counties with above-average growth rates relative to the Kentucky statewide average.
3. **Infrastructure Needs Assessment** -- Review the Kentucky Infrastructure Authority Intended Use Plan for funded and waitlisted water, wastewater, and stormwater projects. Cross-reference with the Kentucky Division of Water Sanitary Survey reports and Consent Decree/Agreed Order lists for systems under enforcement action.
4. **Transportation Project Pipeline** -- Review the KYTC Six-Year Highway Plan, active MPO TIPs (Lexington Area MPO, KIPDA, OKI for Northern Kentucky), and local road aid fund project lists for upcoming design needs.
5. **Competitive Landscape** -- Identify firms currently serving the target market by reviewing ACEC-KY membership, KYTC prequalification lists, and recent public project awards on Kentucky Model Procurement postings.
6. **Funding Source Mapping** -- Catalog available funding: KIA SRF loans, CDBG (Department for Local Government), ARC grants (Appalachian counties), USDA RD (rural water/wastewater), FEMA HMGP and BRIC, local bond issues, and TIF district revenues.
7. **Client Relationship Inventory** -- Map existing firm relationships to the target area: current clients, past clients, board members, elected officials, and referral partners. Score relationship strength as strong, moderate, or nascent.
8. **SWOT Synthesis** -- Compile strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats specific to the firm entering or growing in this market. Reference specific competitors, project backlog, and staffing constraints.
9. **Go/No-Go Recommendation** -- Provide a clear recommendation with supporting rationale, estimated pursuit cost, timeline to first revenue, and key milestones (e.g., attend Bluegrass ADD quarterly meeting, present at KRWA conference).

## Output Format
- Market Analysis Report (5-10 pages) with executive summary, data tables, maps, and SWOT matrix.
- Prioritized list of target clients and projects with estimated fee ranges.
- Funding source matrix matching project types to available programs.
- Competitive positioning summary with firm-vs-competitor comparison on relevant selection criteria.
- Action plan with 90-day, 6-month, and 12-month milestones.

## Quality Checks
- All demographic data cites a specific source and vintage year; no data older than 3 years unless trend analysis requires it.
- Competitor information verified against at least two sources (prequalification list and project award record).
- Funding programs confirmed as currently active with open or upcoming application cycles.
- SWOT entries are specific and evidence-based, not generic platitudes.
- Recommendation includes a quantified estimate of pursuit investment vs. expected return.

## Template Reference
- Market Analysis Report template with standard section headings and formatting.
- SWOT Matrix template (2x2 grid with color coding).
- Funding Source Matrix template with columns for program name, administering agency, eligible project types, match requirements, and application deadlines.
- Competitive Positioning Scorecard template comparing firms on criteria such as local experience, staff depth, specialty certifications, and fee competitiveness.
