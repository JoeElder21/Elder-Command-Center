# Public Hearing Presentation Preparation

## Purpose

Prepare materials and talking points for public hearings, planning commission meetings, board of adjustments hearings, and zoning/land use proceedings before Kentucky municipal and county bodies. Presentations must convey technical information to non-technical audiences, anticipate public concerns, and provide the factual record needed for a defensible decision under KRS Chapter 100.

## When to Use

- Presenting a zone change (KRS 100.211-100.214), conditional use permit (KRS 100.237), variance (KRS 100.243), or development plan/subdivision plat (KRS 100.277-100.281)
- Attending a public informational meeting for a transportation or infrastructure project
- Presenting stormwater management or environmental mitigation plans to a concerned public audience

## Required Inputs

- **Hearing body**: Planning Commission, Board of Adjustments, Fiscal Court, or City Commission -- meeting date, time, location, procedural rules
- **Application type**: Zone change, conditional use permit, variance, development plan, subdivision plat, or text amendment
- **Project description**: Site address, parcel ID, acreage, current and proposed zoning/use, owner and applicant names
- **Site plan and exhibits**: Site plan, landscape plan, building elevations, traffic study summary, stormwater management summary
- **Staff report**: Planning staff recommendation and findings (obtain from planning department prior to hearing)
- **Public notice documentation**: Proof of published notice (KRS 424), posted sign, adjacent owner notification (KRS 100.212)
- **Known opposition**: Prior public comments, neighbor concerns, organized opposition

## Procedure

1. **Review KRS Chapter 100 criteria for the application type** -- Zone map amendment (KRS 100.213): existing use, suitability, vacant land extent, transportation access, proposal effect, comprehensive plan conformity. Conditional use (KRS 100.237): regulation compliance, effect on neighbors, infrastructure adequacy. Variance (KRS 100.243): unnecessary hardship, unique conditions, minimum variance necessary. Frame the entire presentation around demonstrating statutory criteria are met.
2. **Obtain and review the staff report** -- Contact planning department 3-5 business days before the hearing. Identify staff conditions of approval and determine whether JEDS/applicant can accept them. If staff recommends denial, prepare a point-by-point rebuttal addressing each finding.
3. **Prepare exhibit boards and digital presentation** -- Create exhibits: vicinity/location map with surrounding zoning, existing conditions plan, proposed site plan with dimensions/setbacks/parking, landscape/buffer plan, utility connection summary, 3D renderings (SketchUp/Lumion) for projects with visual impact, and traffic study summary graphic with trip generation and LOS. Use large-format boards (24x36 minimum) for in-person plus digital slideshow. Confirm A/V with planning department.
4. **Draft presentation script** -- Structure for 10-15 minutes (typical KY planning commission limit): introduce yourself/firm/client and state the request; describe site context, surrounding uses, current zoning, and comprehensive plan designation; walk through the proposed development using exhibits covering use, access, utilities, stormwater, landscaping; address each statutory criterion from KRS 100 with supporting facts; acknowledge staff conditions and state acceptance or proposed modifications; conclude with a summary and request for approval.
5. **Prepare for public comment and commissioner questions** -- Anticipate concerns: traffic (ITE Manual trip generation, intersection LOS), stormwater/flooding (detention sizing, KDOW compliance), property values (note KRS 100.213 focuses on land use compatibility, not value speculation), noise/lighting (photometric plan, operational hours), environmental (tree preservation, stream buffers, KY Heritage Council clearance), infrastructure capacity (utility availability letters). Prepare concise, factual responses. Address the board, not opponents.
6. **Coordinate with applicant/owner** -- Brief on hearing procedure, their testimony role, potential conditions, and appeal rights under KRS 100.347 (circuit court within 30 days). Prepare a conditions acceptance matrix: Condition, Accept/Modify/Object, Reasoning, Impact on Project.
7. **Assemble the record packet** -- Compile for the hearing record: application with supporting documents, legal notice proof of publication and posting affidavit, adjacent owner notification with certified mail receipts, traffic impact study, stormwater management report, environmental reports (wetland delineation, Phase I ESA, arborist report), utility availability letters, and staff report with applicant response to concerns.

## Output Format

Deliver a presentation package containing:
- **Presentation Script**: Speaker notes keyed to each exhibit, in direct language for oral delivery to a lay commission
- **Exhibit Set**: Numbered list (Exhibit A: Vicinity Map, Exhibit B: Site Plan, etc.) with print and digital versions
- **Q&A Preparation Sheet**: Anticipated questions organized by topic with concise factual answers
- **Conditions Matrix**: Table of known/anticipated conditions with accept/modify/object disposition
- **Record Packet Checklist**: Itemized document list with completeness confirmation
- **Hearing Logistics**: Date, time, location, A/V needs, JEDS and client attendee list

## Quality Checks

- [ ] Presentation directly addresses each statutory criterion under the applicable KRS 100 section
- [ ] All exhibits are legible at presentation scale and labeled with project name, date, and exhibit number
- [ ] Public notice requirements (KRS 424, KRS 100.212) are met and documentation is assembled
- [ ] Staff report obtained and every staff concern addressed in presentation or Q&A sheet
- [ ] Presentation fits within the hearing body's time limit (confirm with planning staff)
- [ ] All speakers identified with credentials (PE/PLA license number, firm name)
- [ ] Applicant briefed on procedure, potential conditions, and appeal rights under KRS 100.347
- [ ] Q&A responses cite specific plan provisions, code sections, or study data -- no vague assurances
- [ ] A/V and room logistics confirmed with planning department at least 48 hours before hearing
