# Public Hearing Presentation Preparation

## Purpose

Prepare comprehensive materials and talking points for public hearings, planning commission meetings, board of adjustments hearings, and zoning/land use proceedings before Kentucky municipal and county bodies. Presentations must convey technical project information to non-technical audiences, anticipate public concerns, and provide the factual record needed for the approving body to make a defensible decision under Kentucky planning and zoning law (KRS Chapter 100).

## When to Use

- Presenting a zone change application before a planning commission or fiscal court (KRS 100.211-100.214)
- Presenting a conditional use permit application before a board of adjustments (KRS 100.237)
- Presenting a development plan or subdivision plat for planning commission approval (KRS 100.277-100.281)
- Presenting a variance request before a board of adjustments (KRS 100.243)
- Attending a public informational meeting for a transportation or infrastructure project
- Presenting stormwater management or environmental mitigation plans to a concerned public audience

## Required Inputs

- **Hearing body**: Planning Commission, Board of Adjustments, Fiscal Court, City Commission, or other body -- with meeting date, time, location, and procedural rules
- **Application type**: Zone change, conditional use permit, variance, development plan, subdivision plat, or text amendment
- **Project description**: Site address, parcel ID, acreage, current zoning, proposed zoning or use, owner and applicant names
- **Site plan and exhibits**: Current site plan or development plan, landscape plan, building elevations, traffic study summary, stormwater management summary
- **Staff report**: Planning staff recommendation and findings (obtain from the planning department prior to hearing)
- **Public notice documentation**: Proof of published notice (KRS 424), posted sign on property, notification of adjacent property owners (KRS 100.212)
- **Known opposition**: Any prior public comments, neighbor concerns, or organized opposition

## Procedure

1. **Review KRS Chapter 100 requirements for the application type** -- Each application type has specific statutory criteria the approving body must evaluate:
   - Zone map amendment (KRS 100.213): existing use, suitability, extent of vacant land nearby, transportation access, effect of the proposal, conformity with comprehensive plan
   - Conditional use permit (KRS 100.237): compliance with regulations, effect on neighboring properties, adequacy of infrastructure
   - Variance (KRS 100.243): unnecessary hardship, unique property conditions, not merely for convenience, minimum variance necessary
   - Subdivision plat (KRS 100.277): compliance with subdivision regulations, adequate infrastructure, conformance with comprehensive plan
   Frame the entire presentation around demonstrating that statutory criteria are met.

2. **Obtain and review the staff report** -- Contact the planning department 3-5 business days before the hearing to request the staff report and recommendation. Identify any staff conditions of approval and determine whether JEDS/applicant can accept them. If staff recommends denial, prepare a point-by-point rebuttal addressing each finding.

3. **Prepare exhibit boards and digital presentation** -- Create clear, scaled visual exhibits:
   - Vicinity/location map showing the site in context with surrounding land uses and zoning
   - Existing conditions plan with annotated features (topography, tree cover, watercourses, structures)
   - Proposed site plan at readable scale with dimensions, setbacks, parking counts, building footprints
   - Landscape/buffer plan showing required screening per local regulations
   - Utility plan summary showing connection points to public water, sewer, and stormwater systems
   - 3D renderings or perspective views (SketchUp/Lumion output) for projects with significant visual impact
   - Traffic study summary graphic showing trip generation and level of service at key intersections
   Format for the hearing room: large-format boards (24x36 minimum) for in-person, plus a digital slideshow version. Confirm A/V equipment availability with the planning department.

4. **Draft presentation script** -- Structure for a 10-15 minute presentation (most KY planning commissions limit applicant presentations):
   - Introduction: Identify yourself, your firm, your client. State the application type and what is being requested.
   - Site context: Describe location, surrounding uses, current zoning, and comprehensive plan designation.
   - Project description: Walk through the proposed development using exhibits. Cover use, density/intensity, access, utilities, stormwater, landscaping.
   - Statutory criteria: Address each applicable criterion from KRS 100 directly. State facts that demonstrate compliance.
   - Staff conditions: Acknowledge staff-recommended conditions and state acceptance or proposed modifications.
   - Conclusion: Summarize why the application meets the statutory requirements and request approval.

5. **Prepare for public comment and commissioner questions** -- Anticipate concerns based on project type and location:
   - Traffic and access: Have trip generation data (ITE Manual) and intersection LOS ready
   - Stormwater and flooding: Reference the stormwater management report, detention/retention sizing, compliance with local and KDOW standards
   - Property values: Cite relevant data or comparable developments if available; note that KRS 100.213 criteria focus on land use compatibility, not property value speculation
   - Noise, lighting, hours of operation: Reference specific plan provisions (photometric plan, operational hours, sound barriers)
   - Environmental: Speak to tree preservation percentages, stream buffers, wetland avoidance, KY Heritage Council clearance
   - Infrastructure capacity: Confirm utility availability letters from water, sewer, and electric providers
   Prepare concise, factual responses. Avoid argumentative tone. Address the board, not opponents.

6. **Coordinate with the applicant/owner** -- Brief the property owner or developer on hearing procedure, their role (they may need to testify), potential conditions of approval that may be imposed, and the appeals process under KRS 100.347 (circuit court appeal within 30 days).

7. **Prepare a conditions acceptance matrix** -- List each anticipated or staff-recommended condition with columns: Condition, Accept/Modify/Object, Reasoning, Impact on Project. Have this ready so the team can respond in real time if the commission adds or modifies conditions during the hearing.

8. **Assemble the record packet** -- Compile all documents that should be entered into the hearing record:
   - Application and all supporting documents as submitted
   - Legal notice proof of publication and posting affidavit
   - Adjacent owner notification list and certified mail receipts
   - Traffic impact study (if applicable)
   - Stormwater management report
   - Environmental reports (wetland delineation, Phase I ESA, arborist report)
   - Utility availability letters
   - Staff report with applicant response to any staff concerns

## Output Format

Deliver a presentation package containing:
- **Presentation Script**: Annotated speaker notes keyed to each exhibit, written in direct language suitable for oral delivery to a lay commission
- **Exhibit Set**: Numbered exhibits list with descriptions (Exhibit A: Vicinity Map, Exhibit B: Site Plan, etc.)
- **Q&A Preparation Sheet**: Anticipated questions organized by topic with concise factual answers
- **Conditions Matrix**: Table of known/anticipated conditions with accept/modify/object disposition
- **Record Packet Checklist**: Itemized list of all documents to be submitted into the hearing record with confirmation of completeness
- **Hearing Logistics**: Date, time, location, parking, A/V needs, attendee list (who from JEDS and client team will attend)

## Quality Checks

- [ ] Presentation directly addresses each statutory criterion under the applicable KRS 100 section
- [ ] All exhibits are legible at presentation scale and labeled with project name, date, and exhibit number
- [ ] Public notice requirements (KRS 424, KRS 100.212) have been met and documentation is assembled
- [ ] Staff report has been obtained and every staff concern is addressed in the presentation or Q&A sheet
- [ ] Presentation fits within the time limit imposed by the hearing body (confirm with planning staff)
- [ ] All speakers are identified and their credentials stated (PE license number, PLA license number, firm name)
- [ ] The applicant/owner has been briefed on procedure, potential conditions, and appeal rights under KRS 100.347
- [ ] Q&A responses are factual and cite specific plan provisions, code sections, or study data -- no vague assurances
- [ ] A/V equipment and room logistics are confirmed with the planning department at least 48 hours before the hearing
- [ ] Digital and physical copies of all exhibits and the record packet are prepared in sufficient quantity
