# Permit Application Narrative

## Purpose

Generate a clear, technically accurate narrative for permit applications submitted to Kentucky regulatory agencies. The narrative must satisfy agency-specific requirements, demonstrate regulatory compliance, and support timely permit issuance for civil engineering and landscape architecture projects.

## When to Use

- Submitting KDOW (Kentucky Division of Water) KYR10 or individual KPDES stormwater permits
- Filing Louisville MSD or Lexington LFUCG floodplain development permits
- Preparing USACE Section 404 permit applications for impacts to waters of the US
- Submitting Kentucky Division of Air Quality (KDAQ) permit applications for land-disturbing activities
- Filing KDOW dam safety permits under KRS 151.291 through 151.297
- Preparing Kentucky Heritage Council (SHPO) Section 106 consultation narratives
- Applying for KYTC encroachment permits for work within state right-of-way

## Required Inputs

1. **Project description**: Location (county, city, lat/long), owner, total disturbed acreage, project type
2. **Permit type and receiving agency**: Specific permit program (e.g., KPDES KYR10, Section 404 Nationwide vs. Individual)
3. **Site conditions**: Existing land use, soils (NRCS Web Soil Survey data), topography, waters/wetlands present
4. **Proposed improvements**: Scope of grading, impervious area change, utility crossings, BMP selection
5. **Environmental resources**: Threatened/endangered species (KDFWR consultation), cultural resources, karst features
6. **Drainage and stormwater data**: Pre/post hydrology, receiving stream classification per 401 KAR 10:026
7. **Previous agency correspondence**: NOI acknowledgments, pre-application meeting notes, prior permit numbers

## Procedure

1. **Identify the permit pathway.** Confirm the correct permit type by referencing the KDOW permit decision flowchart. For USACE permits, determine whether Nationwide Permit conditions apply or an Individual Permit is required based on impact thresholds (greater than 0.5 acre of waters triggers Individual).
2. **Establish the regulatory framework.** Cite the governing Kentucky Revised Statutes (e.g., KRS 224 for water quality, KRS 151 for floodplain), Kentucky Administrative Regulations (401 KAR Chapter 5 for wastewater, 401 KAR Chapter 4 for stormwater), and any local overlay (Louisville MSD Chapter 159, LFUCG Article 22).
3. **Describe existing conditions.** Present the site physiography, reference the USGS HUC-12 watershed, identify soil hydrologic groups from NRCS data, note the FEMA flood zone designation per the effective FIRM panel, and identify any karst geology per KGS mapping.
4. **Detail the proposed project.** Describe all land-disturbing activities, phasing, and the construction sequence. Quantify total disturbed area, new impervious surface, and cut/fill volumes.
5. **Present the stormwater management strategy.** Describe BMP selection per the Kentucky BMP Planning and Design Manual. For Louisville MSD projects, demonstrate compliance with MSD Design Manual Chapter 8 post-construction requirements. For LFUCG, address the Stormwater Manual water quality volume (WQv) and channel protection volume (CPv) criteria.
6. **Address environmental impacts and avoidance/minimization.** Document the alternatives analysis for Section 404, describe stream and wetland avoidance measures, and reference KDFWR species occurrence data. For SHPO, summarize the Area of Potential Effects (APE) and findings of any Phase I cultural resource survey.
7. **Prepare the compensatory mitigation statement** (if applicable). For unavoidable impacts, describe proposed mitigation through the Kentucky in-lieu fee program administered by the Stream and Wetland Mitigation Fund or permittee-responsible mitigation.
8. **Compile certifications and signatures.** Ensure the narrative is signed and sealed by the Kentucky PE or PLA of record per KRS 322 (engineers) or KRS 323A (landscape architects).

## Output Format

- Narrative organized under agency-prescribed headings (mirror the permit application form section titles)
- 8.5x11 format, single-spaced, 11pt minimum font, with project header on each page
- Figures referenced in-line (Vicinity Map, Site Plan, BMP Plan, Environmental Constraints Map)
- Appendices for supporting data (soil logs, hydrology calculations, T&E species correspondence)
- Digital PDF with PE/PLA seal and signature for electronic submission via KDOW ePortal or USACE ORM

## Quality Checks

- [ ] All acreage figures match between the narrative, NOI form, and plan sheets
- [ ] Stream and wetland impact quantities are consistent with the delineation report
- [ ] Receiving stream name and assessment unit ID match the KDOW 303(d)/305(b) Integrated Report
- [ ] BMP sizing calculations reference correct rainfall data (NOAA Atlas 14 for Kentucky)
- [ ] FEMA flood zone designations match the current effective FIRM panel number
- [ ] Karst features are addressed if the site is within a mapped karst region per KGS
- [ ] Kentucky PE or PLA license number and expiration date appear on the signature page
- [ ] Agency-specific checklists are completed and included (e.g., KDOW NOI checklist, MSD submittal checklist)

## Template Reference

- KDOW KPDES KYR10 NOI form and instructions (current version from KDOW ePortal)
- USACE Louisville District Section 404 Pre-Construction Notification template
- Louisville MSD Floodplain Development Permit application (MSD Form 301)
- LFUCG Stormwater Quality Management Plan (SWQMP) narrative template
- KYTC Encroachment Permit application (TC 99-1 form)
- Kentucky BMP Planning and Design Manual (current edition)
- NOAA Atlas 14 Volume 2, Version 3 -- precipitation frequency estimates for Kentucky
