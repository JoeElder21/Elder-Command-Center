# Meeting Minutes Documentation

## Purpose
Produce clear, accurate, and actionable meeting minutes that serve as the official record of project decisions, action items, and direction for civil engineering and landscape architecture projects in Kentucky. Well-documented minutes protect the firm legally, maintain project momentum, and provide an auditable trail for public projects subject to KRS 61.870 (Open Records Act).

## When to Use
- Every client meeting, whether in-person, virtual, or by phone.
- Internal project team meetings where scope, schedule, or budget decisions are made.
- Pre-construction conferences and progress meetings with contractors.
- Agency coordination meetings with KDOW, KYTC, local planning commissions, or utility providers.
- Public meetings or hearings where the firm presents on behalf of a client (planning commission hearings, fiscal court presentations, public information meetings per KYTC requirements).

## Required Inputs
- Meeting date, time, location (or virtual platform).
- List of attendees with name, organization, and role (distinguish between in-person and virtual attendees).
- Meeting purpose or agenda distributed in advance.
- Reference documents: prior meeting minutes, RFI log, submittal log, project schedule, plan set revision number.
- Any presentation materials, exhibits, or handouts used during the meeting.

## Procedure
1. **Pre-Meeting Preparation** -- Review the previous meeting minutes and open action items. Prepare or distribute an agenda at least 24 hours in advance. Confirm the attendee list and identify any new participants who need background context.
2. **Attendance and Roll Call** -- Record all attendees at the start of the meeting. For public meetings subject to KRS 61.810 (Open Meetings Act), confirm a quorum if the body is a public agency board or commission. Note any attendees who arrive late or leave early.
3. **Agenda Item Documentation** -- For each agenda item, record:
   - Topic discussed and key points raised by each participant.
   - Decisions made, including any votes or consensus reached.
   - Alternatives considered and reasons for selection or rejection.
   - Action items generated, with responsible party and due date.
4. **Technical Decisions** -- Document technical decisions with sufficient detail for a PE or PLA reviewing the minutes to understand the basis of design. Include references to applicable standards (KDOW regulations, KYTC design manuals, local zoning ordinances, AASHTO, ADA/PROWAG requirements). Note if a decision deviates from standard practice and the justification.
5. **Regulatory and Permitting Items** -- Flag any discussion of permit requirements, agency submittals, or regulatory compliance. Note the specific permit (KPDES, floodplain development permit, conditional use permit, KYTC encroachment permit) and the responsible party for each action.
6. **Schedule and Budget Impacts** -- Record any discussion of schedule changes, milestone dates, or budget impacts. Note if a client-directed change may trigger a contract amendment or additional services request.
7. **Open Items Tracker** -- Maintain a running list of open items carried forward from previous meetings. Update the status of each item: closed, in progress, or deferred. Assign new due dates as needed.
8. **Draft and Review** -- Draft minutes within 2 business days of the meeting. Have the project manager review before distribution. For minutes involving regulatory commitments, have the responsible PE or PLA review for technical accuracy.
9. **Distribution** -- Distribute minutes to all attendees and relevant stakeholders within 3 business days. Request corrections or additions within 5 business days. File the finalized minutes in the project record.
10. **Follow-Up** -- Track action items through the project management system. Carry unresolved items forward to the next meeting agenda.

## Output Format
- Meeting minutes document (1-3 pages typical) with the following structure:
  - Header: project name, project number, meeting type, date, time, location.
  - Attendees: table listing name, organization, role, and attendance mode.
  - Agenda Items: numbered list with discussion summary, decisions, and action items.
  - Action Item Table: columns for item number, description, responsible party, due date, and status.
  - Next Meeting: date, time, location, and preliminary agenda topics.
  - Attachments: reference to any exhibits, sign-in sheets, or handouts.

## Quality Checks
- Minutes accurately reflect decisions made, not just topics discussed; every decision has a clear outcome recorded.
- Every action item has a named responsible party (not a team or organization) and a specific due date.
- Technical decisions include enough context that a reviewer unfamiliar with the meeting could understand the rationale.
- Public meeting minutes comply with KRS 61.835 requirements for written minutes of public agencies.
- Minutes are distributed within the 3-business-day standard; late distribution is flagged to the project manager.
- Open items from prior meetings are accounted for with updated status.
- No confidential or privileged information (attorney-client communications, draft opinions) is included in minutes distributed to external parties.

## Template Reference
- Standard Meeting Minutes template with firm letterhead, project header block, and pre-formatted sections.
- Action Item Tracker template (spreadsheet) with sort/filter by responsible party and due date.
- Public Meeting Sign-In Sheet template compliant with KRS 61.810 requirements.
- Pre-Construction Conference Minutes template tailored to Kentucky contractor coordination requirements.
