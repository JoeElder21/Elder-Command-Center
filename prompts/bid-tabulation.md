# Bid Tabulation and Contractor Evaluation

## Purpose

Tabulate construction bids and evaluate contractor qualifications for civil engineering and landscape architecture projects in Kentucky. Ensures a fair, transparent procurement process that complies with Kentucky public bidding statutes, identifies the lowest responsible and responsive bidder, and documents the award recommendation for the owner or governing body.

## When to Use

- Public bid openings subject to KRS 424.260 (city/county over $30,000) or KYTC project lettings
- KIA-funded (State Revolving Fund) water and sewer infrastructure projects
- Private or institutional projects using competitive bidding procurement
- Pre-qualification evaluations or post-bid evaluation when responsiveness or responsibility is in question

## Required Inputs

- Complete bid documents: advertisement, instructions to bidders, bid form, and contract documents
- All submitted bid packages (sealed bids opened at the public bid opening)
- Engineer's estimate of probable construction cost
- Bid bond or bid security from each bidder (minimum 5% per KRS 45A.185 for state projects)
- Kentucky contractor license verification (KRS 198B.060, projects over $15,000)
- Bidder pre-qualification statements, DBE/MBE/WBE commitments, and addenda acknowledgments
- Kentucky Secretary of State business registration status for each bidder

## Procedure

1. **Bid Opening Documentation**: Record each bid as opened, including bidder name, base bid amount, alternate prices (if applicable), unit prices for specified items, and bid security type and amount. For public projects, this must occur at the time, date, and place stated in the advertisement per KRS 424.260. Record the names of all attendees. Note any bids received after the deadline -- these must be returned unopened.

2. **Arithmetic Verification**: Check all bid extensions (unit price times quantity) and subtotals. Where a discrepancy exists between the unit price and the extended amount, the unit price governs per standard bidding practice (EJCDC C-200 Section 13.04, AIA A701, or KYTC Standard Specification 102.07). Correct all extensions and recalculate each bidder's total. Flag any corrected bids and document the original and corrected amounts.

3. **Responsiveness Review**: Verify each bid for compliance with the solicitation requirements:
   - Bid form is complete with all blanks filled, no conditional or qualified language
   - Bid bond or cashier's check meets the required percentage (5% minimum for KY public projects)
   - All addenda are acknowledged
   - Required certifications are included (non-collusion affidavit, prevailing wage compliance certification per KRS 337.505, lobbying certification for federally funded projects)
   - DBE/MBE participation commitment meets the project goal (if applicable)
   - Unit prices for unbalanced bid analysis (see step 5)
   - Bidder signed the bid form and is an authorized representative

4. **Responsibility Evaluation**: Assess the apparent low bidder's ability to perform the work:
   - Valid Kentucky general contractor license (verify at dhbc.ky.gov) with appropriate classification for the project scope
   - Adequate bonding capacity for the contract amount (performance and payment bonds per KRS 45A.190)
   - Relevant experience: minimum of three comparable projects completed in the past five years
   - Current workload and capacity to staff the project
   - Safety record: OSHA EMR (Experience Modification Rate) below 1.0 preferred
   - No debarment or suspension (check SAM.gov for federal, Finance Cabinet for state)
   - Good standing with Kentucky Secretary of State
   - For Louisville MSD projects, verify compliance with MSD's contractor qualification requirements

5. **Unbalanced Bid Analysis**: Compare each bidder's unit prices against the engineer's estimate and against each other. Identify materially unbalanced bids where front-loaded items (mobilization, erosion control, early earthwork) are significantly above the engineer's estimate while later items are below. A mathematically unbalanced bid (where the bid total does not change but individual items are skewed) may be acceptable; a materially unbalanced bid (where the owner may pay more if quantities shift) is grounds for rejection. Flag any unit price that deviates more than 25% from the engineer's estimate for closer review.

6. **Bid Tabulation Assembly**: Create the formal tabulation spreadsheet listing all bidders ranked by corrected total bid price. Include columns for each bid item showing unit price and extension. Show the engineer's estimate alongside for comparison. Calculate the spread between low bid and engineer's estimate (acceptable range is typically within plus or minus 10%). Calculate the spread between low and second-low bidders. Note any bids deemed non-responsive and the specific reason.

7. **Award Recommendation**: Prepare a written recommendation to the owner or governing body identifying the lowest responsive, responsible bidder. For public projects, the award must go to the lowest responsible bidder per KRS 45A.365 (state) or KRS 424.260 (local). Document the basis for any determination that a lower bidder is non-responsive or non-responsible. If all bids exceed the budget or engineer's estimate by more than 10%, recommend options: negotiate with low bidder, re-scope and re-bid, or request additional funding.

## Output Format

- Formal bid tabulation spreadsheet with all bidders, line items, extensions, and corrected totals ranked low to high
- Bid opening minutes documenting time, location, attendees, and bids received
- Responsiveness and responsibility checklist completed for each bidder
- Award recommendation letter (1-2 pages) with recommended bidder, basis of award, and budget comparison
- Engineer's estimate comparison showing percentage deviation by major category and total

## Quality Checks

- All arithmetic extensions have been independently verified and corrected totals are confirmed
- Every responsiveness requirement from the instructions to bidders has been checked for each bid
- Contractor license verification is documented with license number, classification, and expiration
- Bid security amounts meet the minimum percentage required by the solicitation and applicable statute
- Unbalanced bid analysis has been performed and documented for the apparent low bidder at minimum
- Award recommendation complies with KRS 45A.365 or KRS 424.260 as applicable
- Non-responsibility determinations include specific factual findings to withstand protest under KRS 45A.285
- DBE/MBE participation commitments and all addenda acknowledgments are verified for the recommended bidder

## Template Reference

- KRS 424.260 (local competitive bidding), KRS 45A.180-460 (Model Procurement Code), KRS 45A.285 (bid protests), KRS 198B.060 (contractor licensing)
- KYTC Standard Specifications Section 102 (Bidding Requirements and Conditions)
- EJCDC C-200 and AIA A701 Instructions to Bidders
