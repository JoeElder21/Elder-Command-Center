# Pre-Application Meeting Preparation

## Purpose
Prepare a structured briefing package and question agenda for a pre-application conference with a Kentucky regulatory or planning agency. These meetings identify potential objections, confirm submission requirements, and build working relationships with staff before formal application.

## When to Use
- Before filing a zone map amendment, conditional use permit, or development plan with LFUCG or Louisville Metro Planning
- Before submitting a floodplain development permit to the local floodplain administrator or KDOW
- Before filing a KPDES permit or Section 401 certification with KDEP Division of Water
- Before submitting an encroachment permit or access modification to a KYTC District Office
- Before filing a subdivision plat with a county planning commission under KRS Chapter 100

## Required Inputs
1. **Agency and contact**: Agency name, specific division, reviewing staff member if known
2. **Project description**: County, address, PVA parcel ID, acreage, current zoning, proposed use, and preliminary site concept
3. **Application type**: The specific permit or entitlement being sought
4. **Known issues**: Site constraints, prior application history, or neighborhood concerns from due diligence
5. **Attendees**: Names and roles (owner, PE, PLA, attorney, developer)

## Procedure
1. **Agency Research and Scheduling** -- Identify the correct reviewing body: LFUCG Division of Planning, Louisville Metro Planning and Design Services, Louisville MSD, KDOW Floodplain Section, KDEP Surface Water Permits Branch, KYTC District Office, or USACE Louisville District. Request the meeting in writing with a brief project description. Ask the agency to identify all staff who should attend.
2. **Briefing Package Assembly** -- Prepare printed and digital copies containing: (a) one-page project narrative; (b) vicinity map at 1"=2000'; (c) aerial with parcel boundary and adjacent zoning; (d) preliminary sketch-level site plan showing building footprints, access points, open space, and stormwater areas; (e) constraint map overlaying FIRM floodplain, NWI wetlands, slopes exceeding 15%, karst features, easements, and setbacks; (f) zoning summary sheet with dimensional standards and compliance status; (g) utility diagram with connections and estimated demand; (h) KYTC traffic counts with preliminary ITE trip generation estimate.
3. **Question Agenda** -- Prepare numbered questions by topic. Zoning: comprehensive plan consistency, KRS 100.213 findings, binding elements from prior approvals. Site Design: access configuration, landscaping buffers at zone interfaces, open space dedication (LFUCG typically 10% for 50+ lot subdivisions), tree preservation triggers. Infrastructure: traffic impact study scope, sewer capacity, stormwater detention and water quality BMP preferences. Process: review timeline, submission deadlines tied to planning commission cycles, neighborhood meeting requirements, assigned point of contact.
4. **Conduct Meeting** -- Deliver a 5-minute project overview, then transition to questions. Take detailed notes with names and titles of each staff member providing input. Ask staff to identify potential obstacles and recommended design modifications. Request written follow-up confirming substantive guidance.
5. **Post-Meeting Documentation** -- Within 48 hours, prepare a meeting summary memorandum organized by topic. Distribute to the project team and client. Update the project schedule and budget based on newly identified requirements. Flag project-stopper concerns immediately with a recommended path forward.

## Output Format
- Briefing package: tabbed set of exhibits on 11x17 plan sheets and 8.5x11 narrative pages
- Question agenda: numbered questions by topic with space for recording responses
- Post-meeting memorandum: summary organized by topic with action items and responsible parties
- Updated project schedule reflecting any newly identified requirements or review cycles

## Quality Checks
- Package communicates the concept without verbal explanation but does not imply committed design decisions
- All plan exhibits include north arrow, scale bar, legend, and date
- Zoning references cite specific code sections (e.g., LFUCG Article 8-26 for B-6P, not just "B-6P")
- Traffic data uses current KYTC counts from the online traffic count map, not estimates
- Constraint map uses actual data sources (survey, FIRM panels, NWI), not aerial photo approximations
- Question agenda includes process and timeline questions, not only technical items

## Template Reference
- KRS 100.211-100.214 (zone amendment procedures) and KRS 100.237 (conditional use permits)
- LFUCG Zoning Ordinance and Land Subdivision Regulations
- Louisville Metro Land Development Code (LMCO Ch. 1)
- KYTC Access Management Guide; ITE Trip Generation Manual (current edition)
- KDOW Floodplain Management Section application forms
