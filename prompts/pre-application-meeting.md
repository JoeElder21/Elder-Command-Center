# Preparation for Pre-Application Meetings with Agencies

## Purpose
Prepare a structured briefing package and question agenda for a pre-application conference with a Kentucky regulatory or planning agency. These meetings identify potential objections, confirm submission requirements, and build working relationships with staff before formal application.

## When to Use
- Before filing a zone map amendment, conditional use permit, or development plan with LFUCG Division of Planning or Louisville Metro Planning and Design Services.
- Before submitting a floodplain development permit to the local floodplain administrator or KDOW.
- Before filing a KPDES permit or Section 401 certification with KDEP Division of Water.
- Before submitting an encroachment permit or access modification to a KYTC District Office.
- Before filing a subdivision plat with a county planning commission under KRS Chapter 100.

## Required Inputs
1. **Agency and contact**: Agency name, specific division, reviewing staff member if known.
2. **Project description**: County, address, PVA parcel ID, acreage, current zoning, proposed use, and preliminary site concept.
3. **Application type**: The specific permit or entitlement being sought.
4. **Known issues**: Site constraints, prior application history, or neighborhood concerns from due diligence.
5. **Attendees**: Names and roles (owner, PE, PLA, attorney, developer).

## Procedure
**Step 1 -- Agency Research and Scheduling**: Identify the correct reviewing body: LFUCG Division of Planning (zoning/subdivision); Louisville Metro Planning and Design Services (zoning/form district/subdivision); Louisville MSD (stormwater/sewer/floodplain in Jefferson County); KDOW Floodplain Section (floodplain permits outside Louisville MSD); KDEP Surface Water Permits Branch (KPDES/Section 401); KYTC District 5 (central KY) or District 6 (Jefferson County) for state road access; USACE Louisville District Regulatory Branch (Section 404); local fire marshal. Request the meeting in writing with a brief project description and proposed agenda. Ask the agency to identify all staff who should attend.

**Step 2 -- Briefing Package**: Assemble printed and digital copies containing: (a) one-page project narrative stating the site, proposed development, phasing, and specific approval requested; (b) vicinity map at 1"=2000' showing site context with surrounding uses and major roads; (c) recent aerial with parcel boundary and adjacent zoning annotations; (d) preliminary sketch-level site plan showing building footprints or lot layout, access points, open space, and stormwater areas -- communicate intent, not construction documents; (e) constraint map overlaying FIRM floodplain boundaries, NWI wetlands, slopes exceeding 15%/20%, karst features, easements, and setback lines; (f) zoning summary sheet with current zone, proposed zone/use, dimensional standards, and how the concept meets or deviates from each; (g) preliminary utility diagram showing connections to water/sewer/electric/gas with distances and estimated demand; (h) KYTC traffic counts on adjacent roads with preliminary ITE trip generation estimate.

**Step 3 -- Question Agenda**: Prepare numbered questions by topic. Zoning: Does staff view the use as consistent with the comprehensive plan? Are there pending plan amendments affecting the parcel? What KRS 100.213 findings will be most scrutinized? Are there binding elements from prior approvals on this or adjacent parcels? Site Design: Does the access configuration meet agency standards? What landscaping buffers or screening apply at residential zone interfaces? What open space/parkland dedication percentage applies (LFUCG typically 10% for 50+ lot subdivisions)? Are tree preservation requirements triggered? Infrastructure: Will a traffic impact study be required, and at what scope? Is sewer capacity sufficient? What stormwater detention and water quality standards apply, and does staff prefer specific BMP types? Are CIP projects planned that could affect the development timeline? Process: What is the current review timeline from submission to hearing? What are the submission deadlines tied to planning commission cycles? Is a neighborhood meeting required or recommended? Who is the assigned point of contact?

**Step 4 -- Conduct the Meeting**: Bring two copies of the briefing package. Deliver a 5-minute project overview, then transition to questions. Take detailed notes on every staff comment with the name and title of each speaker. Ask staff to identify potential obstacles and what design modifications might address them. Request written follow-up confirming substantive guidance. Do not argue staff positions; the purpose is information gathering, not advocacy.

**Step 5 -- Post-Meeting Documentation**: Within 48 hours, prepare a meeting summary memorandum organized by topic. Distribute to the full project team and client. Update the project schedule and budget based on newly identified requirements. Flag any project-stopper concerns immediately to the client with a recommended path forward.

## Output Format
1. **Briefing Package**: Tabbed set of exhibits on 11x17 plan sheets and 8.5x11 narrative pages.
2. **Question Agenda**: Numbered questions by topic with space for recording responses; header block with project name, date/time/location, attendees, and agency file number.

## Quality Checks
- Package must communicate the concept without verbal explanation but not imply committed design decisions.
- All plan exhibits must include north arrow, scale bar, legend, and date.
- Zoning references must cite specific code sections (e.g., LFUCG Zoning Ordinance Article 8-26 for B-6P, not just "B-6P").
- Traffic data must use current KYTC counts from the online traffic count map tool, not estimates.
- Constraint map must use actual data sources (survey, FIRM panels, NWI), not aerial photo approximations.
- Question agenda must include process/timeline questions, not only technical; missing a submission deadline delays the project by an entire review cycle.

## Template Reference
- ITE Trip Generation Manual (current edition); KYTC Access Management Guide.
- LFUCG Zoning Ordinance and Land Subdivision Regulations; Louisville Metro Land Development Code (LMCO Ch. 1).
- KDOW Floodplain Management Section application forms; KRS 100.211-100.214 (zone amendment procedures).
