# Jurisdiction Profile: Development Requirements

## Purpose
Build a comprehensive reference profile of a Kentucky jurisdiction's development regulations, approval processes, fee structures, and staff contacts. This profile becomes the go-to document when initiating any project within that jurisdiction, eliminating repeated research and preventing missed requirements.

## When to Use
- Entering a jurisdiction where the firm has not previously worked.
- Onboarding a new client with properties in an unfamiliar county or city.
- Updating an existing profile after a jurisdiction adopts new ordinances or fee schedules.
- Preparing a proposal that requires demonstrating familiarity with local regulations.

## Required Inputs
1. **Jurisdiction**: City, county, or consolidated government (e.g., Louisville Metro, LFUCG, City of Bowling Green/Warren County, City of Richmond/Madison County).
2. **Development type**: Residential, commercial, industrial, mixed-use, or all types.
3. **Specific area**: If the jurisdiction has multiple planning areas or overlay districts, identify the relevant geography.

## Procedure
**Step 1 -- Governance Structure**: Identify structure: consolidated city-county (Louisville Metro, LFUCG), independent city, or county with/without KRS Ch. 100 planning and zoning. Determine final approval authority for each type: zone amendments (planning commission to legislative body per KRS 100.211); conditional use permits (board of adjustment per KRS 100.237); subdivision plats (planning commission per KRS 100.277); development/site plans (commission or staff-level); variances (board of adjustment per KRS 100.243). Note whether the jurisdiction has adopted its own subdivision regulations or uses KRS 100.273-100.291 minimums. Confirm NFIP participation and identify the local floodplain administrator.

**Step 2 -- Zoning Ordinance Summary**: Obtain the current ordinance; catalog all zone classifications with permitted uses, conditional uses, and dimensional standards (setbacks, height, lot area, width, FAR, parking). Document form-based code or overlays (Louisville Metro form districts; LFUCG corridor overlays). Record sign, outdoor lighting, and landscaping/buffering requirements for each zone interface. Note specific use standards for wireless facilities (KRS 100.9865-100.987), solar systems, drive-throughs, and home occupations. Record comprehensive plan adoption date, future land use categories, and pending amendments.

**Step 3 -- Subdivision Regulations**: Document classification system (minor vs. major, administrative vs. commission approval). Record minimum design standards: street width/pavement section/ROW, lot dimensions by zone, block length maximums, sidewalk requirements, open space/parkland dedication formula (LFUCG per Parks Master Plan), street tree species/spacing/caliper. Note whether the jurisdiction requires improvement completion before plat recording or accepts surety bonds (KRS 100.277). Document plat submission deadlines relative to commission meeting dates.

**Step 4 -- Stormwater**: Identify authority (Louisville MSD, LFUCG Div. of Water Quality, or local MS4 holder). Record design storm standards for detention (2-, 10-, 25-, 100-year) and release rate methodology. Document approved post-construction BMPs, maintenance agreement requirements, and long-term maintenance responsibility. Note erosion control threshold (typically 1 acre per KPDES KYR10). Record stream buffer/setback requirements beyond KDOW minimums. Note stormwater utility fee and available credits.

**Step 5 -- Utility Matrix**: For each utility (water, sanitary sewer, electric, gas, telecom), document: provider name (e.g., KY American Water, Louisville MSD, KU/LG&E, Columbia Gas, AT&T), contact/phone, tap/connection fee by meter size, extension policy (developer-built vs. utility-reimbursed oversizing), and design standards reference document. Document the will-serve letter process and timeline. Note special requirements: grease interceptors, fire flow testing, backflow prevention.

**Step 6 -- Transportation**: Identify KYTC-maintained and locally maintained roads. Record KYTC district office contact and encroachment permit process. Document traffic impact study trigger thresholds (Louisville Metro: 100+ peak hour trips; LFUCG per Transportation Planning guidelines). Note access management regulations, sight distance standards, turn lane warrant criteria, and complete streets/multimodal requirements (bike lanes, shared-use paths, transit stops).

**Step 7 -- Fee Schedule**: Compile all development fees: zone amendment, CUP, variance, development plan review; preliminary and final plat review; grading permit; building permit (per SF or valuation); stormwater/erosion control permit; utility tap/connection; ROW encroachment permit; impact fees if adopted under KRS 65.760-65.766; park fee-in-lieu; public infrastructure inspection fees. Record source document name and effective date for each.

**Step 8 -- Contacts and Process**: Build directory with direct phone/email for: planning director, principal reviewers, zoning enforcement officer, stormwater manager, public works director, building official, fire marshal, floodplain administrator, KYTC district permit engineer, utility engineering contacts. For each major approval type, document the step-by-step process: pre-application meeting, submission/completeness review, staff technical review cycles, public notification (KRS 100.212 newspaper/mail/posted sign), hearing format, legislative action timeline for zone changes, and appeal period (KRS 100.347). Note typical total timeline from filing to final approval.

## Output Format
Single reference document with: header (jurisdiction, date prepared/updated, preparer); table of contents; governance summary; zoning table with dimensional standards; subdivision standards table; stormwater standards; utility matrix; transportation standards; comprehensive fee table with effective dates; contact directory; process flowcharts for each approval type; appendix linking to downloadable ordinances, manuals, and application forms.

## Quality Checks
- All ordinance references must cite specific chapter, article, and section numbers.
- Fees must include source document and effective date; flag as "verify" if source is over 12 months old.
- Contacts must be verified within 30 days of profile creation; staff turnover is frequent in smaller jurisdictions.
- Profile must be updated at minimum annually or upon ordinance amendments.
- Stormwater standards must be cross-checked against the jurisdiction's KPDES MS4 permit and local design manual.
- Distinguish codified requirements (mandatory) from staff preferences (negotiable).
- For jurisdictions without planning/zoning, note that only state regulations (KDOW, KDEP, KYTC, KRS 198B building code) apply and clearly document the absence of local zoning.

## Template Reference
- KRS Ch. 100 (planning/zoning), KRS 198B (building code), KRS 65.760-65.766 (impact fees).
- LFUCG Zoning Ordinance (Articles 1-23), Land Subdivision Regs, and Comprehensive Plan.
- Louisville Metro Land Development Code (LMCO Ch. 1), Cornerstone 2040 Comprehensive Plan.
- KYTC Access Management Guide and Highway Design Manual.
- KDOW floodplain regulations (401 KAR 4:060) and NFIP community status listings.
