# Project Kickoff Checklist and Setup

## Purpose
Ensure every new project starts with a complete administrative, technical, and communication framework in place before billable work begins. A thorough kickoff prevents scope creep, miscommunication, and compliance gaps on civil engineering and landscape architecture projects in Kentucky, whether for a municipal water line extension or a private commercial site development.

## When to Use
- Immediately upon receipt of a signed contract, notice to proceed, or purchase order.
- When a task order is issued under an existing master services agreement or on-call contract.
- When a dormant project is reactivated after a prolonged hold period (greater than 6 months).
- When a project transfers from one project manager to another mid-stream.

## Required Inputs
- Signed contract or agreement with scope of services, fee, and schedule.
- Client contact list: project owner, day-to-day contact, invoicing contact, and any third-party stakeholders.
- Site information: address, parcel ID, county, planning jurisdiction, and any existing surveys, plans, or reports.
- Applicable permits and regulatory requirements identified during the proposal phase.
- Subconsultant agreements (geotechnical, surveying, environmental, structural) if applicable.
- Insurance certificates: firm COI naming client, subconsultant COIs naming the firm.

## Procedure
1. **Project Number and Accounting Setup** -- Assign a unique project number per the firm's numbering convention. Create billing phases/tasks aligned with the contract scope (e.g., Phase 1: Survey, Phase 2: Design, Phase 3: Permitting, Phase 4: Bidding, Phase 5: Construction Administration). Set up the project in the accounting system with the contract amount, billing method (lump sum, hourly NTE, cost-plus), and retainage terms.
2. **Project Folder Structure** -- Create the digital project folder following the firm's standard hierarchy:
   - 01-Contract and Correspondence
   - 02-Survey and Base Mapping
   - 03-Design and Calculations
   - 04-Specifications
   - 05-Permits and Regulatory
   - 06-Bid Documents
   - 07-Construction Administration
   - 08-Subconsultants
   - 09-Photos and Field Documentation
3. **Team Assignment** -- Designate the project manager, lead designer, QA/QC reviewer, and CAD/BIM technician. Confirm the PE of record (licensed in Kentucky, KRS 322) and PLA of record (licensed in Kentucky, KRS 323A) if landscape architecture services are in scope. Verify all assigned staff have current Kentucky licensure and KDOW certifications as needed.
4. **Scope Review and Work Breakdown** -- Conduct an internal scope review meeting with the project team. Walk through every deliverable listed in the contract. Identify any ambiguities or gaps that need clarification with the client before work starts. Create a detailed work breakdown structure with hours budgeted per task.
5. **Schedule Development** -- Build the project schedule with milestones for: data collection complete, 30%/60%/90%/100% design submittals, permit application submittals (KDOW, KYTC, local planning), client review periods, bid advertisement, and construction start. Account for typical agency review times: KDOW construction permits (60-90 days), KYTC encroachment permits (30-60 days), local planning commission review (varies by jurisdiction, confirm the meeting schedule).
6. **Regulatory Checklist** -- Compile the project-specific regulatory checklist:
   - KDOW: KPDES permit, floodplain development permit, dam safety permit, water/wastewater construction permit.
   - KYTC: encroachment permit, drainage study requirements, access permit.
   - Local: zoning compliance, conditional use permit, subdivision plat approval, building permit.
   - Federal: USACE Section 404 permit, SHPO Section 106 review, NEPA categorical exclusion or EA.
   - Stormwater: KYR10 construction general permit, post-construction BMP requirements per local MS4 ordinance.
7. **Subconsultant Coordination** -- Issue notice to proceed to subconsultants. Confirm their scope, schedule, and deliverable format requirements. Establish the QA/QC review workflow for subconsultant deliverables. Verify subconsultant Kentucky licensure and insurance compliance.
8. **Client Kickoff Meeting** -- Schedule and conduct the external kickoff meeting with the client. Agenda items: introduce the project team, confirm scope and deliverables, review the schedule and milestone dates, establish communication protocols (primary contacts, email vs. phone, meeting cadence), discuss data needs and site access, and confirm invoicing and payment procedures.
9. **Communication Plan** -- Document the communication protocol: meeting frequency (biweekly for active design, monthly for permitting phase), reporting format (written status updates vs. verbal briefings), and escalation path for issues requiring client decisions.
10. **Risk Register** -- Identify project risks at kickoff: site access constraints, environmental contamination potential, utility conflicts, regulatory uncertainty, client funding contingencies. Assign a risk owner and mitigation strategy for each.

## Output Format
- Completed Project Kickoff Checklist (signed by PM and principal-in-charge).
- Work Breakdown Structure with budgeted hours per task.
- Project schedule (Gantt chart or milestone list).
- Regulatory checklist with permit types, responsible parties, and target submission dates.
- Communication plan document shared with the client.
- Risk register with initial entries and assigned owners.
- Internal kickoff meeting notes and external kickoff meeting minutes.

## Quality Checks
- Contract amount in the accounting system matches the signed agreement exactly.
- All team members have verified, current Kentucky licenses for the services they will perform.
- Regulatory checklist reviewed by a senior PE or PLA familiar with the project jurisdiction.
- Schedule accounts for realistic agency review times, not optimistic assumptions.
- Subconsultant agreements are fully executed before subconsultant work begins.
- Client has confirmed the communication protocol and primary contacts in writing (email confirmation acceptable).
- Risk register is reviewed and updated at the first progress meeting.

## Template Reference
- Project Kickoff Checklist template (one-page checklist format with sign-off lines).
- Work Breakdown Structure template (spreadsheet with task, hours, rate, and fee columns).
- Standard Project Folder Structure template (directory tree for digital file setup).
- Regulatory Checklist template organized by agency with fields for permit type, applicability, submittal date, and approval date.
- Risk Register template with columns for risk description, probability, impact, owner, and mitigation.
