# Landscape Plan Narrative

## Purpose
Draft the written narrative that accompanies landscape plan submittals to local planning agencies. The narrative explains design intent, plant selection rationale, compliance with buffer and screening requirements, and maintenance commitments required for plan approval.

## When to Use
- Submitting a landscape plan as part of a development plan or subdivision application
- Responding to planning staff comments requesting additional landscape justification
- Preparing a variance or waiver request for landscape buffer requirements
- Documenting compliance with tree preservation ordinances

## Required Inputs
1. **Approved site plan or development plan** with building footprints, parking, and circulation
2. **Local landscape ordinance** (Louisville Metro LDC Chapter 10, LFUCG Article 18, or applicable county code)
3. **Plant schedule** with botanical and common names, sizes at installation, and quantities
4. **Buffer yard requirements** per the applicable zoning district and adjacent land use
5. **Existing tree survey** if a tree canopy preservation ordinance applies
6. **Irrigation plan or waiver** if jurisdiction requires one
7. **Site context** -- adjacent land uses, street frontage, screening needs

## Procedure
1. **Ordinance Requirements Summary** -- Cite the specific code sections that govern landscaping for this project. Identify the required buffer yard type (A, B, C, or D under Louisville Metro; similar tiering under LFUCG), interior parking lot landscaping ratio, street tree spacing, and screening standards for dumpsters, loading areas, and mechanical equipment.
2. **Design Intent Statement** -- Describe the overall landscape concept: how plantings frame entries, screen incompatible uses, manage stormwater (if bioretention or rain gardens are integrated), and enhance the streetscape. Reference the comprehensive plan's goals for urban tree canopy or green infrastructure if applicable.
3. **Plant Selection Rationale** -- Justify species choices based on USDA Hardiness Zone 6a-6b (most of Kentucky), drought tolerance, salt tolerance for plantings near roadways, native species preference, and disease resistance. Note any species prohibited by the jurisdiction (e.g., Bradford pear, Pyrus calleryana, now prohibited by KY SB 64 effective 2024).
4. **Buffer Yard Compliance** -- For each buffer yard, state the required width, number of canopy trees, understory trees, and shrubs per 100 linear feet. Document how the plan meets or exceeds each requirement. If a reduced buffer is proposed, state the basis for the variance request.
5. **Interior Parking Lot Landscaping** -- Calculate the required landscape area within the parking lot (Louisville Metro requires one canopy tree per 10 parking spaces; LFUCG has similar standards). Confirm island dimensions and soil volume meet minimum requirements.
6. **Tree Preservation** -- If existing trees are being preserved, describe protection measures during construction: tree protection fencing at the drip line, no grade changes within the critical root zone, and arborist supervision for any root pruning within 10 feet of preserved trees.
7. **Maintenance and Surety** -- Describe the maintenance plan for the establishment period (typically two growing seasons). Address replacement standards for plant material that fails to survive. Note any required landscape surety bond and its amount per the local code.

## Output Format
- Title block referencing the project name, case number, and applicant
- Numbered narrative sections matching the ordinance's review criteria
- Plant schedule table (species, size, quantity, spacing, root condition)
- Compliance summary table mapping each code section to plan compliance status
- Signature block for the PLA of record with license number and seal

## Quality Checks
- All plant species verified as available from Kentucky nursery suppliers for the planting season
- Caliper sizes and container sizes match the jurisdiction's minimum installation standards
- Buffer yard calculations verified by measuring actual linear footage, not scaling from a small-scale plan
- No invasive species listed on the KY Exotic Pest Plant Council invasive list
- Narrative addresses every comment item from prior staff review (if a resubmittal)
- PLA seal and signature present on the narrative and plan sheets

## Template Reference
- Louisville Metro Land Development Code Chapter 10 (Landscaping, Screening, and Open Space)
- LFUCG Zoning Ordinance Article 18 (Landscaping and Screening)
- Kentucky Exotic Pest Plant Council invasive species list
- USDA Plant Hardiness Zone Map (current edition)
- KY SB 64 (Callery pear prohibition)
