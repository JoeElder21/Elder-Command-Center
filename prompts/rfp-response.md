# RFP/RFQ Response Preparation

## Purpose

Prepare competitive responses to Requests for Proposals (RFP) and Requests for Qualifications (RFQ) issued by public agencies, municipalities, and private developers in Kentucky. Responses must demonstrate JEDS qualifications in civil engineering and landscape architecture while complying with Kentucky Model Procurement Code (KRS 45A) for public projects and Brooks Act (QBS) selection procedures used by KYTC, LFUCG, and other Kentucky agencies.

## When to Use

- A Kentucky municipality, county, or state agency issues an RFP/RFQ for engineering or landscape architecture services
- A private developer requests competitive proposals from multiple firms
- KYTC posts a project advertisement requiring SOQ submission through the Consultant Information System
- LFUCG, Louisville Metro, or other local government posts a professional services solicitation
- A prime consultant requests JEDS subconsultant qualifications package

## Required Inputs

- **RFP/RFQ document**: Full solicitation including scope, evaluation criteria, page limits, submission format, and deadline
- **Issuing agency**: Name, contact person, submission address (physical and/or electronic portal)
- **Project specifics**: Location, estimated construction cost, project type, funding source (federal, state, local -- affects DBE/MBE requirements)
- **Evaluation criteria and weights**: Technical approach, qualifications, past performance, DBE participation, fee (if applicable)
- **JEDS team availability**: Staff loading for proposed project manager and key personnel
- **Teaming partners**: Subconsultants needed (survey, geotech, environmental, traffic, structural) and their current SF 330 data

## Procedure

1. **Analyze the solicitation** -- Read the full RFP/RFQ. Extract: scope of services, evaluation criteria with point values, page limits, required forms, insurance requirements, DBE/MBE goals, submission deadline and method, contract type (lump sum, hourly, on-call). Flag any mandatory pre-proposal meeting dates.
2. **Score the opportunity** -- Assess go/no-go: Does JEDS have relevant project experience within the last 5 years? Is the project geographically feasible? Do we have the staff capacity? Is the selection criteria weighted toward qualifications (favoring our strengths) or lowest price? Is incumbent competition present?
3. **Build the team** -- Identify JEDS project manager, lead engineer (PE), landscape architect (PLA) if applicable, and CAD/design staff. Identify subconsultant needs. For KYTC projects, all team members must be prequalified in relevant work categories per 603 KAR 2:010. Confirm all proposed PEs hold active KY licensure through the Board (KRS 322.020) and PLAs through KRS 323A.
4. **Prepare SF 330** -- For federal-funded or KYTC projects, complete Standard Form 330 (Architect-Engineer Qualifications). Part I: contract-specific qualifications. Part II: firm profile (update annually). Include project sheets for 5-10 relevant completed projects with client references, contract values, and completion dates.
5. **Draft technical approach** -- Write a project understanding section demonstrating familiarity with the site, jurisdiction, and technical challenges. Describe the approach phase-by-phase. Reference specific Kentucky codes and standards: KY Highway Design Manual, LFUCG Engineering Manuals, KDOW stormwater BMP manual, KY Erosion Prevention and Sediment Control Field Guide. Include a project schedule with milestone dates.
6. **Compile past performance** -- Select 3-5 projects most relevant to the solicitation. For each: project name, client, contract value, completion date, brief narrative of scope and JEDS role, and measurable outcomes (e.g., "delivered 2 months ahead of schedule," "reduced stormwater infrastructure cost by 15% through LID approach"). Include client reference contact information.
7. **Address evaluation criteria directly** -- Structure the response to mirror the evaluation criteria order. If criteria are weighted, allocate proportional effort. For Kentucky QBS selections, emphasize qualifications and approach over fee.
8. **Prepare required forms** -- Complete all required certifications: non-collusion affidavit, campaign finance disclosure (KRS 45A.110), equal opportunity compliance, insurance certificates (minimum $1M professional liability per KRS 45A.245 for state contracts), DBE/MBE participation commitment forms.
9. **Internal review** -- Route the draft response through JEDS principal review. Verify page count against limits, confirm all attachments are included, check formatting consistency, verify all team members' licenses are current through the KY Board verification portal.
10. **Submit** -- For electronic submissions, upload with sufficient lead time (minimum 4 hours before deadline). For physical submissions, arrange delivery to arrive by deadline. Retain a complete copy with timestamp confirmation.

## Output Format

The response document should include:
- Cover letter signed by JEDS principal, expressing interest and confirming availability
- Table of Contents (if response exceeds 10 pages)
- Executive Summary (1 page max, hitting the top 3 evaluation criteria)
- Project Understanding and Technical Approach (organized by evaluation criteria or scope phases)
- Organizational Chart showing project team, roles, and subconsultant relationships
- Key Personnel Resumes (1 page each, emphasizing relevant KY project experience and licensure)
- Relevant Project Experience sheets (1 page each with photos, scope narrative, client reference)
- Project Schedule (Gantt chart or milestone table)
- Required Forms and Certifications appendix
- Fee Proposal (separate sealed envelope if required by solicitation)

## Quality Checks

- [ ] Response directly addresses every stated evaluation criterion in the order listed
- [ ] All proposed PEs and PLAs have active, verifiable KY licenses
- [ ] Page count and formatting comply with solicitation requirements exactly
- [ ] Project examples are within the solicitation's relevance window (typically 5-10 years)
- [ ] DBE/MBE participation meets or exceeds the stated goal percentage
- [ ] SF 330 Part I is complete and project-specific (not recycled boilerplate)
- [ ] Schedule is realistic and accounts for KY agency review periods
- [ ] All required forms, certifications, and affidavits are signed and included
- [ ] Fee proposal (if required) is consistent with the technical scope described
- [ ] Submission method and deadline are confirmed -- electronic portal tested or delivery arranged

## Template Reference

Reference JEDS standard SOQ template and boilerplate project sheets. For KYTC submissions, use the Consultant Information System portal and current SF 330 template from GSA. For LFUCG, check the Division of Central Purchasing website for submission-specific forms. Maintain a master project experience database in `references/project-experience/` for rapid assembly of relevant project sheets.
