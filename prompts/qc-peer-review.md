# QC Peer Review

## Purpose

Conduct an independent internal quality control peer review of civil engineering and landscape architecture deliverables before submittal to clients, agencies, or contractors. The review catches technical errors, code violations, drafting inconsistencies, and coordination gaps that would expose the PE/PLA to liability under Kentucky licensure law (KRS 322, KRS 323A).

## When to Use

- Before any external submittal: 30%, 60%, 90%, or 100% design milestones
- Prior to sealing plan sets for permit applications to KDOW, Louisville MSD, LFUCG, KYTC, or local planning/zoning
- Before issuing construction documents for bid or negotiation
- Before transmitting CA deliverables (pay application reviews, change orders, substantial completion certificates)
- After incorporating client or agency review comments, before resubmittal

## Required Inputs

1. **Deliverable package under review** (plan sheets, specifications, reports, calculations, cost estimates)
2. **Project scope of work and contract** (to verify deliverable completeness against contractual obligations)
3. **Applicable agency checklists** (KYTC plan review, Louisville MSD submittal, LFUCG development review, KDOW permit)
4. **Governing codes and standards** (Kentucky Building Code per 815 KAR 7:120, KYTC Standards, local zoning, ADA)
5. **Previous review comments** (client, agency, or prior QC comments to verify incorporation)
6. **Firm's QC and CAD/BIM standards manual** (layer naming, title block, font, symbol library)
7. **Supporting technical data** (survey, geotechnical report, hydrology study, traffic study, environmental reports)

## Procedure

1. **Confirm reviewer independence.** The reviewer must not have been the primary designer or drafter. The reviewer should hold a Kentucky PE or PLA license (or work directly under one) with relevant project experience. Document reviewer name, license number, and review date.
2. **Verify deliverable completeness.** Check against the scope of work and agency submittal checklist. Confirm all required sheets and attachments are present: cover sheet with index, general notes with code references, demolition plan, EP&SC plan and SWPPP narrative, ADA compliance details, and PE/PLA seal blocks on every sheet requiring certification.
3. **Review plan sheet coordination.** Check for conflicts between disciplines: grading contours vs. spot elevations on utility plans, pipe inverts on profiles vs. plan view callouts, pavement sections on details vs. typical sections, plant schedule quantities vs. per-sheet counts, and north arrows/scales/match lines across all sheets.
4. **Verify engineering calculations.** Spot-check hydrology (drainage area delineation, NOAA Atlas 14 rainfall for the project county, rational method C-values or TR-55 curve numbers). Verify detention routing (stage-storage-discharge, outlet sizing, emergency spillway elevation). Check structural load combinations per AASHTO LRFD or IBC and foundation bearing capacity per the geotech report. Verify pavement design inputs (ESALs, subgrade CBR, layer coefficients per KYTC or AASHTO 93).
5. **Check regulatory compliance.** Verify zoning conditions of approval (setbacks, buffers, impervious limits). Confirm stormwater compliance with MS4 permit requirements (Louisville MSD post-construction standards, LFUCG stormwater manual, KDOW KPDES KYR10). Verify ADA accessibility (5% max running slope, 2% max cross slope, parking per KBC). For KYTC, verify compliance with Highway Design, Drainage, and Geotechnical Manuals. Confirm landscape plans meet local ordinances (LFUCG Article 18, Louisville LDC Chapter 10).
6. **Review drafting quality.** Verify text legibility at plotted scale (minimum 0.08" height), consistent abbreviations and symbols, correct title block (project name, client, sheet number, date, revision, firm, seal), revision clouds with delta numbers, and all referenced details exist with matching callout numbers.
7. **Verify cost estimate** (if included). Spot-check unit prices against recent KYTC bid tabulations (publicly posted). Confirm quantities match plans. Verify contingency and escalation percentages suit the project phase.
8. **Document findings.** Record each comment with location, severity (critical/major/minor/editorial), and recommended correction. Critical findings cause code violations, permit denial, construction failure, or liability exposure. All critical and major comments must be resolved before the deliverable is sealed.

## Output Format

- QC comment log: Comment Number, Sheet/Section, Severity, Description, Recommended Correction, Designer Response, Status
- Summary memorandum signed by the reviewer documenting scope, key findings, and submittal readiness
- Marked-up plan sheets or redlined specifications (PDF with comments) for substantive issues
- Completion checklist confirming all critical and major comments are resolved before sealing

## Quality Checks

- [ ] Reviewer is independent and holds a relevant Kentucky PE or PLA license
- [ ] All sheets requiring a seal have the correct PE/PLA seal block with license number and expiration
- [ ] No conflicting dimensions, elevations, or quantities exist between plan sheets
- [ ] Hydrology calculations use correct NOAA Atlas 14 rainfall data for the project county
- [ ] All previous agency or client review comments have been addressed and incorporated
- [ ] ADA accessibility requirements are met on all pedestrian facilities and public spaces
- [ ] Erosion control plan meets the Kentucky EP&SC Field Guide and applicable permit requirements
- [ ] All critical and major QC comments are resolved before the deliverable is sealed
- [ ] The QC review log is filed in the project record per the firm's quality management system

## Template Reference

- KYTC Plan Review Checklist (by project type: roadway, bridge, drainage, traffic)
- Louisville MSD Plan Review Submittal Checklist and LFUCG Development Plan Review Checklist
- KDOW KPDES KYR10 NOI/NOT Completeness Checklist
- Kentucky Board of Licensure -- Standards of Practice for PEs (201 KAR 18:142) and PLAs (201 KAR 10:030)
- ASCE Quality in the Constructed Project (Manual of Practice No. 73)
