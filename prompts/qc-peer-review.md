# QC Peer Review

## Purpose
Conduct an internal quality control review of engineering and landscape architecture deliverables before they are submitted to clients or regulatory agencies. This structured review catches errors, ensures code compliance, and protects the firm's professional reputation and liability.

## When to Use
- Before any plan set is submitted to a client or agency for review
- Before any report, calculation package, or specification is issued under the firm's seal
- After incorporating agency review comments (verify comment response completeness)
- Before stamping and signing final construction documents for permitting

## Required Inputs
1. **Deliverable package** (plans, reports, specifications, calculations, or exhibits under review)
2. **Project scope of work** or contract defining the required deliverables
3. **Applicable codes and standards** (local ordinance, KYTC standards, MSD design manual, etc.)
4. **Agency comments** from prior review cycles (if a resubmittal)
5. **Design criteria memorandum** or basis of design document
6. **QC reviewer assignment** (must be a licensed PE or PLA not involved in production)

## Procedure
1. **Administrative Check** -- Verify project name, number, and address are consistent across all sheets and documents. Confirm correct client name, revision date, and revision number. Check that the title block, firm name, address, and contact information are current. Verify sheet numbering sequence is complete with no gaps or duplicates.
2. **General Plan Quality** -- Review drafting standards: line weights, text heights (minimum 0.08 inches at plotted scale), north arrow orientation, scale bar accuracy, and match line alignment between sheets. Verify that the plan set is legible at the plotted scale. Confirm all referenced detail sheets exist and are correctly numbered.
3. **Survey and Boundary** -- Verify the survey basis of bearings and datum are stated. Confirm property boundary closure. Check that all easements from the title commitment appear on the plans. Verify benchmark description and elevation match the survey.
4. **Civil Engineering Review** -- Check road profiles against plan view for consistency. Verify pipe slopes produce minimum self-cleansing velocities (2.0 fps for sanitary sewer, 2.5 fps for storm sewer). Confirm structure rim and invert elevations match between the plan, profile, and structure schedule. Verify that design grades provide positive drainage away from buildings. Check ADA accessibility (ramp slopes, cross-slopes, landing dimensions). Verify stormwater detention volumes and release rates match the approved stormwater calculations.
5. **Landscape Architecture Review** -- Verify plant schedule quantities match the plan count. Confirm species selections are appropriate for the hardiness zone and site conditions. Check that buffer yards meet minimum plant counts and widths per the local ordinance. Verify tree planting details show proper root flare exposure and mulch ring dimensions. Confirm spacing meets mature canopy spread requirements.
6. **Specification Review** -- Verify specifications match the materials and methods shown on the plans. Check for conflicting requirements between specification sections. Confirm all products specified are available and current (not discontinued). Verify Kentucky-specific requirements are included: KPDES erosion control, KYTC material standards for public infrastructure, and local utility provider material requirements.
7. **Calculation Verification** -- Spot-check a minimum of three critical calculations: stormwater detention sizing, pipe capacity, structural loading, earthwork volumes, or other project-specific computations. Verify input assumptions match the design criteria and plan information. Confirm calculation methodology follows accepted standards.
8. **Comment Response Verification** -- If a resubmittal, verify every agency comment has been addressed with a specific plan revision or written response. Track each comment by number and confirm the response is adequate and the plan revision is correctly implemented.

## Output Format
- QC review checklist (completed with reviewer initials and date for each item)
- Marked-up plan set (redline comments keyed to checklist items)
- Comment log (item number, sheet, description, severity: critical/major/minor, resolution status)
- Sign-off form with reviewer and project manager signatures

## Quality Checks
- QC reviewer is independent from the production team and holds the appropriate Kentucky license (PE per KRS 322, PLA per KRS 323A)
- All critical and major comments resolved before the deliverable is issued
- Minor comments resolved or documented with justification for deferral
- Sealed documents reviewed for correct license number, seal expiration, and signature
- Final deliverable compared against the QC markup to verify all revisions were incorporated
- QC review record filed in the project archive for liability documentation

## Template Reference
- 201 KAR 18:150 (PE seal and signature requirements)
- KRS 322 (Professional Engineers and Land Surveyors practice act)
- KRS 323A (Landscape Architects practice act)
- ASCE Quality Manual for consulting engineering practice
- EJCDC standard review procedures
