# Fee Proposal Generation

## Purpose

Generate professional services fee proposals for civil engineering and landscape architecture projects in Kentucky. Proposals must comply with KY Board of Licensure for Professional Engineers and Land Surveyors (KRS 322) and Board of Landscape Architects (KRS 323A) requirements, align with ACEC-KY recommended practices, and reflect JEDS standard billing structure.

## When to Use

- Client requests a formal fee proposal for a new project or additional services
- Responding to an informal scope inquiry that requires a written fee commitment
- Preparing a subconsultant fee package under a prime contract
- Issuing a change order or amendment to an existing contract for expanded scope

## Required Inputs

- **Client name and contact**: Entity name, project manager or decision-maker, address
- **Project description**: Site location (county, city, address), approximate acreage or square footage, project type (commercial site, subdivision, streetscape, park, stormwater, etc.)
- **Scope of services requested**: Which disciplines are involved (civil, landscape architecture, surveying, geotechnical coordination, environmental)
- **Regulatory jurisdiction**: City/county planning authority, applicable overlay districts, FEMA floodplain status, MS4 permit applicability
- **Schedule requirements**: Client-driven deadlines, planning commission meeting dates, construction season targets
- **Fee basis preference**: Lump sum, hourly not-to-exceed, percentage of construction cost, or time and materials

## Procedure

1. **Define scope phases** -- Break services into standard phases: Project Initiation, Survey/Data Collection, Schematic Design, Design Development, Construction Documents, Permitting, Bidding Assistance, Construction Administration. Omit phases not applicable.
2. **Identify regulatory submittals** -- List each required submittal: LFUCG subdivision plat, Elizabethtown site plan review, KDOW KPDES permit (KYR10), Army Corps Section 404/Nationwide Permit, KY Heritage Council Section 106 review, Cabinet for Economic Development (if incentive-tied).
3. **Estimate hours by task** -- Use JEDS standard hour ranges: topographic survey (16-40 hrs depending on acreage), grading plan (24-60 hrs), utility plan (16-40 hrs), erosion control/SWPPP (8-24 hrs), landscape plan (16-48 hrs), construction details (8-24 hrs), stormwater management report per KY BMP Manual (16-40 hrs).
4. **Apply billing rates** -- Use current JEDS rate schedule: Principal/PE $185-210/hr, Project Engineer $140-165/hr, PLA $150-175/hr, Design Professional $95-120/hr, CAD Technician $85-105/hr, 2-person survey crew $175-195/hr, administrative $65-75/hr.
5. **Calculate reimbursable expenses** -- Printing/reproduction, mileage at current IRS rate, permit filing fees (pass-through at cost), geotechnical/environmental subconsultant fees (marked up per contract terms, typically 10%).
6. **Build fee summary table** -- Phase-by-phase breakdown showing labor hours, labor cost, reimbursables, subconsultant costs, and phase total. Include grand total and any optional/alternates listed separately.
7. **Draft assumptions and exclusions** -- Explicitly state what is NOT included: Phase I ESA, wetland delineation, traffic impact study, structural engineering, ALTA survey, legal descriptions, utility relocations, material testing during construction.
8. **Add standard contract terms** -- Reference ACEC-KY/EJCDC E-500 or JEDS standard terms. Include payment terms (Net 30), retainer requirements for new clients, interest on overdue invoices per KRS 360.010 (8% statutory rate), and limitation of liability clause.

## Output Format

Produce a structured proposal document with:
- Cover letter (1 page) referencing the project, expressing qualifications, noting PE/PLA licensure
- Scope of Services narrative (organized by phase, with numbered task descriptions)
- Fee Summary Table (phase, hours, fee per phase, total)
- Schedule (milestone dates tied to planning commission calendars or construction windows)
- Assumptions and Exclusions list
- Signature block with PE and/or PLA seal statement per 201 KAR 18:150

## Quality Checks

- [ ] Fee aligns with ACEC-KY survey benchmarks for project type and size
- [ ] All applicable KY permits and submittals are identified in scope
- [ ] Billing rates match current JEDS rate schedule
- [ ] Scope phases correspond to deliverables -- no phase without a named deliverable
- [ ] Exclusions are specific enough to prevent scope creep disputes
- [ ] Schedule accounts for typical review turnaround (LFUCG 4-6 weeks, Elizabethtown 2-4 weeks, KDOW 30-60 days)
- [ ] Contract terms reference appropriate KRS statutes for interest and liability
- [ ] Proposal is addressed to the decision-maker, not a generic recipient

## Template Reference

Reference JEDS fee proposal template in `templates/fee-proposal-template.docx` if available. For EJCDC-based contracts, reference EJCDC E-500 Standard Agreement Between Owner and Engineer. For landscape architecture standalone contracts, reference ASLA standard form or JEDS custom LA services agreement.
