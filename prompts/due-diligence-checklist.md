# Due Diligence Checklist for Land Acquisition

## Purpose
Generate a comprehensive due diligence investigation checklist for prospective land acquisitions in Kentucky, covering legal, environmental, geotechnical, infrastructure, and regulatory dimensions so a PE/PLA can advise a client on material risks before closing.

## When to Use
- Evaluating a specific tract for purchase, option agreement, or land swap.
- Preparing a feasibility study to accompany an offer or letter of intent.
- Performing owner's due diligence before site plan or subdivision plat submittal.

## Required Inputs
1. **Parcel ID**: County, PVA parcel number (e.g., Fayette County PVA map/parcel), address, and acreage.
2. **Proposed use**: Residential (SF/MF/subdivision), commercial, industrial, mixed-use, agricultural, or conservation.
3. **Jurisdiction**: Louisville Metro (LMCO), LFUCG, or county with/without KRS Chapter 100 planning and zoning.
4. **Budget and timeline**: Acquisition price range, development budget, target closing date.

## Procedure
**Phase 1 -- Title and Legal**: Pull current deed and chain of title from the county clerk; verify legal description against PVA plat. Obtain preliminary title commitment; flag easements, deed restrictions, liens, and ROW reservations. Check for mineral rights severances common in eastern KY coal counties (KRS 381.400 broad form deed). Confirm whether the property is enrolled in an agricultural district under KRS 262.850 (3-year conversion notice). Search for pending litigation or condemnation in circuit court.

**Phase 2 -- Environmental**: Order Phase I ESA per ASTM E1527-21; budget for Phase II if historical industrial/ag-chemical use exists. Pull KDEP UST database and EPA CERCLIS records. Obtain FEMA FIRM panel; determine Zone A/AE/0.2% floodplain and community CRS rating. Check KDOW 303(d) impaired waters list for on-site or adjacent streams. Verify wetland presence via USFWS NWI and field delineation (USACE Section 404 if impacts anticipated). Review KY State Nature Preserves Commission T&E database within one mile. Evaluate karst potential using KGS sinkhole inventory maps -- Fayette, Jessamine, Scott, and Warren counties require focused geotechnical investigation.

**Phase 3 -- Zoning and Entitlements**: Confirm current zoning from the local planning commission (LFUCG R-1 through R-5, B-1 through B-6P, I-1/I-2; Louisville Metro form districts and zones under LMCO Ch. 1). Determine if the use is as-of-right, conditional use, or requires a zone map amendment per KRS 100.213. Review adopted comprehensive plan and future land use map for consistency. Identify overlays: historic districts, airport noise contours (SDF/LEX), scenic corridors.

**Phase 4 -- Infrastructure**: Contact the water provider (Louisville Water Co., KY American Water) for pressure, capacity, and will-serve letter. Contact sewer provider (Louisville MSD, LFUCG Div. of Water Quality) for capacity and pump station needs. If no public sewer, evaluate on-site wastewater per 902 KAR 10:085 for systems over 1,000 GPD. Verify electric (KU/LG&E), gas (Columbia Gas, Atmos Energy), and telecom service. Check KYTC traffic counts on adjacent state routes and sight distance at proposed access; determine if a traffic impact study is triggered.

**Phase 5 -- Geotechnical**: Commission boundary and topographic survey per KRS 322.360. Order geotechnical borings at building pad and stormwater facility locations. Evaluate hillside regulations for slopes exceeding 20%. In karst areas, conduct sinkhole investigation using resistivity imaging or dye-trace testing.

## Output Format
Structured checklist with columns: Item #, Investigation Item, Source/Agency, Status (Not Started/In Progress/Complete/N/A), Finding, Risk Flag (Green/Yellow/Red), and Cost Estimate. Include an executive summary tallying risk flags and highlighting deal-critical findings.

## Quality Checks
- Every item must cite a specific Kentucky agency, database, or statute -- no generic references.
- Environmental items must address both federal (CWA Section 404, ESA) and state (KDEP, KDOW) layers.
- Infrastructure items must name the actual utility provider, not a placeholder.
- All KRS citations must be verified as current via the LRC website.
- Items irrelevant to the proposed use should be marked N/A with explanation, not deleted.

## Template Reference
- ASTM E1527-21 (Phase I ESA); FEMA Flood Map Service Center (FIRM panels); KY PVA portals (qpublic.net).
- KGS karst maps and county geologic quadrangles; KYTC Highway Plan and access permit forms.
- KRS Ch. 100 (planning/zoning), KRS Ch. 224 (environmental protection), KRS Ch. 151 (water resources).
