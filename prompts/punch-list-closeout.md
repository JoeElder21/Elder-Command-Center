# Punch List Generation and Closeout Documentation

## Purpose

Generate comprehensive punch lists and prepare project closeout documentation for civil engineering and landscape architecture construction projects in Kentucky. Ensures all deficiencies are identified, tracked, and resolved before final acceptance, and that closeout packages satisfy KYTC, Louisville MSD, local AHJ, and owner requirements.

## When to Use

- Substantial completion inspections for grading, drainage, roadway, or site work
- Final walk-throughs prior to owner acceptance or KYTC final inspection
- Landscape installation acceptance and warranty period initiation
- Utility infrastructure closeout for sanitary sewer districts or Louisville MSD
- Bond release inspections under KRS 100.3681 (performance and maintenance bonds)

## Required Inputs

- Approved construction drawings and specifications (including KYTC Standard Specifications for Road and Bridge Construction, current edition)
- Contractor's submitted as-built redlines and record drawings
- Materials certifications, test reports (concrete, asphalt, compaction, pipe deflection per ASTM D2412)
- Louisville MSD Capacity Assurance Program documentation (if applicable)
- SWPPP inspection logs and NOT filing confirmation from KY Energy and Environment Cabinet
- Contractor daily logs, RFI log, and change order log
- Approved shop drawings and substitution requests
- KPDES permit and associated BMP completion records

## Procedure

1. **Pre-Inspection Preparation**: Compile the contract document set including all approved change orders. Review the specification sections for closeout submittals required (typically Division 01 78 00). Cross-reference the submittal log to identify outstanding items.

2. **Field Inspection by Discipline**: Walk the site systematically by CSI division. For site/civil work, verify final grades against approved grading plan elevations using GPS or total station survey. Check storm drainage structures for proper invert elevations, joint integrity, and CCTV inspection completion per Louisville MSD or local sewer district standards. Inspect pavement markings, signage, and ADA-compliant curb ramps against PROWAG and KYTC Standard Drawings.

3. **Landscape Verification**: Confirm plant species, caliper, height, and spacing match the approved planting plan. Verify tree staking, mulch depth (3 inches minimum per KY BMP manual), and irrigation system pressure tests. Document plant health baseline with photographs for warranty period enforcement.

4. **Punch List Documentation**: Assign each deficiency a unique identifier, location reference (station/offset or grid), specification section, priority rating (life-safety, functional, aesthetic), and responsible party. Include a photograph for each item. Set correction deadlines -- typically 30 days for functional items, weather-permitting for landscape items.

5. **Re-Inspection and Verification**: Conduct follow-up inspections to verify corrections. Document each resolved item with date, inspector initials, and verification photograph. Items requiring destructive testing or hidden-work verification must reference the original inspection report.

6. **Closeout Package Assembly**: Compile the following for the owner and AHJ: as-built record drawings (AutoCAD and PDF), O&M manuals, warranty letters (minimum one year general, two years mechanical/electrical, typical), final lien waivers per KRS 376.010, Contractor's Affidavit of Payment of Debts and Claims, Certificate of Substantial Completion (AIA G704 or equivalent), and consent of surety.

7. **Bond and Retainage Release**: Prepare recommendation for reduction of retainage per KRS 371.405 (public projects). For subdivision infrastructure, coordinate with local planning commission for bond release inspection per KRS 100.3681. Document that all public improvements meet acceptance standards for dedication.

## Output Format

- Punch list spreadsheet with columns: Item Number, Location, CSI Division, Description, Priority, Responsible Party, Due Date, Status, Verification Date, Photo Reference
- Closeout transmittal letter to owner summarizing package contents and outstanding items
- Certificate of Substantial Completion with attached deficiency list
- Final inspection report narrative with photo documentation appendix

## Quality Checks

- Every specification section with installed work has been inspected and documented
- All required test reports (compaction, concrete breaks, asphalt cores, pipe mandrel/deflection) are present and within tolerances per KYTC or project specifications
- SWPPP NOT has been filed or justification for continued coverage is documented
- Retainage release recommendation complies with KRS 371.405 timelines
- As-built drawings have been reviewed against field conditions and surveyed data
- All lien waivers are unconditional final waivers from contractor and subcontractors

## Template Reference

- AIA G704 Certificate of Substantial Completion
- AIA G706 Contractor's Affidavit of Payment of Debts and Claims
- AIA G706A Contractor's Affidavit of Release of Liens
- AIA G707 Consent of Surety to Final Payment
- KYTC TC 63-27 Final Inspection Report
- Louisville MSD Closeout Checklist for Capacity Assurance Projects
- KRS 45A.435-460 (public construction contract administration requirements)
