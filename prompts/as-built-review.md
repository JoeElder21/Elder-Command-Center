# As-Built Review

## Purpose

Review as-built drawings (record drawings) against the approved construction plans to verify that field conditions and constructed improvements are accurately documented, deviations are identified and assessed for compliance, and the final record reflects what was actually built. This supports project closeout, regulatory compliance, and future maintenance or expansion of infrastructure in Kentucky.

## When to Use

- At project closeout before issuing the PE/PLA certification letter to the owner or agency
- When a contractor submits redline markups for conversion into formal as-built drawings
- Before submitting record drawings to Louisville MSD, LFUCG, KDOW, or KYTC for acceptance
- When reviewing as-built surveys against approved plans for subdivision plat recordation
- Prior to releasing a contractor's retainage, when as-built acceptance is a contract requirement
- During post-construction stormwater BMP certification per KDOW or local MS4 permit conditions

## Required Inputs

1. **Approved construction plans** (the stamped, permitted plan set with all approved revisions)
2. **Contractor's redline markups** (field-marked drawings showing deviations from approved plans)
3. **As-built survey data** (licensed surveyor's field measurements of constructed improvements)
4. **Construction inspection reports and daily logs** (documenting field changes, RFIs, and change orders)
5. **Approved change orders and RFI responses** (formal documentation of authorized deviations)
6. **Agency-specific as-built requirements**: Louisville MSD Record Drawing Standards, LFUCG as-built checklist, KYTC Final Records requirements per Guidance Manual Section 500
7. **Material testing reports and certifications** (compaction tests, concrete breaks, pipe deflection tests)

## Procedure

1. **Establish the base comparison.** Overlay the approved plan set (including all approved revisions and change orders) as the baseline. Organize contractor redlines, survey data, and inspection records by plan sheet for systematic comparison.
2. **Review horizontal alignment and layout.** Compare as-built survey coordinates for roadway centerlines, curb lines, building corners, and utility alignments against the approved plan. For KYTC projects, verify centerline stationing matches the approved alignment per the Design Memorandum. Flag any horizontal shifts exceeding the tolerance specified in the contract (typically 0.10 ft for structures, 0.50 ft for earthwork).
3. **Review vertical grades and elevations.** Compare as-built invert elevations for storm and sanitary sewers against approved profiles. Verify finished floor elevations for structures meet or exceed the base flood elevation (BFE) plus the required freeboard (typically 1.0 ft above BFE for Louisville MSD, 2.0 ft for LFUCG). Verify pavement finished grades provide positive drainage.
4. **Review utility infrastructure.** Confirm pipe sizes, materials, and invert elevations match approved plans or approved change orders. Verify manhole rim and invert elevations. For Louisville MSD acceptance, confirm that storm sewer deflection test results (maximum 5% deflection for flexible pipe) and TV inspection (CCTV) reports are satisfactory. Verify fire hydrant locations and water main alignments match Louisville Water Company or Kentucky American Water approved plans.
5. **Review stormwater management facilities.** Compare as-built BMP dimensions, outlet structure elevations, and storage volumes against the approved Stormwater Pollution Prevention Plan (SWPPP) or Stormwater Quality Management Plan (SWQMP). Recalculate detention storage if as-built grades deviate from design by more than 0.25 ft at the control structure. Verify emergency spillway elevations and overflow routing.
6. **Review grading and earthwork.** Compare as-built contours or spot elevations against the approved grading plan. Verify retaining wall top and bottom elevations. Confirm that slopes do not exceed the maximum stable gradient recommended in the geotechnical report (typically 3H:1V for fill slopes, 2H:1V for cut slopes in Kentucky soils without engineering analysis).
7. **Review landscape and site improvements.** For PLA-sealed plans, verify that planted areas, tree locations, and hardscape features match the approved landscape plan. Confirm that required landscape buffer widths per local zoning (e.g., LFUCG Article 18, Louisville LDC Chapter 10) are maintained as-built. Verify ADA-accessible route slopes do not exceed 5% running slope and 2% cross slope.
8. **Assess deviations.** For each deviation identified, determine whether it was authorized (covered by an approved change order or RFI response) or unauthorized. For unauthorized deviations, evaluate whether the as-built condition meets code, permit, and design intent. Recommend corrective action or request a formal deviation acceptance from the reviewing agency.
9. **Prepare the as-built certification.** Draft the PE/PLA certification statement indicating that the as-built drawings have been reviewed and substantially represent the constructed improvements. For Louisville MSD, use the required Record Drawing Certification form. For KYTC, follow the Final Records certification process per the KYTC Guidance Manual.

## Output Format

- Deviation log in tabular format: Sheet Number, Feature, Approved Design Value, As-Built Value, Deviation Magnitude, Change Order Reference (if any), Compliance Assessment, Recommended Action
- Annotated plan set showing deviations (PDF with highlighted differences)
- PE/PLA-sealed as-built certification letter to the owner and/or reviewing agency
- Summary memorandum noting all critical deviations, their resolution, and any outstanding items
- Digital CAD files updated to reflect as-built conditions (DWG format per agency requirements)

## Quality Checks

- [ ] All as-built survey data is from a Kentucky-licensed land surveyor (KRS 322.010)
- [ ] Every utility invert elevation has been field-verified and documented
- [ ] Storm sewer CCTV inspection reports are included and pass the agency's defect criteria
- [ ] Detention facility as-built volumes are within 5% of the approved design volumes
- [ ] Finished floor elevations meet or exceed the required flood protection elevation
- [ ] All deviations have been categorized as authorized (with change order reference) or unauthorized
- [ ] ADA-accessible routes meet slope requirements per the Kentucky Building Code and ADA Standards
- [ ] As-built drawings bear the PE/PLA seal, signature, date, and Kentucky license number
- [ ] Agency-specific as-built checklists are completed (MSD, LFUCG, KYTC as applicable)
- [ ] CAD file naming and layering conventions match the receiving agency's standards

## Template Reference

- Louisville MSD Record Drawing Standards and Certification Form
- LFUCG As-Built Drawing Submittal Requirements (Division of Engineering)
- KYTC Guidance Manual Section 500 (Final Records and Documentation)
- Kentucky Board of Licensure for Professional Engineers and Land Surveyors (KRS 322)
- Kentucky Board of Landscape Architects (KRS 323A)
- FEMA Elevation Certificate (FEMA Form 086-0-33) for floodplain structures
- ADA Standards for Accessible Design (2010 Standards)
